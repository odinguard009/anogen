# Chapter 6: Lance Nine

The arms yard was empty after class. Eden stayed. He held his lance. He moved it slowly. Left, right, up, down. Each move was small. He did not swing. He placed the lance where he wanted it.

A shadow moved at the gate. Vesk. He was small and thin. His eyes were bright, like lamps. He wore a dark coat. He leaned on the wall and watched.

"You are slow," Vesk said.

"I am careful," Eden said.

"Careful is slow," Vesk said. "Slow is dead."

Eden did not answer. He placed the lance again. Left. Right.

Vesk walked closer. "I heard about the exchange. The Veil. You did it alone."

"I was not alone. Lysette was there."

"Lysette is not a fighter," Vesk said. "She is a witness. She watches. She does not kill."

"She held the door," Eden said.

Vesk smiled. It was not a kind smile. "I know. I was on the roof. I saw."

Eden stopped. He looked at Vesk. "You were there?"

"I am always there," Vesk said. "That is my job. I see. I hear. I tell you. You act."

Eden put down the lance. "Then tell me. What else did you see?"

Vesk's eyes narrowed. "The clerk at the desk. He was not asleep. He was watching you. He let you pass on purpose."

Eden picked up the lance again. "Why?"

"I do not know. But he smiled when you left. A small smile. Not a happy smile. A knowing smile."

Eden thought about this. A clerk who let them pass. A Veil upstairs. The clerk knew. Maybe the clerk was a Veil too. Or maybe the clerk was something else.

"Thank you," Eden said.

Vesk shrugged. "Do not thank me. Pay me. I need a new coat. This one has holes."

Eden reached into his pocket. He gave Vesk a coin. Vesk took it and left. He moved like a shadow. In three steps, he was gone.

Eden stood alone. The yard was dark now. The lamps were on. He picked up his lance and left.

In his room, he sat on his bed. He opened his notebook. He wrote: "Clerk. Exchange. Smiled. Knows. Check."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He thought about Vesk. Vesk was not a friend. Vesk was a tool. But tools were useful. Eden kept tools.

He slept. No dreams.
