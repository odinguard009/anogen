# Chapter 14: The Roof Stakeout

Eden went to the roof garden. It was midnight. The city was dark. The lamps were on. They looked normal.

He sat on the bench. He had a small telescope. He looked at the lamp house on the hill. It was dark. No light. But the machines were running. He could see the glow from the windows. Faint. Blue.

He watched. An hour passed. Nothing happened. Two hours. Nothing.

At 2 AM, a light appeared at the lamp house. A door opened. A figure came out. Tall. Dark coat. The figure walked down the hill. Toward the city.

Eden followed. He stayed on the roofs. He jumped from building to building. The city was quiet. Only the trams were still. They did not run at night.

The figure walked to the exchange. The telephone exchange. The same one where Eden had killed the Veil. The figure went inside.

Eden waited on the roof across the street. He watched the windows. Lights went on and off. The figure was doing something inside.

After twenty minutes, the figure came out. It walked back up the hill. To the lamp house.

Eden followed again. He watched from a rooftop. The figure entered the lamp house. The door closed. The lights went out.

Eden sat on the roof. He thought. The figure went to the exchange. Then came back. It was checking something. Or it was reporting.

He needed to know who the figure was. He needed a face.

The next night, he went again. Same time. Same place. The figure came out. Eden was ready. He had a small mirror. He used it to catch the light from a street lamp. He flashed it toward the figure's face.

The figure stopped. It turned. It looked up. For one second, Eden saw the face.

It was the clerk from the exchange. The same clerk who had let them pass. The same clerk who had smiled.

Eden put down the mirror. The clerk stood in the street. Looking up. Looking at the roof where Eden was. Then the clerk turned and walked back up the hill.

Eden left. He went home. He sat on his bed. He wrote in his notebook: "Clerk. Exchange. Lamp house. Same person. He is the one."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He thought about the clerk. A clerk who controlled wires. A clerk who controlled lamps. A clerk who was not a Veil but worked with Veils.

Eden did not sleep. He planned. Friday was three days away. He had three days to plan.

He would need help. He did not want help. But he would need it.

He thought about Lysette. She would want to help. He thought about Vesk. Vesk could watch. He thought about Felix. Felix was loyal.

He would use them. All of them. But he would not tell them everything. He would tell them what they needed to know.

He closed his eyes. He did not sleep. He planned.
