# Chapter 15: The Tail

Eden followed the clerk the next day. Not at night. In the day. The clerk left the exchange at noon. He walked to a bakery. He bought bread. He walked to a park. He sat on a bench. He ate.

Eden sat on a bench across the park. He watched. The clerk ate his bread. He looked at the people in the park. Children playing. Mothers watching. Old men on benches.

The clerk finished his bread. He stood. He walked to a building. It was a small building. One floor. A sign on the door said: "Records Office."

Eden waited. The clerk went inside. He came out ten minutes later. He had a paper. He walked back to the exchange.

Eden followed. He stayed fifty steps behind. The clerk did not look back. He walked into the exchange and did not come out.

Eden went home. He thought about the Records Office. What records? What did the clerk want?

He went to the Records Office the next day. He wore his coat. He looked like a student. He asked the clerk there for records of city lamps.

The clerk looked at him. "Why?"

"School project," Eden said.

The clerk shrugged. He gave Eden a paper. It listed all the lamp houses in the city. Twelve. Eden read it. He found the one on the hill. It was listed as "Lamp House 7. Status: Active. Controller: Exchange."

Controller: Exchange. So the exchange controlled the lamp house. That meant the clerk controlled it.

Eden thanked the clerk and left. He went to the roof garden. Lysette was there.

"I found it," Eden said. "The clerk controls the lamp house. He is the one."

Lysette looked at him. "What do we do?"

"We watch. Friday. He plans to turn off the lamps. We stop him."

"How?"

"I do not know yet," Eden said.

Lysette was quiet. Then she said, "I will bring my Witness."

"Your Tenet?"

"Yes. If he tries to turn off the lamps, I can see what he does. I can replay it."

Eden thought about this. Lysette's Tenet was Witness. She could replay the last minutes of a place. Like a ghost-light. If she stood in the lamp house, she could see what the clerk did.

"Okay," Eden said. "Friday. We go to the lamp house. You use Witness. I will be there."

Lysette nodded. She did not smile. She was serious. That was good.

Eden went to his room. He sat on his bed. He opened his notebook. He wrote: "Friday. Lamp house. Clerk. Lysette: Witness. Stop the lamps."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He thought about Friday. Three days. He had three days to plan.

He planned. He always planned.
