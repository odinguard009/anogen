# Chapter 17: The Lose

The next day, Eden lost a fight.

It was in the arms yard. A duel. Eden vs. a student named Brand. Brand was tall. He had a long reach. His lance was longer than Eden's.

They faced each other. Okoru watched. The other students watched.

Brand attacked first. A thrust. Eden parried. Brand thrust again. Eden parried. Brand thrust a third time. Eden stepped back. The thrust caught his arm. Not deep. But it stung.

Eden thrust back. Brand parried. Eden thrust again. Brand parried. Eden thrust a third time. Brand stepped inside and hit Eden in the chest with his lance butt.

Eden fell. He hit the ground. The stone was cold. His chest hurt.

"Out," Okoru said.

Eden stood. He was not hurt badly. But he had lost. He had lost on purpose? No. He had lost because Brand was better today. Brand was faster. Brand was stronger.

Brand offered his hand. Eden took it. Brand pulled him up.

"Good fight," Brand said.

"Good fight," Eden said.

Brand left. The students left. Eden stayed. He held his lance. He looked at it.

He had lost. He had let himself lose. Why? Because Brand was watching him. Brand had been watching him for weeks. Brand was curious. Brand wanted to know how good Eden was.

If Eden won too easily, Brand would tell others. Brand would say, "Eden is too good. He is hiding something." That would bring attention. Attention was bad.

So Eden lost. He lost by a little. Not by much. Just enough to make Brand think Eden was good but not too good. Just enough to make Brand stop watching.

Lysette found him in the yard. "You lost," she said.

"Yes."

"On purpose?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because Brand was watching me. If I won too easily, he would talk. I need him to think I am normal."

Lysette looked at him. "You are not normal."

"I know. But he does not need to know that."

Lysette was quiet. Then she said, "You are always planning. Always hiding. When do you stop?"

"When it is safe," Eden said.

"It is never safe," Lysette said.

"Then I never stop," Eden said.

Lysette shook her head. She left. Eden stayed in the yard. He held his lance. He thought about Brand. Brand was not a threat. Brand was curious. Curious people were dangerous. But manageable.

Eden would watch Brand. Brand would watch Eden. They would both pretend. That was how it worked.

He went to his room. He wrote in his notebook: "Lost to Brand. On purpose. He was watching. Now he thinks I am normal. Good."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He thought about losing. It was not hard to lose. It was hard to lose on purpose. To let someone beat you when you could beat them. That took more control than winning.

He slept. No dreams.
