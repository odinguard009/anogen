# Chapter 19: The Kitchen Blast

The kitchen was under the dining hall. It was hot. Steam. Oil. Noise. Students did not come here. Only cooks.

Eden came here to hide. He sat on a sack of flour. The flour was white and soft. He leaned against the wall.

A cook named Maren looked at him. "You should not be here," she said.

"I know," Eden said.

Maren was old. She had grey hair. She had a scar on her hand. She chopped vegetables. Fast. The knife hit the board. Thack. Thack. Thack.

"You are the quiet one," Maren said.

"Yes."

"I have seen you. On the roof. In the yard. Always alone."

Eden did not answer.

Maren stopped chopping. She looked at him. "You are hiding something."

"Everyone hides something," Eden said.

Maren nodded. She went back to chopping. Thack. Thack. Thack.

Eden sat on the flour. He thought about Friday. Two days. He needed to eat. He had not eaten since morning. His stomach was cold.

Maren put a bowl in front of him. Soup. Hot. Bread on the side.

"Eat," she said.

Eden ate. The soup was good. Warm. He ate the bread. It was fresh.

"Thank you," he said.

Maren went back to chopping. "Come back tomorrow. I will have more."

Eden left the kitchen. He went to his room. He felt better. The soup had helped. His stomach was warm now.

He sat on his bed. He opened his notebook. He wrote: "Maren. Cook. Gives food. Kind. Does not ask questions."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He thought about kindness. Maren was kind. She did not know what he did. She did not need to know. She just gave him food.

That was rare. Most people wanted something. Maren wanted nothing.

He slept. No dreams.
