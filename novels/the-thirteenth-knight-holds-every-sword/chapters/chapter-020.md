# Chapter 20: Valerius

Valerius stood on the stairs. He was tall. He had dark hair. He wore a white coat. He looked like a prince. He was a prince.

Eden walked up the stairs. Valerius blocked the way.

"Wren," Valerius said.

"Brandmeier," Eden said. That was Valerius's last name. Valerius hated it when people used it.

Valerius's eyes narrowed. "You are skipping class again."

"I am going to class."

"You are going the wrong way."

Eden looked. He was going the wrong way. He turned. He walked the other way. Valerius followed.

"You lost to Brand yesterday," Valerius said.

"Yes."

"You should not have lost."

"I know."

"You are better than Brand. Everyone knows it."

"Then everyone is wrong," Eden said.

Valerius stopped. He grabbed Eden's arm. "What are you hiding, Wren?"

Eden looked at Valerius's hand. "Let go."

Valerius let go. He looked at Eden. His eyes were sharp. "I know you are not what you seem. I have known for a year. You skip class but score highest. You disappear at night. You are always alone."

"And you care why?" Eden asked.

"Because I am the council president. It is my job to know."

"Then know this," Eden said. "I am a student. I attend class. I score well. That is all."

Valerius was quiet. Then he said, "We will see."

He left. Eden watched him go. Valerius was dangerous. Not because he was strong. Because he was smart. He saw things. He noticed things.

Eden went to class. He sat in the back. He did not look at Valerius. He took notes. He did not need to take notes. He already knew the material. But he took notes to look normal.

After class, Lysette found him. "Valerius was asking about you," she said.

"What did he ask?"

"Where you go at night. Who you talk to. What you do."

"What did you say?"

"I said I do not know."

"Good," Eden said.

Lysette looked at him. "Eden. Valerius is not stupid. He will find out."

"Let him find out," Eden said. "He will find a student who skips class. That is all."

Lysette was not convinced. She wrote in her notebook: "Valerius watching. Be careful."

Eden went to his room. He sat on his bed. He thought about Valerius. Valerius was the crown prince. He was powerful. He had resources. If Valerius decided to investigate Eden, he could find things.

But Eden had planned for this. He had layers. Each layer showed a different face. To Valerius, he was a lazy student. That was the top layer. Below that, there were other layers. Valerius would have to dig deep to find them.

Eden did not think Valerius would dig that deep. Not yet. Valerius had other things to worry about. The Concord. The war. The Ashen Crowns.

Eden was not important enough. Not yet.

He slept. No dreams.
