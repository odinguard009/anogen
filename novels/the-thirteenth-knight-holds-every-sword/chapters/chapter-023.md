# Chapter 23: The Second Safehouse

Eden found a second safehouse. It was in the basement of a bakery. The bakery was on a side street. It smelled like bread.

Eden went at night. He unlocked the door. The basement was dark. He lit a lamp. The room was small. A table. A chair. A bed. A window, high up, looking at the street.

He sat at the table. He opened his notebook. He wrote: "Safehouse 2. Bakery basement. Quiet. Good for hiding."

He heard footsteps above. The baker. Working. The smell of bread came down. Warm. Good.

Eden thought about the Ashen Crowns. They were everywhere. The clerk. The teacher. The dye-works. The lamp houses. They were planning something big. Friday.

He needed to know more. He needed to know who was in charge. The clerk said a man. The teacher said the Ashen Crowns. But who? Who was the leader?

He would find out. He always found out.

He went to Vesk. Vesk was on his usual roof. Eating an apple.

"I need more," Eden said.

"You always need more," Vesk said.

"The Ashen Crowns. Who is in charge? Here. In the city."

Vesk looked at him. His bright eyes narrowed. "You do not want to know."

"I do."

Vesk was quiet. He ate his apple. He looked at the city. Then he said, "A man named Mott. He runs the cell here. He is in the exchange. Second floor. Behind a door that does not open."

"How do you know?"

"I watch. I listen. I see."

"Can you get me in?"

Vesk smiled. "For a price."

Eden gave him a coin. Vesk took it. "Tomorrow night. I will leave the back door open. One minute. That is all you get."

"Thank you," Eden said.

Vesk shrugged. "Do not thank me. I am a tool."

"I know," Eden said. "Tools are useful."

Vesk finished his apple. Eden left.

He went home. He sat on his bed. He planned. Tomorrow night. The exchange. Mott. One minute.

He would need to be fast. He would need to be quiet. He would need to see everything in one minute.

He could do it. He always did.

He slept. No dreams.
