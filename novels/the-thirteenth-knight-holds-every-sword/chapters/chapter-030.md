# Chapter 30: The Duke

The Duke came to the academy. Duke Aldous Argentine. He was Eden's father. He was loud. He was large. He wore a bright coat. He laughed at everything.

Eden stood in the hall. The Duke walked toward him. His footsteps were heavy. The floor shook.

"Eden!" the Duke said. "My boy!"

He hugged Eden. The hug was tight. Eden could not breathe. The Duke smelled like wine and smoke.

"Father," Eden said.

The Duke let go. He looked at Eden. His eyes were wet. "You have grown. You are thinner. Are you eating?"

"Yes."

"You are lying."

"I am not."

"You are. I can tell. You get it from me. I also lie about eating."

The Duke laughed. It was a big laugh. It filled the hall. Students turned to look.

Eden did not laugh. He never laughed. But he almost smiled. Almost.

"Come," the Duke said. "I have brought gifts."

They went to Eden's room. The Duke carried a box. It was large. He put it on the bed. He opened it.

Inside was a coat. Black. Long. It was beautiful. The fabric was heavy. The buttons were silver.

"For you," the Duke said. "From the Argentine house. Black. Like your armor."

Eden touched the coat. It was soft. It was warm.

"Thank you, Father," he said.

The Duke sat on the bed. The bed creaked. "How are you? Really."

"I am fine."

"You are not fine. You are hiding something. I can see it."

Eden looked at his father. The Duke's eyes were serious. The laugh was gone. The Duke was not just a loud man. He was a knight. A master. He saw things.

"I am handling it," Eden said.

"Handling what?"

"Things. At the academy. In the city."

The Duke was quiet. Then he said, "Your mother would have been proud of you."

Eden's chest tightened. He did not show it. "I know," he said.

The Duke stood. He hugged Eden again. This time, Eden let him. The hug was warm. The Duke's coat smelled like leather.

"Be careful," the Duke said. "The world is not safe. Not even for Argentines."

"I know," Eden said.

The Duke left. His footsteps were heavy. The floor shook. Then they faded.

Eden stood in his room. He held the black coat. He put it on. It fit. It was warm.

He looked in the mirror. He looked like a knight. A black knight. The coat hid his armor. But the armor was there. Underneath. Always.

He took off the coat. He put it in his closet. He would wear it when he needed to. Not now.

He sat on his bed. He opened his notebook. He wrote: "Father visited. Brought black coat. He knows I am hiding something. He did not push. He is worried."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He thought about his father. The Duke was loud. But he was also wise. He had seen through Eden's lie. He had not pushed. He had given Eden space.

That was rare. Most people pushed. The Duke did not.

Eden thought about his mother. Liora. She was dead. The Duke had mentioned her. Eden did not remember her face. He had been too young. But he remembered her hands. They were warm. She had held him. Once. He remembered that.

He closed his eyes. He did not sleep. He thought about his mother. He thought about his father. He thought about the coat.

He would wear the coat. He would be a black knight. Not just in armor. In everything.

He slept. No dreams.
