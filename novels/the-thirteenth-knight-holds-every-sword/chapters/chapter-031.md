# Chapter 31: The Wall

The Southern Wall was on the Teeth. The Teeth were mountains. Sharp. White. The Wall was stone. It was old. It was high. It was the last defense against the Hush.

Eden went with a group. Twenty students. The Winter Rotation. Three weeks on the Wall. Training. Watching. Waiting.

The Wall was cold. The wind was strong. Snow on the ground. Ice on the stone. Eden wore his black armor under his coat. He wore gloves. He wore a scarf.

The first day, they stood on the Wall. They looked south. The Pale was there. Grey. Flat. Silent. It stretched to the horizon. No life. No sound. Just grey.

"That is the Pale," the instructor said. "It is where the Hush has eaten the land. Nothing grows there. Nothing lives there. It is dead."

Eden looked at the Pale. It was vast. It was empty. It was quiet.

He thought about the invasion. 500 years. 10 generations. The Pale had been growing for 500 years. The Wall had held for 500 years. But the Pale was still growing.

He looked at the students. They were scared. They had never seen the Pale. They had only read about it.

Eden was not scared. He had seen worse. In his dreams. In the Tutor's lessons. The Pale was just the beginning. The Hush was the real enemy. And the Hush was patient.

"Eden," Lysette said. She was beside him. She had come on the rotation. "Are you okay?"

"Yes," Eden said.

"The Pale is... big."

"Yes."

"It is scary."

"Yes."

They stood on the Wall. They watched the Pale. The wind blew. The snow fell. The Wall held.

Eden thought about the invasion. 500 years. The world had adapted. Crown Wells. Knight orders. The Southern Wall. The Thirteenth Vigil. All to end the invasion. All to push back the Pale.

But the Pale was still there. And the Hush was still there. And the Veils were in the walls.

Eden would fight. He always fought. But he could not fight the Pale alone. He needed the circle. He needed the army. He needed everyone.

He looked at Lysette. She was cold. Her nose was red. Her hair blew in the wind.

"Let us go inside," he said.

They went inside. The barracks were warm. A fire. Food. Rest.

Eden sat by the fire. He warmed his hands. He thought about the Pale. He thought about the Wall. He thought about the 500 years.

He would not let it be 501. He would end it. Somehow. Someday.

He did not know how. But he would find a way.

He always found a way.
