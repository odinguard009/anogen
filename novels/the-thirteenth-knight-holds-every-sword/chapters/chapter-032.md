# Chapter 32: The Choir Silence

Night on the Wall. The stars were out. The Pale was dark. No moon. No light. Just stars and snow.

Eden stood on the Wall. He was on watch. Two hours. He held his lance. He wore his black armor. The wind was cold.

He heard a sound. Not a normal sound. A silence. A silence that was too quiet. Like someone had taken the sound away.

He stopped. He listened. The wind was gone. The snow was gone. The stars were gone. Everything was gone. Just silence.

"Choir," Eden whispered.

A Choir was a Hushborn. It was not a monster. It was a sound. A silence. It ate sound. It ate light. It ate everything.

Eden could not see. He could not hear. He could only feel. The stone under his feet. The cold in his armor. The lance in his hand.

He moved. One step. Two. He touched the Wall. The stone was solid. He followed it. Left hand on the stone.

He heard a sound. A footstep. Not his. Someone else was on the Wall. Someone who should not be there.

Eden stopped. He held his breath. He listened. The silence was thick. Like water. He could not hear anything.

He felt a hand on his shoulder. He turned. He thrust. His lance hit something. Something hard. Metal. Or bone.

A voice. "Eden. It is me. Lysette."

Eden stopped. He lowered his lance. "Lysette. You should not be here."

"I heard the silence. I came to find you."

"The Choir. It is here. We need to move. Together."

They moved. Side by side. Left hands on the Wall. Right hands holding weapons. Eden's lance. Lysette's knife.

The silence followed them. It was close. Eden could feel it. Like a cold breath on his neck.

They reached the gate. The gate was closed. Eden knocked. Three times. The code. The gate opened. They went inside.

The silence stayed outside. It did not follow. It could not enter the gate. The gate was warded. Mana in the stone. The Choir could not cross.

Eden and Lysette stood inside. They breathed. The sound came back. The wind. The snow. The stars.

"That was close," Lysette said.

"Yes," Eden said.

"You knew what to do."

"I have been trained."

"By whom?"

Eden did not answer. He looked at the gate. The stone was cold. The wards glowed faintly. Blue. Mana.

"By the Tutor," Eden said. "In dreams."

Lysette looked at him. "Dreams?"

"Yes. The Tutor teaches me. In dreams."

Lysette was quiet. Then she said, "I want to meet him."

"You cannot. He is in my mind."

"Then tell me what he teaches you."

Eden looked at her. Her blue-violet eyes were steady. She was not afraid. She was curious.

"He teaches me about doors," Eden said. "About shadows. About mana. About things that are not human."

Lysette nodded. "Teach me."

"Maybe," Eden said.

They went to the barracks. They sat by the fire. They warmed their hands. The silence was gone. The Wall held.

Eden thought about the Choir. It was a small thing. A sound. But it was dangerous. In the dark, with no light, a Choir could kill.

He needed to be ready. He needed light. He needed edges. Doors needed edges. And edges needed light.

He would carry a lamp. Always. From now on.

He wrote in his notebook: "Choir. Silence. Eats sound and light. Need lamp. Need edges. Doors need light."

He closed the notebook. He slept. No dreams. The Tutor did not come. Not tonight.
