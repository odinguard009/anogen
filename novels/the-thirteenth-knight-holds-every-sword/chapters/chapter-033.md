# Chapter 33: The Inspection

The inspection was on the third day. An officer came. He was tall. He wore a white coat. He had a clipboard.

He checked everything. The Wall. The gates. The wards. The soldiers. The students.

Eden stood with the students. He held his lance. He wore his armor. The officer looked at him.

"You are Eden Wren," the officer said.

"Yes," Eden said.

"The top student. The one who skips class."

Eden did not answer.

"I have heard about you," the officer said. "You fought a construct. You found a Veil. You are... unusual."

"I am a student," Eden said.

The officer looked at him. "You are more than a student. I can see it."

He moved on. He checked the next student. Eden relaxed.

Lysette was beside him. "He knows," she whispered.

"He suspects," Eden said. "He does not know."

"How do you know?"

"Because if he knew, he would not be inspecting. He would be arresting."

Lysette was quiet. The officer continued. He checked the Wall. He found a crack. He wrote it down. He checked the wards. They were strong. He wrote that down too.

At the end, the officer stood before the group. "The Wall is strong," he said. "The wards hold. The soldiers are ready. But the Pale is growing. Every year, it grows. We need more soldiers. More wards. More mana."

He looked at the students. "You are the future. You will hold the Wall. You will fight the Hush. You will not fail."

The students nodded. Eden did not nod. He watched the officer. The officer was worried. Eden could see it. The officer knew the Wall was not enough. The Pale was too big. The Hush was too strong.

Eden thought about this. The Wall held. But the Pale grew. The Hush was patient. It had been growing for 500 years. It could grow for 500 more.

Someone had to stop it. Not just hold it. Stop it.

Eden did not know how. But he would find a way.

He always found a way.

---

After the inspection, Eden went to the cistern. The water was still dark. Still cold. He did not touch it.

He went to the gate. The wards glowed. Blue. He touched them. They were strong.

He went to the Pale. He stood on the Wall and looked south. The Pale was there. Grey. Flat. Silent. But tonight, there was something else. Movement. Far away. In the grey.

Eden watched. He used his telescope. He saw shapes. Moving. Slow. They were not human. They were Hushborn. Small ones. Crawling. On the ground. Moving toward the Wall.

Eden counted. Ten. Twenty. Thirty. More. They were coming. Not fast. But steady. They would reach the Wall in three days.

He flashed a signal. Three flashes. Pause. One flash. "Enemy approaching."

The next tower flashed back. "How many?"

Eden flashed: "Many. Thirty plus. Moving slow."

The tower flashed: "We will prepare."

Eden put down the mirror. He looked at the Pale. The shapes moved. Slow. Steady. Like a river of grey.

He went to the barracks. He woke the commander. "Hushborn. Thirty plus. Approaching from the south. Three days."

The commander looked at him. "How do you know?"

"I saw them."

"From where?"

"The Wall. With a telescope."

The commander looked at the telescope. He looked at Eden. "You are a student."

"I am a student who sees things," Eden said.

The commander did not argue. He sounded the alarm. The Wall came alive. Soldiers. Weapons. Wards. Everything.

Eden stayed. He watched. He planned. Three days. He had three days to prepare.

He would be ready. He always was.
