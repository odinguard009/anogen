# Chapter 34: The Cistern

The cistern was under the Wall. It was a large room. Stone. Water. Dark. It held water for the soldiers.

Eden went down. He had a lamp. The light was yellow. The walls were wet. The floor was stone. Water dripped. Drip. Drip. Drip.

He was looking for something. The archivist had told him about the cistern. "The Thirteenth Vigil used the cistern," the archivist had said. "Long ago. There are marks on the walls."

Eden found the marks. They were on the far wall. Scratches. In the stone. He held his lamp close.

The marks were the same code. The Thirteenth Vigil code. He read them.

"Do not trust the water. The water carries the Hush. The Hush is in the veins. The veins are in the stone. The stone is in the Wall. The Wall is in the water."

Eden read it again. "The Hush is in the veins." The mana veins. The Hush traveled through mana veins. Like water through pipes. The Hush was in the water. The water was in the cistern. The cistern was in the Wall.

Eden looked at the water. It was dark. Still. He could not see the bottom. He did not know how deep it was.

He dipped his hand in the water. It was cold. Very cold. Colder than it should be. The Wall was cold, but the water was colder. Too cold.

He pulled his hand out. His fingers were numb. He wiped them on his coat. He looked at the water again.

The Hush was in the water. The Hush was in the veins. The veins were in the stone. The stone was in the Wall.

The Wall was not just a wall. It was a container. It held the Hush. The Hush was inside the stone. Inside the water. Inside the mana veins.

If the Wall broke, the Hush would come out. Not from the south. From inside. From the stone. From the water.

Eden backed away. He went up the stairs. He went to the barracks. He sat by the fire. He warmed his hands.

He wrote in his notebook: "Cistern. Thirteenth Vigil code. Hush in veins. Hush in water. Wall contains Hush. If Wall breaks, Hush comes from inside."

He closed the notebook. He looked at the fire. The flames were warm. The fire was good. The fire was light. Light was good.

He thought about the lamps. The lamps were eyes. The Hush was in the water. The Hush was in the veins. The Hush was everywhere.

He needed to find a way to stop it. Not just hold it. Stop it.

He did not know how. But he would find a way.

He always found a way.
