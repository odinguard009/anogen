# Chapter 35: The Lodge Story

The lodge was a small building. Near the Wall. It was for soldiers. It had a fire. It had food. It had beds.

Eden sat in the lodge. He ate rations. Hard bread. Dried meat. Water. The food was bad. But it was warm.

A soldier sat beside him. He was old. His face was lined. His hands were rough. He wore a uniform. It was patched.

"You are the student," the soldier said.

"Yes," Eden said.

"The one who found the Veil."

"Yes."

The soldier ate his bread. He chewed slowly. "I have been on this Wall for thirty years," he said. "I have seen the Pale grow. I have seen the Hush come. I have seen men die."

Eden listened. He did not speak.

"Thirty years," the soldier said. "And the Wall still holds. But for how long? The Pale grows. The Hush comes. We lose men. We gain men. But the Pale grows."

He looked at Eden. "You are young. You have time. You will see the Pale grow more. You will see men die. You will see the Hush come."

"Or I will stop it," Eden said.

The soldier looked at him. He did not laugh. He did not smile. He looked at Eden's eyes. He saw something there.

"Maybe," the soldier said. "Maybe you will. Or maybe you will be one more name on the Wall."

He stood. He left. His footsteps were heavy. The door closed.

Eden sat by the fire. He ate his bread. It was stale. He did not mind.

He thought about the soldier. Thirty years on the Wall. Thirty years of watching the Pale grow. Thirty years of men dying.

Eden did not want to be on the Wall for thirty years. He wanted to end it. He wanted to push the Pale back. He wanted to close the Wound.

He did not know how. But he would find a way.

He always found a way.
