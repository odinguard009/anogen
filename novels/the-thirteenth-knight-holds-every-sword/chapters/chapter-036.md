# Chapter 36: The Signal Mirror

Eden stood on the Wall. He had a signal mirror. Small. Metal. He used it to flash light. To send messages. To signal.

He flashed a message to the next tower. Three flashes. Pause. Two flashes. The code for "all clear."

The next tower flashed back. Three flashes. Pause. Two flashes. "All clear."

Eden put down the mirror. He looked south. The Pale was there. Grey. Silent. Nothing moved.

He thought about the signal mirror. It was simple. Light. Flash. Message. No wires. No electricity. Just light.

The Veils were in the walls. The lamps were their eyes. But the signal mirror was not a lamp. It was not connected to wires. It was not connected to the exchange. It was safe.

Eden would use the signal mirror. He would send messages. He would warn the Wall. He would tell them about the Veils. About the lamps. About the Ashen Crowns.

But he could not tell them everything. He could not tell them about the Thirteenth Vigil. He could not tell them about the Tutor. He could not tell them about the Hush in the veins.

He could tell them about the clerk. About the lamp houses. About the dye-works. About the teacher.

That was enough. For now.

He flashed a message. Three flashes. Pause. One flash. "Enemy spotted."

The next tower flashed back. "Where?"

Eden did not answer. He did not know where. He just knew the enemy was there. Somewhere. In the walls. In the lamps. In the water.

He put down the mirror. He looked at the Pale. It was still. It was quiet. It was waiting.

Eden waited too. He was patient. He could wait longer than the Pale.

He always could.
