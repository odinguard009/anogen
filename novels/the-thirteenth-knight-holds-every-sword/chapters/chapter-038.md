# Chapter 38: The Fire

Fire. The dining hall was on fire.

Eden woke to the smell. Smoke. He opened his eyes. His room was dark. Smoke came under the door.

He stood. He opened the door. The hall was full of smoke. Students ran. Screaming. The fire was in the dining hall. Flames. Orange. Hot.

Eden did not panic. He put on his coat. He took his lance. He went to the hall.

The fire was large. It had started in the kitchen. The kitchen. Maren's kitchen. Eden ran to the kitchen.

The door was hot. He opened it. The kitchen was on fire. Stoves. Oil. Everything burned. Maren was on the floor. She was not moving.

Eden went in. He grabbed Maren. He pulled her out. She was heavy. She was not breathing.

He put her on the floor. He pressed his palm to her chest. Mana flowed. Not much. Just enough. Maren coughed. She breathed.

"Eden," she said. "The fire. It started in the oil. Someone poured oil on the stove."

"Who?" Eden asked.

"I do not know. I was cooking. I turned around. The stove was on fire."

Eden looked at the kitchen. The oil. The stove. Someone had poured oil on the stove and lit it. On purpose.

He helped Maren up. She was shaking. Her hands were burned. Eden took her to the infirmary.

The fire was out by morning. The dining hall was damaged. The kitchen was destroyed. No one died. But Maren was hurt. And the fire was not an accident.

Eden went to the roof garden. He sat on the bench. He wrote in his notebook: "Fire. Kitchen. Maren hurt. Arson. Oil on stove. Someone wants to hurt the academy. Or someone wants to hurt Maren."

He closed the notebook. He thought about Maren. She was kind. She gave him food. She did not ask questions. Someone had hurt her.

Eden would find out who. He always found out.

He went to the infirmary. Maren was in bed. Her hands were bandaged. She looked at him.

"You saved me," she said.

"I pulled you out," Eden said.

"You saved me," Maren said again.

Eden sat beside her. "Who did this?"

"I do not know. But Eden. Be careful. The person who did this is still here."

"I know," Eden said.

He stayed with her. She slept. He watched the door. No one came.

He thought about the fire. The Ashen Crowns. The lamps. The Veils. Now fire. They were escalating. They were not just watching. They were attacking.

Eden would have to act. Soon. Before more people got hurt.

He would act. He always acted.
