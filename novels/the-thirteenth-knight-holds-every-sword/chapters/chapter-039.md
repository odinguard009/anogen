# Chapter 39: Rowan's Question

Rowan found Eden on the roof. Rowan was out of breath. He had run up the stairs.

"Eden," Rowan said. "I need to ask you something."

"Ask," Eden said.

Rowan sat beside him. He was quiet. Then he said, "Clara. The girl from the Wall. She is missing."

Eden looked at him. "Missing?"

"She did not come back from the Winter Rotation. They said she was reassigned. But she was not. I checked."

Eden thought about Clara. She was a student. She had been at the Wall. She had been quiet. She had been kind.

"When did she disappear?" Eden asked.

"Three days ago. After the inspection."

Eden thought. The inspection. The officer. The crack in the Wall. The Choir. The cistern.

"Rowan," Eden said. "Do not look for her."

"Why not?"

"Because if she is missing, someone took her. And if someone took her, they are watching. If you look for her, they will see you."

"I do not care," Rowan said. "She is my friend."

"I know. But you cannot help her if you are dead."

Rowan was quiet. His hands were clenched. His jaw was tight.

"Eden," Rowan said. "You know things. You always know things. Tell me what you know."

Eden looked at him. Rowan's eyes were wet. He was scared. He was angry.

"I know that the Wall is not safe," Eden said. "I know that the Hush is in the water. I know that the Veils are in the walls. I know that someone took Clara."

"Who?"

"I do not know yet."

"Then find out."

"I will," Eden said. "But you must wait."

Rowan stood. He left. His footsteps were heavy. The door slammed.

Eden sat on the bench. He thought about Clara. She was missing. Three days. The inspection. The officer.

He would find out. He always found out. But he needed time. And time was running out.

He opened his notebook. He wrote: "Clara missing. 3 days. After inspection. Rowan angry. Need to find her. Need time."

He closed the notebook. He looked at the city. The lamps glowed. The trams ran. The city was normal.

But Clara was not normal. Clara was missing. And Eden had to find her.

He would find her. He always found things.
