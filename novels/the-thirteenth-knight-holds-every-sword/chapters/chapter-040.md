# Chapter 40: Ysolde

Ysolde came to the academy. She was the king's secretary. She was 38. She was tall. She had dark hair. She wore a grey coat. She was deadpan. She never smiled.

She met Eden in the headmistress's office. Hildegard was there. Ysolde sat across from Eden.

"Eden Wren," Ysolde said.

"Eden Aster Argentine," Eden said.

Ysolde looked at him. "So you know."

"I have always known," Eden said.

"Then you know what I am here for."

"The king's envelope," Eden said.

Ysolde nodded. She took an envelope from her coat. She put it on the desk. "From the king. For you."

Eden took the envelope. He did not open it. He put it in his coat.

"What does it say?" Hildegard asked.

Ysolde looked at her. "That is between the king and Eden."

Hildegard nodded. She did not push.

Ysolde stood. "Eden. The king is worried. The Pale is growing. The Ashen Crowns are active. The Veils are in the walls. He needs you."

"I know," Eden said.

"Do you? The king holds a kill-order. If the Void wakes, he will kill you. He does not want to. But he will."

"I know," Eden said again.

Ysolde looked at him. Her face was stone. "Be careful, Eden. The king is not your enemy. But he is not your friend either. He is a king. He does what kings do."

She left. Her footsteps were quiet. She was gone.

Hildegard looked at Eden. "What was that about?"

"Nothing," Eden said.

"It was not nothing. I am old. I am half-blind. But I am not stupid."

Eden looked at her. "I know you are not stupid, Dame Hildegard."

"Then tell me."

"I cannot. Not yet."

Hildegard was quiet. Then she said, "When you can, tell me. I have watched you for two years. I know you are not a normal student. I know you do things. I do not know what. But I know."

"Thank you," Eden said.

He left. He went to his room. He opened the envelope. Inside was a letter. From the king.

It said: "Eden. The world is changing. The Pale grows. The Ashen Crowns are bold. I need you. Not as a student. As an Argentine. Come to Solmarch. We will talk. Ysolde will arrange it. Be careful. The walls have ears. — Theodric."

Eden read it again. The king wanted him. Not as a student. As an Argentine. As an heir.

Eden put the letter back in the envelope. He put it under his bed. Next to the Thirteenth Vigil file.

He sat on his bed. He thought about the king. The king held a kill-order. If the Void woke, the king would kill him. But the king also needed him.

That was politics. That was power. Eden did not like it. But he understood it.

He would go to Solmarch. He would talk to the king. He would find out what the king wanted.

But first, he had to find Clara. And stop the Ashen Crowns. And deal with the teacher. And the Veils. And the lamps.

He had a lot to do. He always had a lot to do.

He opened his notebook. He wrote: "King's letter. Wants me in Solmarch. As Argentine. Kill-order still active. Ysolde delivered. Need to go. But first: Clara. Ashen Crowns. Teacher."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He stared at the ceiling.

The crack in the corner looked like a river. It always looked like a river.

He slept. No dreams.
