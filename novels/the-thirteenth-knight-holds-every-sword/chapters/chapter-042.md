# Chapter 42: Roll Call

Morning. The Wall was busy. Soldiers everywhere. Weapons checked. Wards tested. Food prepared.

Eden stood with the soldiers. He was not a soldier. He was a student. But he was there. He had seen the Hushborn. He had warned them. They let him stay.

The commander called roll. Names. One by one. Each soldier answered. "Here."

Eden was not on the list. He did not answer. He stood at the back. He watched.

Lysette was there. She had come back. She stood beside Eden. She had her notebook. She had her knife.

"You should not be here," Eden said.

"I am here," Lysette said.

The commander finished roll call. He looked at the group. "Hushborn. Thirty plus. Approaching. Three days. We will hold the Wall. We will not let them through."

The soldiers cheered. It was not a big cheer. It was a small cheer. Tired. But real.

Eden did not cheer. He watched the soldiers. Some were old. Some were young. Some were scared. Some were calm.

He thought about the fight. Three days. Thirty Hushborn. The Wall. The wards. The soldiers.

He had a plan. He would use the doors. The Hushborn moved on the ground. If he opened doors in their path, he could drop them. Into the cistern. Into the basement. Into the void.

But he needed edges. Light. The Hushborn moved in the dark. He needed lamps. He needed light.

He would carry lamps. He would set them along the Wall. He would create edges. Doors needed edges.

He told the commander. The commander listened. He did not understand doors. But he understood light. He ordered lamps set along the Wall.

Eden watched. The lamps were set. The light was bright. The edges were there.

He was ready. Or as ready as he could be.

He wrote in his notebook: "Roll call. Soldiers ready. Hushborn 3 days. Lamps set. Edges ready. Plan: doors in path. Drop them."

He closed the notebook. He looked at the Pale. The shapes moved. Slow. Steady.

Three days.
