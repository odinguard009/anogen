# Chapter 47: The Loan

The loan was Thessa. Thessa was a relic. A small relic. A stone. It could hold a corridor. It could block a path. It was not a weapon. It was a tool.

Eden had found Thessa in the archivist's office. It was in a box. Labeled: "Minor relic. Thessa. Loan only."

Eden took it. He did not ask. He would return it.

He gave Thessa to Felix. Felix was on the Wall. He had come to help. He was a Silent Assassin. He was fast. He was quiet.

"Take this," Eden said. "Hold the corridor."

Felix took the stone. He looked at it. "What does it do?"

"It holds. You put it on the ground. It blocks the path. Nothing can pass."

Felix nodded. He put the stone on the ground. The stone glowed. Faintly. Blue. A wall of light appeared. Invisible. But solid.

"Thessa," Felix said.

"Yes."

"I will hold the corridor."

Eden left. He went to the Wall. He fought. He opened doors. He dropped Hushborn.

Felix held the corridor. The stone glowed. The wall held. No Hushborn passed.

But the Hushborn were smart. They went around. They found another path. They attacked from the side.

Felix moved. He took the stone. He ran. He placed it on the new path. The wall appeared. The Hushborn stopped.

Eden saw this. He nodded. Felix was good. Felix was reliable.

The battle lasted all day. By evening, the Hushborn were gone. The Wall held. The camp was destroyed, but the Wall held.

Eden found Felix. Felix was sitting on the ground. The stone was in his hand. It was dark. It had used all its mana.

"Good work," Eden said.

Felix looked up. "I held the corridor."

"You held it well."

Felix smiled. It was small. But real. "Thank you, Eden."

Eden nodded. He took the stone. He put it in his pocket. He would return it to the archivist.

He went to his room. He sat on his bed. He wrote in his notebook: "Thessa. Loan to Felix. Held corridor. Felix reliable. Good."

He closed the notebook. He lay back. He thought about Felix. Felix was his friend. His one close friend. Felix had fought beside him. Felix had held the corridor.

Eden was grateful. He did not say it. He never said it. But he was grateful.

He slept. No dreams.
