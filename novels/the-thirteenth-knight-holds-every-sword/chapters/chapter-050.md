# Chapter 50: The Morning

Morning. The Wall. The Pale. The sky.

Eden stood on the Wall. He looked south. The Pale was there. Grey. Silent. But something was different. The light. The dawn light. It fell on the Pale. And for a moment, just a moment, the Pale was not grey. It was green.

Green. Like grass. Like life. Like hope.

Eden stared. The green was faint. It lasted only a second. Then the Pale was grey again. But Eden had seen it. Green. In the Pale.

He thought about this. Green in the Pale. Life in the dead. Hope in the grey.

Maybe the Pale could be healed. Maybe the land could be green again. Maybe the Wound could be closed. Maybe the Hush could be ended.

Maybe.

Eden did not know. But he had seen green. And that was enough.

He turned. He looked north. The city. Solmarch. The academy. The world. Alive. Green. Full of people. Full of light.

He would protect it. He would fight for it. He would end the invasion. For the green. For the light. For the people.

He walked down from the Wall. He went to the barracks. He packed his things. He was leaving. The Winter Rotation was over.

Lysette was there. She was packing too. She looked at him.

"Eden," she said.

"Yes."

"The Pale. This morning. Did you see it?"

"Yes."

"Green."

"Yes."

Lysette was quiet. Then she said, "Maybe it can be healed."

"Maybe," Eden said.

They packed. They left the Wall. They took the train back to Solmarch. The train rocked. The hills passed. The lamps of small towns glowed in the distance.

Eden looked out the window. The world was green. The world was alive. The world was worth fighting for.

He closed his eyes. He did not sleep. He thought about the green. He thought about the Wound. He thought about Aiwa. He thought about the future.

He would end the invasion. He would close the Wound. He would bring green to the Pale.

He did not know how. But he would find a way.

He always found a way.

The train rocked. The world passed. Eden watched.

The morning was bright. The morning was green. The morning was hope.

Eden smiled. It was small. But it was real.

He had smiled. Once. On the roof. With Lysette. And now. On the train. Alone.

He smiled. Because of the green. Because of the hope. Because of the morning.

He would fight. He would win. He would end it.

For the green. For the light. For the world.

The train rolled on. Eden watched. The world passed. The morning came.

And Eden smiled.
