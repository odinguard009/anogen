# Perbandingan gaya: bab lama vs bab baru (bab 1-3)

Sumber: lama = `archive/restart-2026-11-25/chapters/`, baru = `chapters/`. Kutipan asli, tidak diubah. Penilaian di kolom "Catatan" adalah pendapat saya, bukan hasil pengukuran.

## Bab 1: pembuka

**Lama**
> Professor Brandmeier checked the names twice in every Spell Grammar class. She did it once at the first bell and again forty minutes before the end.
> She thought students ran away at the halfway point, like geese crossing a road. She was right about the geese. Her second check gave me a plan. If I left after the first one, I had eighty minutes before anyone knew I was gone.
> Eighty minutes, minus the stairs.

**Baru**
> Spell Grammar lasted fifty minutes, and I had already spent ten of them on the stairs. That left forty. I meant to use every one.

Catatan: pembuka lama punya tokoh (Brandmeier), kebiasaan spesifik, lelucon kecil ("She was right about the geese"), dan alasan kenapa Eden punya rencana. Pembuka baru lebih bersih dan mudah dibaca pelajar, tapi hanya angka, tanpa tokoh atau kepribadian.

## Bab 1: atap

**Lama**
> Someone had made a garden on it long ago and then, I always guessed, got bored. ... It had no eyepiece, and it pointed at a piece of sky that stopped being interesting eighty years ago. ... I put it in the box in my head where I keep things that are none of my business.

**Baru**
> But up here the grass was thick and green, as if nobody had told it. ... In the corner lay a broken telescope on its side, rusty and useless, like a big dead insect. ... asking questions was not my job. A good bed is a good bed.

Catatan: keduanya mengatakan "rumput hijau di musim gugur". Versi lama memberi Eden cara berpikir sendiri (kotak di kepala). Versi baru memakai perbandingan umum ("like a big dead insect") dan kalimat penutup yang rapi ("A good bed is a good bed").

## Bab 2: jadwal dari Lysette

**Lama**
> *Any absence will be treated as a flight risk and pursued.* ... the handwriting was so straight it looked angry. By then I had already lost the war.

**Baru**
> TIMETABLE, YEAR 5. ... Signed, L. Hollen. She had written every line in a different colour of ink, and she had underlined the word "attendance" twice. It was the scariest paper anyone had ever handed me.

Catatan: jadwal lama terdengar seperti suara Lysette (kaku, sedikit lucu, bernada perintah). Versi baru lebih datar. Detail tinta berwarna dan garis bawah itu bagus, tapi penutup "scariest paper" menjelaskan perasaan, bukan menunjukkannya.

## Bab 3: pembuka malam pasar

**Lama**
> The tax clerk bought the same thing every night. It was one candied pear on a stick ... For three nights the old woman's cat got up and left when he came close ... "The cat knows," I said. "I can't file a cat."

**Baru**
> The tax clerk smiled at every lantern seller in the market, and the smile never changed. ... For a market, it was beautiful. For a hunt, it was a bad place, because every face looked friendly and every shadow moved.

Catatan: versi lama membuka dengan petunjuk aneh yang spesifik (kucing menghindari si juru pajak) lalu dialog lucu singkat dengan Ysolde. Versi baru membuka dengan suasana pasar dan menjelaskan latarnya ke pembaca ("For a hunt, it was a bad place..."). Paragraf pengenalan Vesk di versi baru juga berupa deskripsi berurutan, bukan ditunjukkan lewat aksi.

## Pola yang terlihat di ketiganya
1. Lama: dibuka dengan detail aneh yang spesifik. Baru: dibuka dengan keterangan umum.
2. Lama: humor keluar dari watak (Eden, Ysolde, Vesk). Baru: humor lebih sedikit dan bergantung pada pengulangan lelucon.
3. Lama: Eden punya suara sendiri. Baru: narasi lebih netral dan lebih jelas bagi pelajar bahasa Inggris, tapi lebih datar.
4. Baru: sering ada kalimat rangkuman rapi di ujung paragraf atau bab.
