# Royal Academy of Hallowcrest (reference)

## Basics
- Terraced plateau above Solmarch, reached by the Long Stair and a vein-stone funicular. Founded PR 9 "to forge the hands that push the grey back". The most famous academy on Elysmar; foreign exchange places are a diplomatic weapon.
- Run by the Bellwardens Order. Headmistress Dame Hildegard Ostwald (half-blind, dry, Liora's old friend; knows Eden's name and power, not the Thirteenth Vigil). Vice-headmaster Halvard Strent runs daily life (beloved; the Tailor).
- About 2,400 students, 400 per year.

## Six years; the Twentieth Rule
- Entry at 14 (after the Kindling at 12-14) with a Bell test of at least 2 Bells.
- Years 1-2 Foundations: mana control, spell grammar, history, riding, arms, languages.
- Years 3-4 Arms and Tenet: Tenet discipline, duels, Undercroft drills (training maze under the plateau). Year 4: refugee-convoy escorts.
- Year 5 Field: five-student Lances; Winter Rotation on the Southern Wall (live Hushborn, supervised).
- Year 6 Command: lead younger Lances, Concord honor guard, the Crossing (a night on the Teeth beyond the Wall). Graduation at 19-20; postings the same month to the front or as squire-knights to an order.
- Failed years are repeated. The Twentieth Rule: no student may remain enrolled past their 20th year (expelled on their 21st birthday). Dropouts usually go to the Wall as common soldiers.
- Eden: entered PR 108 at 14; now Year 5 at 18; graduates end of Year 6 (ch 200) at 19. Year 5 = arcs 1-2 (ch 1-100); Year 6 = arcs 3-4 (ch 101-200).

## Ranks, exams, life
- The Ladder: public year ranking from semester exams (theory, arms, field marks) and sanctioned duels. Top ten wear a bronze pin ("the Ten"). Year 5 now: 1 Valerius, 2 Rowan, 3 Clara Belmont, 4 Lysette, 6 Seraphine, 8 Eden (always 7-9, never higher). Felix: 19.
- Semesters: autumn (Bell Assessment, midterms, the Lantern Ball at semester's end, finals), winter break with Year 5's Winter Rotation, spring (the Silver Lists tourney, finals), summer break.
- The Lantern Ball: end-of-autumn dance in the glass atrium; students invite partners. Year 5's is the first where invitations are formal.
- Steward Council (student government; president Valerius). Discipline Committee (enforces attendance, curfew, duel rules; vice-chair Lysette, who does the actual work). Clubs: fencing, griffon-riding, star-reading, choir, cooking club (chaos), and the Rooftop Garden club (one member, Lysette, secretly).
- Dorms: stone towers by year; Eden rooms alone (permit). Curfew at the tenth bell.

## The rooftop
The Old Observatory's roof garden: grass, a dwarf apple tree, herb beds, a broken telescope. Lysette tends it at dawn and cries there when she must. Eden sleeps there most days under Ostwald's quiet permit (on paper: "medical exemption"). Neither knew the other used it until ch 1.

## Key staff
- Tesfane Okoru: Arms and Field instructor, Year 5 Lance supervisor; one-armed Mansakar refugee; Tenet Stitch.
- Professor Ilse Brandmeier: Spell Grammar, terrifying, comic foil.
- Halvard Strent: History of the Breach; hums an old lullaby while grading; never stands directly under lamps.

## Year 5 Lances (formed ch 6)
- Lance Nine: Eden, Felix, Lysette (assigned to "supervise" him), Rowan, Clara Belmont (the girl Rowan invited to the Ball).
- Lance One: Valerius, Seraphine and three others.
