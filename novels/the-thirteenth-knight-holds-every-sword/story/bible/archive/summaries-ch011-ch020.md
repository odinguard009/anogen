## Ch 11 - The Direction of Shadows

In the first graded field run, Okoru sends the five Lances through the Undercroft maze with lamps, false walls and two timed constructs. Lance Nine works as a unit for the first time: Felix's Trail reads dragged stone, Lysette's Witness replays the hands that moved a wall, Rowan and Clara handle the constructs, and Eden reads the lamp shadows to find a gap over the exit without taking credit. They finish third in eleven minutes. A man in a staff coat watches Rowan from the dark gallery and vanishes when Eden looks up.



## Ch 12 - Perrin

Felix finds Eden behind the stables and asks why nobody gets close to him. Eden says he does not know how. Felix gives his middle name, Perrin, before asking for anything, and Eden says honestly that he has no name he can give back. Lysette arrives with a second lunch, says she is not losing track of him, and does not look at him once. Felix tells her his middle name on his way out. The two-lunch routine begins.



## Ch 13 - The Empty Frame

Eden ends a Veil wearing the Long Stair's lamplighter in the lamp-house at third bell and leaves no trace. In the morning Lysette runs the Committee inquiry while Eden stands against the wall. Her **[Witness]** shows a figure in black and a door-shaped shadow, and her **[Testimony]** shows the replay to the students before her wrist gives out. She finds an empty pale frame-shape on the wall, like one on her desk at school, and refuses to explain it. Last line: "The Thirteenth Vigil was here. I will find out who opens the doors."



## Ch 14 - The Herb Bed

Eden tries to keep a perfect nap record while Lysette's attendance campaign grows into warrants, runners and a stakeout. Felix plays double agent for lunch. Seraphine's joke count on Felix reaches nineteen. Eden sleeps in Lysette's herb bed for eleven minutes; she finds him, leaves him asleep, covers his boots with her coat, and asks him to tell Felix only the number. She knows he has kept her garden secret for a month.



## Ch 15 - The Boy Who Pays for Bread

Lysette follows Eden into the lower city on a Committee pretext while he scouts the dye-works from a distance for Ysolde. He knows she is there, changes route to keep her away from the Ember watcher, and ends at a refugee bakery where the children know his name. He pays for bread and does not eat it. The two agree not to write down the walk back. That night the Dragon stops mid-complaint with "I promised someone" and will not say who.





## Ch 16 - This Line Is Not Crossed

Valerius demands a sanctioned Ladder duel. In the sand yard he makes pale walls with ****[Injunction]****, each ruling costing a breath, while Eden stays behind them and refuses to use the lance point. Eden's ****[Hinge]**** turns a charge past him and lands the butt behind the knee; he keeps every miss within a finger's width. Valerius notices the restraint and the count of his breaths. The bell ends the round and Valerius drops his sword: "Again. And this time try."


## Ch 17 - A Finger's Width
The duel resumes. Valerius presses honestly and Eden repeats **[Hinge]** until it is no longer a surprise. He waits for the bell, moves his shield hand a finger's width, and lets the prince's blade touch him as the bell's sound begins, so the judges record a narrow loss. Lysette binds his split knuckles too hard and scolds while he lets her. Valerius, angry but controlled, says his father knows who Eden is, and so does he, and next time he will make him show them.


## Ch 18 - Alive, on the Night of Lights
Ysolde orders Eden to watch Rowan without being seen. Rowan notices everything except the grey-coat man following him through the lower city. In an unlit lane Eden opens a Door in a wall shadow, puts the watcher asleep in an empty cellar without a chase, and reads his book: Corwen, bagman. A folded copy of the exchange call list carries two added words, "Alive. Night of lights." Ysolde confirms the list is the Ball guest list. The Ball is nine days away.


## Ch 19 - The East Kitchen
Rowan volunteers Lance Nine for the cooking club's Ball-week bake. Clara heals a burn but cannot raise Seraphine's fallen soufflé, and Seraphine's joke count on Felix reaches forty-one and fails. When the oven throws flame across the kitchen, Eden silently folds **[Exa]** into the door-shield and turns the blast back into the oven; Seraphine calls it luck and he agrees. Rowan almost notices the garden soil on Lysette's apron but a dropped tray of rolls distracts him. Seraphine asks Lysette who she is going to the Ball with, and she writes "Nobody."


## Ch 20 - The Bend in the Line
At the war council Valerius hears ministers whisper of a hidden "Void Host" and orders a paper with names, not a summary. He studies the unexplained bend in the front around Verandel and sees his father's tiredness in the empty chair and the untouched cup. Count Merrow, his old map teacher, tells him to worry about the Ball instead: four hundred people, a hundred lanterns, one band, and the prince's name on the paper if anything goes wrong. Merrow asks which hall the lanterns hang in, and Valerius answers without thinking twice.
