# Arc roadmap, part 1b (arcs 5-7, ch 201-350). Same rules as arcs-1.md.

## Arc 5 The Teeth (201-250) monster war, siege. Graduates posted.
Q: can they hold the narrowest place on Elysmar? Enemy: Hierarch the Tidewright, who drains the strait to make a land bridge.
Beats: Eden posted as a junior knight under the Crimson Rampart; Kirana's fleet guards the strait's sea side; the feather truth (205); kill-order revealed (214), Eden keeps fighting for a king who might kill him; Felix's friendship began as an order from his father (236), bond strained then healed; Sixth Pillar Sefa (240); Clara replaced by a Veil during a night patrol (228, not yet known); Lord Marshal Gideon Valdis dies holding the inner gate (247).
Tactics: falling-tide war; drum-code against Choirs; mining under Mawgates; door-bridges over a drained strait.
Win (245-250): the Tidewright drains the strait for a final push. Eden prepared for a storm-front with Kirana's forecasts; under the clouds' shadow he wakes 10,000 of the First Legion while Valerius' Verdict holds the breach. Victory is certain once he chooses; the cost is that the world sees black knights. Cost: rumours of black knights at the Teeth reach every capital; Gideon's death.
Growth: Valerius takes Gideon's place at the Wall; Rowan learns to command, not shine.
Resolution: the Teeth hold, Thrennos and the Tidewright fall, Gideon is honoured, the kill-order faced and survived (Eden chooses to serve anyway), Felix's friendship tested and kept.
Hook: the counteroffensive across the Middle Sea is ordered; Rowan notices Clara forgot his nickname.

## Arc 6 Across the Middle Sea (251-300) monster campaign + ally politics.
Q: can the south be taken back? Enemy: Hierarch the Ashen Choir; Shah Kaveh's bargain.
Beats: crossing to Okarune; Han Shuzhen's Qianlu corps joins; Mansakar's Prince Dawit; Ezharan cuts the Sandneck supply until Verandel promises well rights; Eden breaks his own rule and kills an unconfirmed suspect to save a convoy (260) and Ea refuses him (until 330); Clara is a Veil (262), imprisoned alive so she can be saved one day; Ashen Crowns under Ilsabet Rorke join the war (270); Armory of a Thousand Names (280, his answer to losing Ea); front stalled by design (285); Void Host revealed when Eden wakes three legions (290); Lysandre (300).
Tactics: desert lancers vs Husk tides; burning grass for noon shadows; relic loans to an army; night siege of a grey city.
Win (295-300): retaking the Crown Well of the Ashen Highlands. Eden is strong enough but overdrawn after the Armory; the Ashen Crowns he spared hold his flank (mercy to Ilsabet, ch 25) and Han Shuzhen raises the causeway Eden asked for in arc 3. Cost: the world now knows Verandel's "hidden order" is one man.
Growth: Rowan grieves Clara without hating; Lysette's Witness becomes a war tool.
Resolution: the Ashen Highlands Crown Well retaken, Clara's Veil caught alive (so she can be saved), the Ashen Crowns redeemed in battle, the Void Host revealed as Eden's.
Hook: the whole world knows; Volkhovar demands a Concord tribunal for "the Void's Son", and Ea still refuses him.

## Arc 7 The Void's Son (301-350) legal, religious, economic.
Q: is Eden a saviour or the cause? Enemy: Concord tribunal (Halberg as witness), Aurelis Inquisition, Volkhovar embargo and propaganda.
Beats: Eden summoned to trial at Solmarch; Volkhovar embargo starves the Wall; Inquisitor Theodora investigates fairly; Seraphine defends him as envoy; Valerius stands against his own father's kill-order (330); Ea returns when Eden confesses the rule he broke in open court (330); Kaedros (350); the Emperor gave himself, Eden is not the Emperor (340); king refuses to hand Eden over, risking war (345).
Tactics: evidence duels in court; embargo convoy war; a cathedral assassination.
Win (346-350): the tribunal leans toward "surrender him". Eden refuses to use force on a court; he had asked Theodora to investigate (310); her findings and Lysette's Witness of the Iron King's ritual chamber carry the vote. Cost: Verandel is isolated; Eden's name is feared.
Growth: Eden says "I'm not fine" to Felix's daily question for the first time.
Resolution: the tribunal won by evidence, Ea returns, the king stands by Eden, Melchsee's gift and name revealed.
Hook: Hush raft-fleets launch from Ruhnmara; in Eden's shadow a voice he has never heard calls him a usurper (Nihl).
