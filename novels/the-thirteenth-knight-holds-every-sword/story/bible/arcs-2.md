# Arc roadmap, part 2 (arcs 8-12 and 16-17; arcs 13-15 in arcs-4.md). Romance: romance.md. Light chapters: comedy.md. Same victory rule as part 1: Eden wins; the tension is the path and the price. ALL DATES: bible/calendar.md (wins).

## Arc 8 The Grey Sea (351-400) naval monster war + Volkhovar sabotage.
Q: can the sea hold when the land cannot? Enemy: Hush raft-fleets from Ruhnmara; Volkhovar and the Isthmus Company trying to buy or seize Admiral Kirana (Thalos's host); Nihl.
Beats: Kirana and Akizane's duelist Tsukishiro Kaede; a Frost Hand plot to kill Kirana near a groomed successor (succession hunt, foiled 370); Ombrevar (380); Felix lost overboard (382), feared dead; Nihl declares Eden a usurper (390); Felix found alive on a Jayavara reef (397, Thessa's return law and Kirana's tide); Throne Door (400).
Tactics: doors on wave troughs; ships as moving shadows; boarding grey rafts; typhoon battle with Kirana steering it.
Win (395-400): Nihl takes half the First Legion. Eden could crush him; instead he keeps the Emperor's law (never kill humans) that Nihl breaks, and the soldiers return to the one who keeps it. Cost: his hold on the Door is shaken; Felix's bow arm is ruined.
Growth: Felix becomes scout-commander; Seraphine refuses to grieve him (382) and runs to him (397).
Resolution: the Grey Sea holds, Ossuary Reef sinks, Felix is found alive, Nihl's mutiny broken by the Emperor's law, Throne Door earned.
Hook: the Tutor says the Vault has called: to go on, Eden must enter Heaven Gate, and the price is not named.

## Arc 9 Heaven Gate (401-450) divine.
Q: what does Eden owe the dead who made him? Enemy: Valkyrie Marshal Aethra and Sepiroth's court, who call Eden an abomination.
Beats: the Tutor's wish (400); entry costs Eden his memories of Lysette (420); Oriane (410); Vault floors 4-6; the Dragon is Sepiroth (425); the Ten Sovereigns and the Binding of Thrones; Han and Kirana host gods (430); Kaurveth is the tenth (440); Lysette brings him back by speaking both their middle names (445); her confession, afraid it would end like before (450).
Tactics: duels on floating weapon-racks; trial by ten relics; a fight in which Eden cannot remember why he protects her.
Win (441-445): Aethra's court rules to unmake him. Eden out-argues and out-fights the trial by ten relics, but cannot remember Lysette; she, outside, speaks the names, and his Keeping blood answers. Cost: some small memories never return.
Growth: Eden feels jealousy, fear and joy at once; Lysette chooses him knowing he might forget her again.
Resolution: Aethra's court accepts Eden, his memories of Lysette restored, her confession given, the gods' structure revealed.
Hook: Kaurveth is a god; Vehra gave the Rite; a living Crown Well is found under the Skyspine.

## Arc 10 The Skyspine (451-500) monster campaign + corporate greed.
Q: can a dead continent be given back? Enemy: Hierarch the Thousand Mouths; Norhaven's Isthmus Company.
Beats: Tawanqar's Wayra; Vehra gave the Rite (455); Rowan awakens Ashkar as Noonbringer (470); Clara pulled from the Hush (480, the rule from ch 105, her middle name given by Rowan) and awakens Myrrhen as Wellspring; the Isthmus Company tries to claim the liberated Crown Well; mana is Myrrhen's body (495); first green grass in the Pale (498); Eden's confession and first kiss (500).
Tactics: terraces; condor drops; relic chain to cleanse a Crown Well; thin-air Husk fights.
Win (496-500): Thousand Mouths swallows sound; Eden's drum-code (arc 1) and Rowan's sun break it on schedule. Cost: Rowan's light marks him for Aurelis and Vehra; Clara feels every wound she heals.
Growth: Rowan and Clara; Eden laughs aloud (499).
Resolution: the Skyspine liberated, Clara pulled back from the Hush, the first green grass, Eden's confession and first kiss.
Hook: Aurelis declares a crusade against "the Void's Son", and the god they want to free sleeps inside Rowan.

## Arc 11 The Ten Thrones (501-550) gods and hosts.
Q: what if the gods themselves want the world unmade? Enemy: Vehra through Dragomira; Aurelis crusade ("free Ashkar" = kill Rowan); the Volkhovar succession hunts.
Beats: Ishani hosts Niveth (505); Nihl's betrayal was a test (515), he joins; Ashkar, through Rowan, denounces the Luminarch's false oracle (520); Dragomira is Vehra's host (530); Dragomira refuses Vehra's last order and dies (540); Vehra jumps to Valerius (545); a council of eight hosts at Solmarch.
Tactics: a war inside dreams (Ishani's domain); a sunlit cathedral without shadows (Eden fights without doors); Han's mountain versus Vehra's threads.
Win (546-550): the crusade's sun leaves no shadows; Eden plants doors in the shadows of the crusaders' own banners (Valerius' joke, ch 330). Cost: the Tutor fades further; Valerius now carries Vehra.
Growth: Seraphine becomes Concord Speaker; Valerius starts his inner war.
Resolution: the crusade ends, Ashkar speaks for himself, Dragomira's last free act, Nihl's test passed and he joins.
Hook: Vehra now lives in Valerius; the coalition must march on the Pale with a friend who carries the enemy.

## Arc 12 The Pale (551-600) monster final campaign.
Q: can every nation march together once? Enemy: Kaurveth's seven Hierarchs; Vehra pulling Valerius.
Beats: coalition of thirteen nations and eight hosts; Legions 6-9 (560); Kell hosts Kaurveth's will (560); Vault floors 7-9; Kaelthos speaks through Han (575); Lysette awakens Aurelune as Seedtime (580); Eden duels a Vehra-strained Valerius and must hold back (585); the Vault's tenth floor opens to Lysette and Eden together (590); proposal (600).
Tactics: seven fronts, seven Hierarchs, seven tactics; polar night with no shadow edges (they build lines of light for doors).
Win (595-600): the march reaches the Wound because every ally's earlier choice is in place. Cost: Eden's Frost turns to fever; Lysette's Witness burns out for a year; Valerius almost lost to Vehra.
Resolution: the pole is reached, Lament falls, Valerius masters Vehra, Eden proposes and Lysette says yes.
Hook: Lament's fall cracks the Wound wider: titans break north toward three continents.

## Arc 16 Eden (751-800) divine finale.
Q: what should end, what should be kept, what should begin? Enemy: Vehra and Kaurveth's body, both reaching for the empty throne through Kell.
Beats: Gorrath wakes from the Void road (790); Myrrhen speaks through Clara (780); Kell chooses to give Kaurveth an ending, a want fulfilled (790); Valerius binds Vehra to time without power over mortals (793); the Tutor finishes (795); Ending, Keeping and Beginning (Eden and Lysette) close the Wound; Myrrhen accepts Eden (795); vow with middle names (798); epilogue (800).
Win: earned by everything planted (loans, names, doors, mercy, allies' choices). Cost: Eden gives up the Door and the Ten Pillars, who sleep with honour.
Resolution: the Wound closed, Kaurveth ended, Vehra bound, Melchsee finished, Myrrhen accepts Eden, the vow spoken.
Hook: a quiet one: "So. What now?" (the weddings).

## Arc 17 Names Spoken Openly (801-850) slice-of-life finale, light stakes.
Q: what do people do with a world they kept? Mostly comedy and warmth, one gentle thread: the duchies' parliament (Valerius gives the conquered kingdoms a voice) and the Pillars' farewell.
Beats: Hallowcrest reopens; Lysette teaches discipline, Eden lectures from the roof (805); the Duke's one-day retirement (812); a three-nation cooking contest (820); Pillars' farewell feast (828); weddings: Felix and Seraphine (835), Valerius and Ishani (840), Eden and Lysette (845, roof garden); epilogue at the greening Pale (850).
Battles: one flex scuffle with leftover Husks (817), played for awe and laughs.
Resolution: three weddings, the duchies' parliament, the Pillars' farewell, the Pale greening.
Hook: none; final image on the roof garden.
