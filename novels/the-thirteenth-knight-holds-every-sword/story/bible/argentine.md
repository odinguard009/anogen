# House Argentine (reference)

Ducal house, hereditary masters of the Argent Order (Royal Knight Order), closest to the crown for centuries. Seat: Silverfell, a grey-stone palace on a lake two days north of Solmarch; town house in Solmarch's Crown Quarter. Silver-grey hair and grey eyes run in the line.

## Comic surface, chilling depth
The household is loud, warm and ridiculous, and every adult in it could end a regiment. Comedy and menace live in the same scene: the staff bicker about napkin folds while quietly disposing of six assassins between soup and fish.

## Duke Aldous Argentine (50)
- Booming voice, beard he grooms with ceremony, wears ridiculous house slippers with the ducal crest. Writes heroic ballads about Eden ("The Lad Who Napped Through a Siege") and has them performed for the staff; Eden confiscates the sheet music; the Duke has copies.
- Disguises himself to "secretly" check on Eden at the academy with fake eyebrows and a fishmonger's apron. Fools no one but Eden pretends not to see.
- Competes with the butler over who brews Eden's tea, and loses every time.
- Serious mode: goes very quiet, sits down, and the room's temperature seems to drop. Tenet Sentence (when he names a target and a deadline, his blade will not miss before that deadline). Wears Vowsilver, forged by Liora (liora.md). Fought the Iron King's guard and the Emperor of Void himself (PR 93), and lived.
- Loves Eden and Liora's memory openly. Keeps her truth from Eden because Liora asked him to. Regrets it every day.

## Staff
- **Bertram Quell**, butler, 60s. Silent, immaculate, former Argent assassin-knight. Can open any lock with a teaspoon. Calls Eden "young master" only when they are alone.
- **Greta Vos**, head maid. Small, round, ladle in hand; former Argent shield-captain. Rules the kitchen and the Duke. Bakes Eden's favourite honey-rye bread every visit.
- **Pim and Odessa**, junior maids, twins, gossip specialists, run the household's intelligence on the town.
- **Old Hammond**, stablemaster, deaf by choice when the Duke yells.
All of them know Eden is the heir and are sworn to silence; none know the Black Order. Eden can only smile at them; they treasure the smile and pretend it is enough.

## Eden's hidden existence
- At Eden's own request (age 13), his existence is hidden until he chooses to declare himself heir. Public story: the Duke has a son raised quietly by tutors in the north, never seen; rumours say sickly, or dead, or invented.
- Day-to-day he is Eden Wren, a Thornmere-born commoner ward on a royal scholarship, using his mother's cover surname (see timeline.md: why Wren is safe).
- He declares himself at ch 160, by his own choice, to shield Rowan and Lysette in open court.

## The Duke's showpiece (ch 193-194)
During the graduation coup the Iron Faction's elite and three grafted Crowns storm the palace's west court to reach the king. The Duke arrives late, in slippers, eating. He puts the sandwich down, knocks once on Vowsilver's case, and the silver closes over him with the sound of a lake freezing. "Sentence. All of you. Before the ninth bell." Sixty knights and three Crowns fall in the time it takes the bell tower to finish ringing nine; nobody dies; every blade he meets is cut at the hilt. Then he picks the sandwich back up. Witnesses: Valerius (who stops arguing that the Duke is a buffoon), Lysette, Felix. Battle tag: flex, aura.

## Hidden habit: inside the armor
After every battle, alone, the Duke closes himself in Vowsilver, helm down, and sits. Liora folded Keeps for him into it: her humming, her laugh, once his name. He holds the inside of the left gauntlet, where her feather is stamped, and weeps where no one can see, then comes out loud and cheerful. He spends a Keep only on the worst nights; he is rationing her.
- Beat 1, ch 30 (Duke interlude): after the Silverfell bandit scuffle he "checks the fit" of the helm in the chapel and stays an hour. Only humming is heard.
- Beat 2, ch 164: Pim asks why the helm's padding is always damp. Bertram, polishing it: "Condensation." He says it gently.
- Beat 3, ch 200: after the coup Eden sees his father walk into the armory and not come out; he almost follows, doesn't.
- Release, ch 251: on the far side of the Middle Sea, Eden enters his father's tent and finds him in the armor, shoulders shaking. The Duke opens the helm and, without a word, puts it on his son's head. A Keep plays: "Aldous, you fool. Come home and eat something." Eden laughs, and then cannot stop crying, and does not know which one it is. The Duke holds his gauntlet over Eden's. Neither says anything for a long time. After that, Eden asks "Eaten today?" too.
- Payoff, ch 795-796: the last Keeps play through Aster and Vowsilver at once; father and son hear her together.
