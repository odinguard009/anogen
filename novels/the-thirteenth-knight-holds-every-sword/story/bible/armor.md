# Armor (canon; invent nothing)

**Armor-up set piece.** 150-300 words, slower than the fight around it: trigger, each plate with look, sound and temperature, helm last, first breath inside, first step. Then weight and changed movement, which part takes the hit, cost after (cold, Frost, the Tolls as heavy joints, never numbers). Armor-ups are aura moments: show how people nearby react. No Raiment in public unless canon says so.

**Black armor rule.** Eden's 13 Knights and the Tutor wear black armor like Eden. Whenever armor is worn, called, or hit, show it. Always write the armor label/name when worn.

**Per-class armor.** See tech.md for each job's armor set and weapon.

**Spirit-bonded armor.** A bonded knight's armor may carry the spirit's mark or shape (e.g., Eden's black mechanical horse wears matching black armor). Show the bond mark on armor when relevant.

**Raiment.** Special named armor sets (Umra, Aster, etc.) are rare; public use only when canon allows. Each has a unique look and cost (Frost, Tolls).

**Frost and Tolls.** After armor use: cold, Frost (mana drain), Tolls (heavy joints). Never numbers; describe as weight and cold.

**Scale.** Armor weights and sizes scale to Elysmar's 3x world; humans same size, gear heavier/longer.
