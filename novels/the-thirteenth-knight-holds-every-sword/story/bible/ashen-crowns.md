# The Ashen Crowns (antagonist group, reference)

## Who
A secret network of heirs, exiles and grieving families of the eleven kingdoms the Iron King conquered (PR 53-68): Thornmere, Valmoria, Ostrevaal, Kaldmark, Lirenne, Morvanne, Ysterholt, Caradonne, Sarnhold, Aubrevelle, Gallowmarch. Those kingdoms are now Verandel duchies; their crowns were melted for the Iron King's war chest. Emblem: a broken crown drawn in ash on black card.

## Goal (layered)
1. Public face: revenge on House Aurenmark; restore the eleven kingdoms. Their grievance is real and partly just.
2. Their plan: prove the crown summoned the Emperor of Void and that this widened the Wound, turning the people and the Concord against Aurenmark; seize the "Void Host" the world believes Verandel hides, and use it to break the kingdom.
3. The Regent's private aim: Count Lucan Merrow, born crown prince of Ostrevaal, wants to become a new Iron King himself, with Rowan (lost heir of Lirenne, whose Dawnbreak can be paraded as a sign) as puppet banner.
4. Their hidden sponsor: the Tailor (Hush spymaster, wearing Halvard Strent) feeds them Grey Grafts to destabilize Verandel and to force Eden's seal to crack so the Emperor's essence can be taken.

## Ranks
- The Regent: leader, unseen; speaks through a cracked porcelain mask. (Merrow.)
- The Eleven Crowns: elite, one per fallen kingdom, each with a title ("Crown of Kaldmark"). Each carries a Grey Graft and a true Tenet. Several are honourable people doing terrible things.
- Embers: cell leaders in cities and the army.
- Cinders: recruits, often poor duchy folk and refugees.

## Methods
- Grey Grafts: a sliver of Hush flesh sewn under the skin. Gives a second, borrowed Tenet-like power (the only known exception to one-soul-one-Tenet) at the cost of slow greying; the grafted eventually turn Hushborn. Members do not know where grafts come from until ch 95.
- Assassinations, kidnappings of conquered-kingdom descendants, sabotage of supply roads, bombings with unstable mana stones, staging fake "Thirteenth Vigil" murders to turn the public against the crown's secret order.
- Recruitment hook: a black card with a pressed ash-rose and the name of your family's lost kingdom, and one word: "Remember." Sent to anyone with a conquered-kingdom surname.

## How Eden is involved by accident
His mother's cover name Wren belongs to an extinct Thornmere house. In ch 7 a Cinder slips him a recruitment card: "Remember Thornmere." He ignores it; the Ashen Crowns mark him as a possible sympathizer, then as an obstacle when he blunders through their Lantern Ball attack (ch 24-26) while trying to reach the roof.

## Known Crowns (more can be added)
- Crown of Kaldmark: Ilsabet Rorke, ice-axe giant of a woman, honourable, first Crown Eden fights (ch 25); he spares her.
- Crown of Morvanne: "the Ferryman", a gentle-voiced poisoner.
- Crown of Thornmere: empty seat; they hope to fill it (with Eden, ironically).
- Crown of Lirenne: empty; reserved for Rowan.
- Crown of Gallowmarch: Desmond Harrow, grafted fanatic; dies at ch 50 delivering the Tailor's message.
- Allies of convenience: the Iron Faction and the Lanternhouse Compact (politics.md).
