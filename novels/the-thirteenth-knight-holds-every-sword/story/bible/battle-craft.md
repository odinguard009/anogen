# Battle craft: toolkit for the Lead Writer (reference)

## Principles
- Eden's base style, named forms and team combos: combat-style.md. Stage I fights are calm and one-sided; variety grows as retainers and relics unlock.
- Eden is overpowered; fights stay exciting because of HOW he wins. Every fight introduces at least one new application (list below; never repeat a trick as the centrepiece).
- Banned default: "summon a bigger weapon". Escalating raw power is the last answer, never the first.
- Use power by theme and constraint: doors need edges; relics have wills and odd properties; loans need middle names; Raiments toll.
- Prefer the odd property over damage: a candle that never goes out beats a sword that cuts harder.
- Terrain and environment first: light, shadow, water, crowds, height, weather.
- Turn the enemy's strength against them: their wall is his cover, their drain is his flood.
- Combos with allies: lend a relic for a pairing (Verdict plus a wall-armor, Witness plus honest light).
- Information warfare: false schedules, masks, Gorrath's voices, letting an enemy see what he wants them to see.
- Economy: smallest force that ends it; spare when he can (non-lethal is a skill, not weakness).
- Self-imposed handicap: hiding his identity, protecting someone, a rule he keeps, a tool he must not use. Say which in the scene.
- Protect while fighting: someone fragile in the frame raises tension without lowering his power.
- Aura beats: a sliver of the Emperor's authority, used sparingly; show reactions.
- Narrate as a tactician: options, costs, choice, result. Keep the plan hidden until it works.
