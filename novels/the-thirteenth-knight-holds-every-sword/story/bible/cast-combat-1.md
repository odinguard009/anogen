## Lysette Hollen: "the Ledger of Wrongs" (Witness)
Theme: evidence. Twin short batons of Golden Seal style (non-lethal, rule-bound) plus Witness, which replays the last minutes of a place as ghost-light. Stance upright, almost formal; rhythm of a cross-examination: probe, probe, pin. Tactics: she fights the past as much as the present: replays where an enemy stood a minute ago to predict where he steps next. Signatures: Recall Step (moves into a replayed opponent's blind spot), Citation (strikes the exact spot a replay showed was injured), Testimony (shows a crowd what really happened). Weakness: needs time and a still place; panics when she cannot see what happened. Growth: batons and Witness (1-200); Dawnglass loan, Witness plus honest light (210, 507); Wingwake flight (446); Seedtime, starting new outcomes where things ended (580+). With Eden: he reads the present, she reads the past; together they see a whole fight. Against Eden: arc 3 (140) she corners him with Witness and evidence, not force. Spotlight: 72 raid beside her mother; 139 proving a murder in court with a replay; 446 first flight; 597 Lament's song broken by one child's voice.

## Felix Ashgrove: "the Patient Path" (Trail)
Theme: tracking. Ranger longbow, hatchet, traps. Trail shows the path anything took. Low crouch, still for minutes, then one shot. Tactics: never where you think; prepares ground. Signatures: Back-Trail (follows a spell back to its caster), the Snare Line, the Wardenshell Rush (47). Weakness: loses his bow arm (382); hates crowds. Growth: archer (1-380); scout-commander who fights with hatchet, shield and a voice that carries (400+); leads scouts on three continents (601-650). With Eden: finds, Eden ends. Spotlight: 98 finds the last bomb cart; 397 survives three days on a reef; 626 holds a pass alone with his off hand.

## Valerius Aurenmark: "the Court in Session" (Verdict)
Theme: law. Longsword and Verdict: a spoken ruling becomes force ("This line is not crossed." becomes a wall). Square stance, textbook perfect; rhythm of a judge: deliberate, then final. Tactics: rules the battlefield into shapes. Signatures: Injunction (a wall), Sentence of Silence (mutes a Choir for one breath), Redwall Ruling (with the Redwall Raiment, 247). Weakness: rigid; every ruling costs breath and he can be baited into over-ruling. Growth: duellist (1-200); field commander at the Teeth (201-250); learns to rule less and trust more (330); fights Vehra inside himself (545-793). With Eden: rival in arc 1 (16-18), shield-brother from 194. Against Eden: 585 strained duel under Vehra, Eden holding back. Spotlight: 247 holds the breach; 636 holds the Teeth alone; 793 binds a god.

## Seraphine Aurenmark: "the Masquerade" (Mischief)
Theme: misdirection. Rapier, illusions and swapped positions. Light, dancing, laughing; rhythm of a joke: set-up, set-up, punchline. Tactics: makes enemies fight the wrong person. Signatures: Switcheroo (swaps herself and a target), Curtain Call (a false Seraphine takes the hit), Mirrorlace Bow (330). Weakness: shallow mana; illusions fail against honest light. Growth: duellist-prankster (1-150); envoy who fights only when she must (151-500); the Speaker's guard-breaker in council halls (500+). With Eden: the swap that gets him into the Concord hall (149). Spotlight: 106 escapes Belozerov's agents through a ballroom; 330 survives an assassination mid-speech; 658 smuggles Valerius out of a puppet city.

## Ishani: "Sleepwalker" (Niveth host)
Theme: dreams. No weapon; a silver stylus she draws with. Puts people to sleep within sight; reshapes dreams. Fights lazily, lying on a wall. Tactics: wins before the fight starts. Signatures: Lullaby Line (a row of soldiers asleep standing), the Paper Door (draws a door in a dream that opens for real), Duck Storm (comic). Weakness: cannot sleep herself; exhaustion, hallucination. Growth: prankster (115-500); revealed host (505); dream war general (505-510, 675-680); fights Vehra inside Valerius (550-585). With Eden: hosts his team inside dreams. Spotlight: 507 a whole battle in a dream; 678 frees an army from puppet threads in their sleep.

## Rowan Tallis: "Dawn Hand" (Dawnbreak, Ashkar)
Theme: sunrise. Spear and shield; light that burns Hushborn. Open, honest stance, charges first; rhythm of a sunrise: slow, then everywhere. Signatures: First Light (burst), Noonbringer (470: a sun-disc that cleanses a valley), Long Shadow (his light throws hard shadows Eden can use). Weakness: predictable; drains warmth and memory of night. Growth: brave kid (1-200); commander who doesn't shine (250); Ashkar's host (470+). With Eden: light makes doors (499). Spotlight: 47 holds students beside Clara; 271 Daybreak Hauberk at dusk; 520 Ashkar speaks through him.

## Clara Belmont: "Wellspring" (Myrrhen)
Theme: mending. Staff and healing; later mends land and wells. Calm, rooted; rhythm of breath. Signatures: Stitchless Heal, Green Line (living ground Husks cannot cross), Wellspring (498). Weakness: feels every wound she heals. Growth: healer (1-227); lost to the Hush (228-480); Wellspring host (480+). Spotlight: 47 wounded shielding Rowan; 498 first green grass; 780 Myrrhen speaks.
