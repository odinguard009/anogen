# Eden's combat style: "Lintel and Lamp" (reference)

Feel: calm, minimal, unhurried, precise; reading the opponent; one-sided exchanges; composure that frightens. Original details only.

## Weapons in hand
- Left: the dark lance, short (1.5 m), held at the hip, point forward. Closes into the door-shield (knocker ring outward) in one beat.
- Right: Inead, drawn only for the final stroke: gold light, held low and back, one cut, then gone.
- He never fights with both at full extension. One frames (the lintel), the other lights (the lamp).

## Stance and footwork
- Threshold Stance: side-on, weight on the back foot, lance angled like a half-open door. He looks bored.
- Half-pace steps only. He pivots on the lead foot like a door on a hinge, so attacks pass the space he was in.
- He lets the opponent come. He rarely takes the first step.

## Rhythm: Close, Open, Light
Shield (close), lance (open), sword (light). In practice: block to read, open their guard, end it with one cut. Most of his duels in Stage I last three or four exchanges.

## Signature opening
He lowers both weapons and waits. The first exchange he only blocks, watching shoulders and shadows. The second exchange ends the fight. Veterans recognize it and stop smiling.

## Tells
Slow blink before he finishes. One tap on the knocker ring when he decides. "Fine." before going serious. Polite and very quiet, glove off, when he is angry.

## The forms (named; debut chapters)
1. Latch: the shield catches a blade and locks it against the knocker ring (5).
2. Hinge: pivot past a charge; lance butt to a joint (16).
3. Draught: lance through the narrow gap in a guard, like wind through a door (22).
4. Lintel: shield overhead, Inead under it, one motion (25).
5. Lampwick: Inead follows the seam its light reveals (armor joints, grafts, illusions) (49).
6. Doorstop: lance planted; the line holds against a charge (66).
7. Threshold: a door closes on an attack and keeps it (180).
8. Open House: every retainer, every loan, one coordinated moment (596).
9. Last Light: with Liora's finished sword, the only form he never repeats (786).

## Style progression (each stage adds variety; battle-craft.md rules still apply)
- Stage I, Lintel and Lamp (1-100): solo, forms 1-6. Retainers only in secret roles: Vesk scouts, Gorrath decoys, Mirelle stands in. First loan to Felix (47).
- Stage II, Door and Retinue (101-250): Doorstep (70) and Hald (80) bring range; first open team combos; Threshold (180); Aster's stages; legion squads; Sefa (240) brings long distance.
- Stage III, Armory (251-350): relic swapping and lending; the Armory of a Thousand Names (280). Without Inead (260-330) he fights with the lance and borrowed relics, which forces new forms. Lysandre (300), Kaedros (350).
- Stage IV, Commander (351-450): Ombrevar (380) moves legions; Oriane (410) relays orders; Eden fights at the front and commands at once. Throne Door (400). Nihl mirrors his style as a foe.
- Stage V, Two Sovereigns (451-750; arcs 13-15 add retainer-led fronts and the Void road): Sepiroth fully his (Gatewing), combos with god-hosts; Nihl joins (515); all ten retainers.
- Stage VI, Garden (751-800): with Lysette's Seedtime; the last fight returns to the beginning: one lance, one light, nothing wasted.

## The Ten Retainers (Ten Pillars): how they fight
- X Vesk, the Lantern-Eyed (start): scout; wears shadows as disguises; knives; sarcastic gossip.
- IX Gorrath, the Bastion (start): giant with a tower shield; gentle; terrible liar; does the Thirteenth Vigil's voices.
- VIII Mirelle, the Quiet Blade (start): one long thin blade; almost never speaks; strikes in silence.
- VII Hald, the Last Archer (80): greatbow; arrows into one shadow, out of another; grumpy, counts every arrow.
- VI Sefa, the Cartographer of Doors (240): links shadows across distance with a folding chain-compass; chatty scholar.
- V Lysandre, the Mender (300): hooked staff; heals wounds and the Frost; soft-spoken; lectures Eden about sleep.
- IV Kaedros, the Siegebreaker (350): war hammer; loud battle-joy; adores walls because he can break them.
- III Ombrevar, Warden of the Deep Ranks (380): general; terse; moves a legion like a hand.
- II Oriane, the Herald (410): banner-spear; strategist; sardonic noblewoman; first to guess who Eden is.
- I Nihl, the First (foe 390, ally 515): fights in Eden's own style, mirrored and darker; strongest of the ten.
