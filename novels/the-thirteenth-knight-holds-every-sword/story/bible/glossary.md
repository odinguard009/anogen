# Glossary (plain words for low-level English readers)
Plain meaning first. Use the short word on the page; the bracketed name is only for the story.

## People and ranks
- Ladder = the school's list of the best students in the year (1 is best).
- Lance = a team of five students.
- Committee = the students who check rules, attendance and curfew.
- Vice-chair = the second in command of the Committee.
- Prefect = a student who helps the teachers.
- Cadet = a student on the Wall.
- Justiciar Seal = the school's police-like office.
- Concord = the meeting of nations.

## Places
- Wall = the long stone wall that holds the grey country back.
- Undercroft = the stone rooms under the school.
- Observatory = the old tower with the roof garden.
- Exchange = the telephone exchange (Bell Lane).
- Atrium = the glass hall where the Ball is held.
- Silverfell = the Duke's house in the north.
- Mawgate = the hole under the camp where grey things come from.
- Cistern = a deep stone tank of water under the Wall.

## Power words
- Aura = a knight's will made strong enough to stop a bullet; tiring.
- Mana = the power in a spell.
- Tenet = a person's signature power (Witness, Trail, Stitch, Verdict).
- Door = Eden's shadow door; needs a sharp edge of light and dark.
- Husk = a dead grey thing that keeps moving.
- Veil = a creature that wears a dead person's face and voice.
- Crown = a grafted Ember leader.
- Ember = a soldier of the Ashen Crowns.
- Cinder = a new recruit of the Ashen Crowns.
- Graft = a sliver of grey flesh sewn under the skin.
- Umra = the black armor Eden wears; silent and shadow-blended.
- Sevar = the Duke's silver armor.
- Ward = a spell that guards a place, made of words.
- Ward-plate = steel plate that stops a ball when aura is raised.
- Mana-lock = the spell in a gun that fires the ball.
- Melsche Door = the name people use for Eden's shadow power.

## Attacks and moves
- [Latch] = lance folds into a door-shield to catch and steer a blade.
- [Exa] = black lance that folds into a door-shield.
- [Inead] = gold sword that shows a Veil's grey body.
- [Dawnbreak] = Rowan's gold light that burns Husks.
- [Witness] = Lysette replays the last minutes of a place as ghost-light.
- [Trail] = Felix sees the path anything has taken.
- [Stitch] = Okoru binds wounds and limbs with threads.
- [Injunction] = Valerius's sword drags a line that becomes a wall.
- [Hinge] = Eden's pivot on the front foot like a door.
- [Lintel] = shield flat overhead, Inead drawn under it in one cut.
- [Lampwick] = Inead follows a seam its light shows.
- [Switcheroo] = Seraphine swaps her place with a target.
- [Grask] = a grafted arm swells grey and grabs.
- [Hrak] = a grafted arm swells grey and grabs (Ilsabet's).
- [Knock and Answer] = Gorrath's thirteen voices from thirteen shadows.
- [Tavu] = Okoru's threads bind a limb.
- [Osk] = Felix's silent arrow down a path he hears.
- [Citation] = Lysette strikes the exact spot a replay showed was hurt.
- [Ithe] = Rowan's gold burst from the spear tip.
- [Wyl] = Clara's green line Husks cannot cross.
- [Mott] = Vesk wearing another man's shadow.
- [Draught] = lance through a narrow gap in a guard.

## Tech
- Veinline = the line of mana under the ground that a train runs on.
- Vein-steel = the rails that carry the veinline.
- Bellwire = wire that carries news by ringing bells.
- Steam press = a machine that prints many papers fast.
- Motor-car = an early car.
- Telephone exchange = the place where calls are connected by hand.

## Other
- Thirteenth Vigil = the secret group that kills Veils; a half-believed legend.
- Ashen Crowns = a network of heirs of conquered kingdoms.
- Ball = the school dance (Lantern Ball).
- Rotation = the winter posting on the Southern Wall.
- Form 13-B = a form for reporting a Veil or a strange thing.
- Frost = the cold that comes after wearing Umra.
- Keeps = the memories stored in Liora's armor.
- Unbind = a person's power waking fully; never spoken of before its chapter.
