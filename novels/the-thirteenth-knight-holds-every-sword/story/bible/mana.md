# Mana and Tenets (reference)

**Mana.** The living current of Elysmar. It rises from the planet's deep core, runs through rock in veins (thick as rivers under mountains, thin as hair under farmland) and pools in wells. Also used as fuel (mana stone).

**Crown Wells.** The 21 great wells of the world. 8 lie under the Pale now. 13 remain, one per surviving nation. A nation without a Crown Well cannot train knights at scale; this is the root of all politics.

**Deep ocean breaks veins.** Shallow seas and land bridges carry them. Ships use vein-keels (keels of vein-stone) to draw mana for sails and wards.

**Daily use.** Lanterns, water pumps, wards on doors, healing salves, field rations that store warmth, engines, batteries. Guns and cannon exist (Meridian Age), but mana fields foul powder and aura turns aside bullets, so crossbows, wards and knights still rule the front.

**The core.** A knot of mana behind the sternum. Everyone has one; only some are deep enough to fight.

**Bells.** Capacity, measured once a year by the Resonance Bell (a bronze bell the height of three men in the Hallowcrest atrium). The candidate lays a palm on it and pushes mana. One clear tone per grade.
- 1 Bell: ordinary person. 2: soldier. 3: knight-capable. 4: officer, top 5% of students. 5: captain-grade, one or two per year. 6: order masters, legends. 7: once a generation.

**Recovery.** Sleep, food, standing near a well. Mana-wither from overdraw: hair greys, hands go cold, heart slows. Severe wither kills.

**Tenet (ability theme).** Every person develops exactly one Tenet between ages 12 and 14, at the Kindling (a fever night). A Tenet is a theme born from the person's deepest conviction or wound, not a power level. Two people with the same Bells can have Tenets of opposite natures.

Anatomy: Theme (the idea), Form (how it shows: weapon, sense, field, beast-shape), Law (the one rule it cannot break).

Tiers (depth): Spark (it reacts), Shape (it obeys), Edge (it creates new uses), Crown (it changes the area around you), Sovereign (it changes the rules of the world in a small radius; only a handful alive). The tier deepens only when conviction is tested and chosen again. Training refines control, not depth.

A Tenet can warp if conviction breaks. Warping is a known tragedy.

Registration: in Verandel, Tenets registered with the middle name at the order or academy, sealed. Only the order master may open the record.

**The Refusal.** Elysmar's mana slowly unmakes beings not born of it. Hushborn survive by turning land Pale. Valkyries survive a while by sealing their wings.

**Valkyrie arts.** Not Tenets. Wing-light and Keeping (lock a thing exactly as it is). Using them on Elysmar speeds the Refusal.

**Knights and magic.** Knights do not need physical weapons. Many fight with Tenet forms, spells (structured mana patterns learned at the academy: wards, bolts, sigils of force) or both. Orders train specific spell schools.

**Holy weapons (relics).** Before PR 0 every nation had relics, forged by saints, smiths and dragons over millennia. All vanished on the same night. Truth: the Vault, now inside Eden. Any relic seen in public would be a political earthquake.

**Job gauge (aura).** Each class/job has its own gauge mechanic on top of mana. All share mana; gauge builds/spends differ by job. See power-system.md.

**Spirit bond.** Some knights bond with a spirit/beast (great spirit of Elysmar). Not all can. Bond gives sense, aid, mana sharing, unique skill. Eden's bond: black mechanical horse in black armor.
