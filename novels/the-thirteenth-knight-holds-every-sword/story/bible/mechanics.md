# Mechanics cheat sheet (battle clarity; canon only, summarised from power-system.md and first-use.md)
Use this before writing any fight. One line per mechanic: what the reader SEES, what HAPPENS, what it COSTS. Never show where a Door leads: say the thing "is gone" or "is held in the dark". Do not add rules; if a rule is missing, ask or leave it out.

| Mechanic | The reader sees | What happens | Cost / limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Door (shadow door) | Two knocks on a hard shadow edge; a black upright rectangle, as big as the shadow, edge of pale light on one side | Things pushed in are held in the dark; Eden shuts it and they stay. It is also the exit of his shadow knights | Needs a sharp edge of light and dark. No edge, no Door. Deep use brings the Frost |
| Exa | Black lance, a little longer than his arm; one knock on the iron ring-knocker | Folds into a black door-shield, tray-sized, ring on the outside; one more knock opens it back | Only as strong as his grip |
| Latch | The shield snaps shut around a blade mid-swing | The ring holds the blade; he steers it where he wants | Only against a blade; the shock numbs his arm |
| Inead | A gold sword of plain light, one clear note when drawn | Veil faces turn to glass and the grey body shows; one cut ends a Veil | Light shows him; an ache behind the eyes |
| Lintel | Shield held flat overhead, Inead drawn from under it in one move | One cut from cover; must take a blow on the shield first | Needs a first blow and a roof of cover |
| Lampwick | Inead's light shows a thin seam | The blade follows the seam | Needs light and a seam |
| Osk (Felix) | One arrow, total quiet | A single silent shot | One arrow |
| Citation / Witness (Lysette) | Ghost-light replay of a past moment | Shows where a thing was hurt or who stood where | Needs a still place; headache |
| Ithe (Rowan) | Gold burst from the spear tip | Burns Husks back, throws hard shadows | Leaves him cold; makes him easy to see |
| Wyl (Clara) | A green line along a crack | Blows come back into her | She feels each blow |
| Grask / Hrak (grafted Embers) | A grey arm that swells | A crushing grab; each use spreads grey up the neck | Grey creeps toward the head |
| Veil | A person whose shadow does not breathe | Cannot say the victim's middle name; dies to grey dust | Only Inead's light shows it |

Rules of use: say the mechanic in one plain sentence at or before the moment it matters; at most ONE new mechanic per scene; name a Door by what it does ("a black door opened behind its arm"), not by the word, after the first mention.
