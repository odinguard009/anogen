# The Mother: Aiwe, the mind of the planet (SECRET; owner order 2026-11-25)
Name chosen: **Aiwe** (say "EYE-weh"). Other proposals: Duna, Tova. Short, old-sounding, not an English word, no look-alike in canon_names.txt. Scholars use "aiwe" as an old word for "the reading"; nobody knows it is a name.

## The secret
Elysmar is alive. Its mind is Aiwe, the mother of every living thing on it, including the Ten Sovereigns. The registry that reads a person's class and jobs at the Bell is her: she names her children.

## How it fits existing canon (no contradiction; a deeper layer)
- The Ten (gods.md) believe they made worlds "before worlds". True: Aiwe dreamed them first, her ten first names. They went out and made other worlds on her pattern, and forgot where they came from.
- Myrrhen, the Wellmother, is her eldest daughter. She came home and dissolved into her mother as mana. "Mana is Myrrhen's body" (T2-28, ch 495) stays true: Myrrhen is the current Aiwe's life runs through.
- The Refusal is Aiwe's immune reaction: it unmakes what she did not birth (Valkyries, Void-born, Hushborn). Myrrhen softens it for those she loves; that is why she can accept Eden at ch 795.
- The Hush is Kaurveth's body pushing up through her Wound at the south pole. The Pale is her dead flesh around the wound.
- The Emperor's "nothing" wraps Eden. Aiwe cannot read nothing; she can read its shape. The reading is live: at his Assessment he stood as Eden Wren with only a lance and a sword, so she read "Black Lancer, White Swordsman". When he acts with the Door she names what wraps him: Void Heir.
- Why "Void Heir": she knows the lid on her Wound (Melchsee) is ending inside Eden. Somebody must inherit Ending, or the Hush comes up. The name is two things at once, and the doubt is the point: a crown (heir to the lid) and a quarantine mark (a foreign body).
- What she wants: to heal. Not a lid for ever, but the Wound closed, so nothing has to be "kept out". That is the series ending (795): Lysette's Beginning and Eden's Ending and Keeping close the Wound; Eden gives up the Door; the Refusal lets him stay. His last reading is only his own name.
- The Pillars and legions have no reading: she does not read what she did not birth. Hushborn lose their reading as they go grey.
