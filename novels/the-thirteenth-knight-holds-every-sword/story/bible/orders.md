# The Knight Orders of Verandel (reference)

Twelve public orders, each about 2,000-6,000 knights plus squires, each with a color worn as a cloak lining and sash. Masters sit on the Order Council under the king. Knights fight with weapons, spells, Tenets or any mix. The orders, the academy and the Southern Wall are why Verandel ranks second in the world.

1. **Argent Order** (silver). The Royal Knight Order: guards the crown, the capital and the Concord. Hereditary master: House Argentine (now Duke Aldous Argentine). Style: disciplined formations, ward-lances. Secret: in PR 93 its master Aldous and the then-prince turned the order against the Iron King.
2. **Crimson Rampart** (red). Holds the Southern Wall on the Teeth. Master: Lord Marshal Gideon Valdis. Style: force walls, heavy infantry. Rivalry: despises the Golden Seal's "desk knights".
3. **Azure Tide** (blue). Navy and strait patrol, vein-keel warships. Master: Admiral Rosalind Mereaux. Style: water and wind spells.
4. **Verdant Thorn** (green). Rangers of forests and frontier marches; scouts the Pale's edge. Master: Warden Fennick Ashgrove (Felix's father). Style: traps, camouflage wards.
5. **Golden Seal** (gold). Internal law, investigation, treason trials. Master: Lady Justiciar Beatrix Hollen (Lysette's mother); her house still carries the shame of her son Corentin's execution as a traitor. Style: truth-wards, binding seals. Rivalry: obsessed with exposing the Black Order.
6. **Violet Lantern** (violet). Mage corps; guards and studies the Crown Well under Solmarch. Master: Archmage Cyprian Vey. Style: high spellcraft, siege magic.
7. **Ivory Order** (ivory). Healers, battle medics and wardens of the sick. Master: Mother Superior Agnete Rosk. The Duchess Liora volunteered in its infirmaries (where she saved the infant Lysette, PR 94). Style: healing, sealing, purification.
8. **Grey Gale** (storm-grey). Cavalry and griffon riders; rapid response. Master: Captain-General Matthias Kord. Style: speed charges, wind-lances.
9. **Amber Hearth** (amber). Logistics, refugee camps, well engineering, roads. Master: Steward Helene Varga. Style: utility magic, fortification wards. Quietly the most important order.
10. **Bronze Bell** (bronze). Academy wardens and instructors; recruit training; guards Hallowcrest. Master: Headmistress Dame Hildegard Ostwald. Tesfane Okoru teaches under it.
11. **Indigo Veil** (indigo). Foreign intelligence and diplomacy; spies abroad. Master: Count Lucan Merrow. Rivalry: jealous of the Black Order. Secret: Merrow is the Regent of the Ashen Crowns (reveal ch 150).
12. **Umber Anvil** (umber brown). Siege engineers, sappers, mana-arms forges, tunnels under the Wall. Master: Forgemaster Brann Okafor-Dunst (half Mansakar). Style: earth spells, engines.

13. **Black Order** (black). Officially founded PR 107 by royal writ; members unknown; records sealed by the king. Duty: hunt Veils who wear human faces, by any means, including nobles and officers. Executions are announced as "treason". Public belief: 13 masked agents ("the Black Thirteen"), assassins above the law; abroad, many assume it is the remnant of the Void Host. Feared by almost every other order; hated by the Golden Seal.
- Reality: Eden alone, plus his Pillars and shadow-soldiers posing as masked agents (Gorrath does the voices, Vesk does disguises). Black mask, plain black coat, no insignia.
- Known only to: Eden, King Theodric, Ysolde Lisle (handler), Duke Aldous.
- Methods: Ysolde sends sealed tasks; Eden confirms a Veil (shadow tell, then Ea light) before killing. Rule he set himself: no kill without confirmation. Planned: he breaks it once at ch 260 under pressure; it haunts him.
- Count so far: 31 Veils killed in five years. One was the Veil wearing Corentin Hollen (PR 110).
- Paperwork: Ysolde requires Form 13-B for every death. Running joke.

## Ward-plate by order (common armor; titled Raiments are all in the Vault)
Argent: silver plate, lake-blue enamel, lion-less plain breast (modesty is the house style). Crimson Rampart: heavy red-lacquered plate, tower pauldrons, tall shields. Azure Tide: light blue scale over leather, salt-proof wards. Verdant Thorn: green-brown leather and mail, leaf-cut cloaks. Golden Seal: gold-trimmed black half-plate, seal-shaped buckles. Violet Lantern: violet robes over mail, lantern-staves. Ivory: ivory surcoats over light mail, red stitch-crosses. Grey Gale: storm-grey riding plate, winged helms, griffon harness. Amber Hearth: amber-dyed padded coats, tool belts. Bronze Bell: bronze-rimmed breastplates with a small bell at the gorget. Indigo Veil: no armor in public; indigo-lined coats. Umber Anvil: soot-brown heavy plate, rivet patterns, sapper masks. Black Order: Umra (Door-drawn, never issued).
