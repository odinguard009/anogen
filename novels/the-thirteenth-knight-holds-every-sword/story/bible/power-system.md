# Power system: the authoritative primer (owner orders 2026-11-25)
This file wins over powers.md, mana.md, combat-style.md and battle-craft.md where they conflict. Tech: tech.md. Reveals: reveal-ladder.md. The planet-mind secret: mother.md.

## Ringkasan untuk Odin
1. Ada empat lapis, selalu urutannya sama: MANA -> AURA -> TENET -> CLASS/JOB.
2. MANA = bahan bakar. Disimpan di "inti" di belakang tulang dada. Bell hanya mengukur besar tangki, bukan kekuatan, bukan peringkat.
3. AURA = lapisan tekanan tak terlihat di sekitar tubuh ksatria terlatih. Menepis yang cepat dan ringan (peluru), tetapi tembus oleh yang lambat dan berat (baut busur silang, kapak, peluru meriam).
4. Aura juga menahan rasa panik dan dingin Hush, dan bisa dirasakan orang lain. Orang biasa hampir tidak punya, karena butuh latihan bertahun-tahun.
5. TENET = satu kekuatan unik per orang, lahir dari keyakinan terdalam (Witness Lysette, Verdict Valerius). Bukan level.
6. CLASS/JOB = peran yang "dibaca" planet saat orang mendaftar sebagai calon ksatria. Class = jenis petarung; Job = peran yang sedang dijalani. Job memberi nama pada skill yang dilatih. Bukan level.
7. Bacaan class/job hanya terlihat oleh pemiliknya (dan pembaca). Arsip kerajaan hanya mencatat job yang didaftarkan.
8. Eden terdaftar sebagai Black Lancer dan White Swordsman (tombak Ixa, pedang Ea). Saat menjadi Ksatria Hitam, bacaannya berubah menjadi Void Heir.
9. Mantra (ward, bolt, sigil) = pola mana yang dipelajari; masuk ke dalam skill sebuah job.
10. Relik dan Raiment = perlengkapan pinjaman dengan kemauan sendiri dan harga sendiri (Tolls). Kekuatan dewa (Door, Gate, Throne) berada di luar tumpukan.
11. Tidak ada tabel level. Pertarungan diputuskan oleh pasangan lawan, medan, persiapan, harga, dan kecerdikan.
12. Senjata api ada, tetapi medan mana merusak mesiu dan pemicu, dan aura menepis peluru. Jadi ksatria tetap penting (lihat tech.md).
13. Planet punya kesadaran: Aiwa, ibu semua makhluk, termasuk Sepuluh Dewa. Registri adalah dia. Ini rahasia besar; terungkap jauh nanti.
14. Penulis wajib memakai 10 aturan pertarungan dan daftar cek 6 butir di bawah.
15. Setiap kekuatan yang muncul pertama kali wajib dijelaskan (rupa, cara pakai, efek, batas).
16. Serangan khas tiap tokoh ditulis sebagai label tebal **[Nama]** saat serangan terjadi (bagian 13). Maksimal 1-2 per bab.
17. Kebangkitan puncak bernama UNBIND (bagian 14): langka, mahal, bukan peringkat. Dipicu keyakinan yang dipilih lagi dengan harga nyata, lalu menyebut nama tengahnya sendiri dengan keras. Contoh: Eden = Evrin (bab 590), Lysette = Auda (bab 141), Valerius = Esk (bab 194).

## The stack (one line each)
MANA (fuel) -> AURA (body field) -> TENET (one unique power) -> CLASS/JOB (registered role that names learned skills).
Outside the stack: gear (relics, Raiments, Ward-plate, guns) and god powers (Door, Gate, Valkyrie arts, Thrones).

## 1. MANA: the fuel
Analogy: mana is lamp oil; the core is the lamp's tank.
Mana rises from the planet's deep core through veins of rock and pools in wells. Every person holds some in a core, a knot behind the breastbone. Bells measure only how much the tank holds: the Resonance Bell rings one tone per step, 1 to 7. A big tank lasts longer; it does not hit harder. Mana is spent by every act of power: holding aura, casting a spell, using a Tenet, holding a relic. It comes back with sleep, food and time near a well (a night's sleep refills most of it). Overdraw is mana-wither: hands go cold, hair greys at the temples, the heart slows; bad wither kills.
- NOT: a rank, a strength score, or a measure of skill.

## 2. AURA: the body field
Analogy: aura is the heat-shimmer over a summer road, but it pushes back.
A trained core leaks mana outward as a thin field one hand deep around the body. The knight "raises" it with one deep breath and a tightening of the chest, and "drops" it by breathing out slowly. Raised, it does five plain things:
1. Turns aside small fast things: bullets, sling stones, thrown knives. They slide off like rain off oiled cloth.
2. Dulls blades: an untrained person's sword cuts the coat, then stops like it hit a wet sack. A trained knight pushes their own aura into their blade and cuts through.
3. Makes the body strong and quick: longer jumps, harder blows, falls that do not break bones.
4. Holds back panic, fear-magic and the Hush's cold.
5. Shows to the senses: others feel weight in the air, animals go quiet, hair lifts.
Cost: holding it is like carrying a full bucket; every hit it stops is a gulp of mana. Slow and heavy things get through: crossbow bolts, axes, fists, falling stone, cannonballs.
Civilians have almost none: an untrained core leaks and cannot hold its shape. Holding aura takes years of breath drills, which is why knights come from academies.
An AURA MOMENT in the story means a person lets their aura out fully, on purpose, so everyone feels it. Eden keeps his aura low in public, the same way he rings only three Bells. Under his own aura sits the Emperor's weight (the "nothing" that wraps him); in his aura moments people feel that, too, and do not know what it is.
- NOT: armor (it fails against heavy things), a weapon on its own, or a light show.

## 3. TENET: the one unique power
Analogy: a Tenet is a person's signature, written in power.
Everyone gets one Tenet at the Kindling (a fever night, age 12-14). It grows from their deepest conviction or wound. Anatomy: Theme (the idea), Form (how it shows), Law (one rule it never breaks). It deepens only when the owner is tested and chooses the conviction again.
Stages of use (descriptive, never a rank; a person can be strong at an early stage and win):
- It reacts (old word: Spark): it works by itself when the owner is moved.
- It obeys (Shape): the owner can call it on purpose.
- It invents (Edge): the owner finds new uses.
- It spreads (Crown): it changes the space around the owner.
- It rewrites (Sovereign): it bends a world rule in a small space. Almost nobody alive.
Cost: each Tenet has its own price, shown on the page (Witness: headache, then nosebleed).
- NOT: a level, a job, or something you can train faster by drilling. Drilling refines control only.

## 4. CLASS AND JOB: the registered role
Analogy: class is the kind of tool you are; job is the work you are doing with it today.
At the Bell Assessment the year a person turns 14 (and again each year), they lay a palm on the Resonance Bell. The Bell measures the tank. In the same touch, the planet's registry reads them, and a quiet line appears in their own head: their CLASS and one to three JOBS. Scholars call this the reading and believe it is a law of mana. The truth is in mother.md: the registry is a living mind, Aiwa.
- CLASS: what kind of fighter or person the planet reads you as. One class. It changes only at a life turn. Examples: Knight, Ranger, Mender, Duelist, Warden, Commander. Rare classes exist that registrars only hear of in stories.
- JOB: the role you act in now. One to three. A job names your learned skills (forms, spells, drills) and gives each skill one clear limit. A new skill comes from training plus a real test, never from a number going up.
- The live reading can change mid-scene when the role changes (Eden is the example).
- VISIBILITY: only the owner sees their own reading. Other characters see only what the owner declares. The Crown Register, the kingdom's record office, writes down the declared jobs, checked against the Bell's tones by a Golden Seal clerk. The Register is sealed like Tenet records: only the order master or the king's office may open it. Boards post ranks, never jobs. The reader sees a reading only through the POV owner.
- NOT: levels, stats, a hit-point bar, or a ranking. Two people with the same job can fight in opposite ways.

### On the page (format)
One italic line, alone, in the owner's head. No numbers, no lists, no brackets. Only when the reading appears for the first time in the story, or changes. At most once per chapter; most chapters have none.
Example: *The reading came, quiet as a thought: Void Heir.*
First use rule applies: the first time a class, job or skill appears, describe in 2-4 plain sentences what it looks like, how the body uses it, what it does, and its limit.

### Spells (inside jobs)
Spells are learned mana patterns: a hand shape or drawn sign, a word, and mana. They take one to three breaths to cast, so they are slow in close fighting.
- Ward: a fixed shield of mana. Can be cast fresh or built into an object (Ward-plate, door wards, ward-nets on city walls).
- Bolt: a thrown push of force, about thirty steps, knocks a man down.
- Sigil: a mark on a weapon or object that holds an effect for a while (a blade that cuts through aura).
- Small works: light, warmth, mending cloth, calming a horse.
A Choir's silence stops spoken words; hand-sign spells still work, slowly.

## 5. Gear and god powers (outside the stack)
- Relics: holy weapons with a will, from the Vault. Holding one drains mana each second. A borrower answers the third knock with their middle name.
- Raiments: holy armor. Seven Tolls; the seventh Keeps the wearer forever (armor.md).
- Ward-plate: order-issued plate with built-in wards. No Tolls, just weight.
- God powers: Melsche Door, Sepiroth Gate, Valkyrie arts, Throne powers. They sit where a Tenet would sit but are not Tenets. They have their own prices.

## 5b. One example: all layers in one moment
A Crimson Rampart knight holds a gate on the Wall. Six steps away, an Ember fires a pistol. She breathes in and raises her aura (MANA spent like a full bucket): the ball slides off her shoulder plate, and her hand stings. A second man lifts a crossbow, and she knows aura will not stop a bolt. Her job is Warden; its skill is a door-sized ward, and she casts it in two breaths (SPELL, a sprint of mana). The bolt cracks against it. Then her TENET, Shield, makes the ward hold its place while she steps out from behind it. Her reading never shows; the man only sees a tired woman who will not move.

## 6. What trained people can do (no levels; for imagination)
- Ordinary person: no aura. A bullet, bolt or sword kills them. Household spells at most (warm a cup, light a lamp-stone).
- Recruit (first years, soldiers): aura in bursts of a few heartbeats. Jumps a fence. A bullet still wounds; aura only stops a scratch. One or two simple spells, slow.
- Trained knight: holds aura through a fight of ten to fifteen hard minutes. Jumps onto a cart roof or a low wall, falls a full storey and rolls. Pistol balls from twenty steps slide off; a musket at close range lands like a club (bruise, cracked rib). A crossbow bolt still goes in. Three to five battle spells, one door-sized ward.
- Veteran or order master: holds aura for an hour of fighting. Jumps to a first-floor balcony. Walks through a scattered volley, tired after. Wards a gateway. Cannon still kills if it hits. Their aura alone makes a room go quiet.
- Rare legends: stories, not numbers. The Duke catches a thrown knife without looking.
Who wins is never settled by this list. A small tank used well beats a big tank used badly.

## 7. Rules of battle (10)
1. Say the distance in steps. Guns and bolts rule from fifty steps; spells from thirty; blades and Tenets mostly inside ten.
2. Aura stops fast and light, not slow and heavy. Knights fear crossbows, axes and falling stone more than pistols.
3. Every action spends mana: a spell is a sprint, holding aura is a full bucket, a Tenet costs its own price. Show tiredness rising.
4. Aura is the base; spells and Tenets go on top. Hitting a knight's body means first beating their aura: a heavy blow, an aura-pushed blade, a sigil, or a trick.
5. Spells need breaths; in a close fight, a fast blade beats a slow spell. Prepared wards beat both.
6. A Tenet bends the fight's rules once in a clever way, inside its law. Show the law.
7. Matchup beats size: Ea beats illusions and Veils; Rowan's light beats Husks; deep water beats the Hush; a Choir's silence beats spoken spells.
8. Fatigue is real: after about fifteen hard minutes a trained knight's aura thins; mistakes start. Cost lands after the fight (cold hands, Frost, nosebleed).
9. Guns and cannon do not end knights: mana fields foul powder and mana-locks near strong aura or wards; aura turns aside balls; wards catch shells; knights move faster than cannon aim (tech.md).
10. Terrain and preparation decide most fights: light and shadow, height, crowds, water, ropes laid days before.

## 9. The Hush and its creatures
- The Hush: a silent grey dimension eating land from the south pole. It drinks mana. Land it eats turns Pale (grey, dead, cold).
- Husks: grey beasts. Lead goes in and the grey flesh closes around it like wet clay, so guns only slow them. Aura-pushed blades, spells, Rowan's light, Ea and deep water stop them.
- Choirs: Hushborn that make silence: no voices, no spoken spells. Hand signs and aura still work.
- Veils: Hushborn wearing a human's face and life. Tells: their shadow does not breathe; they cannot say the victim's middle name; Ea's light shows the grey shape. A blade with aura through the core ends them; they fall to grey dust.
- Hushborn in general: their cold brings panic. Aura holds it off; civilians break first.
- Grey Grafts: Hush flesh grafted into a person; borrowed strength and cold, slow turning Hushborn.

## 12. Writer's checklist for every fight
1. Where is everyone, how many steps apart, what light and shadow, what terrain?
2. What is each fighter's aura doing, and what gets through it?
3. Which one ability (Tenet, spell, relic, Door trick) changes the fight, and what is its law or limit?
4. What does each big action cost, and do we see the cost rise?
5. Is anything used for the first time? Then describe it (look, body, effect, limit).
6. Why does the winner win: matchup, terrain, preparation or a clever trick? Never "more power".

## 13. Signature attacks and the label
- A signature attack is a person's rare, decisive move. One per person at first; more unlock later for a story reason (a test, a loan, a new stage).
- Ordinary technique (named forms such as Latch, Hinge, Draught, Doorstop, Doorstep) can carry the label on its first use and when it decides a fight.
- LABEL FORMAT: **[Name]** in bold square brackets, at the moment the move lands or is done, on its own short line or inside the action sentence. It is narration, not speech: never in dialogue quotes unless the person really says it.
- The first use gets the first-use description in the SAME scene (look, body, effect, limit). Later uses: the label plus one fresh detail.
- At most 1-2 labels per chapter. The label never replaces description.
- Italic = the class/job reading. Bold brackets = an attack. Never mix them.

## 14. UNBIND: the awakening (rare peak state; not a rank)
Name history: first proposed "Ondra" (with Kaith, Uvar); the owner rejected it as too occult and asked for a plain, strong keyword in the style of TCG keywords (Manifest, Advent). Chosen 2026-11-25: **Unbind** (the act: "Unbind"; the state: "Unbound"; label **[Unbind: Form name]**, for example **[Unbind: Evrin]**). Form names (Evrin, Auda, Esk...) are still the old proposals and can be changed on request.
What it is: for a few minutes, a person's Tenet may break the ONE limit it normally has, while its Law still holds. It is not more power. It is the same power without its usual limit, so a good matchup or terrain can still beat it.
- TRIGGER: the owner's conviction is tested at a real cost, and they choose it again; then they speak their own middle name aloud. In Verandel middle names are secret, so the price begins at once: everyone in earshot now knows it. No anger power-ups, no last-second luck.
- THE SIGN: every bell within earshot rings once by itself (the planet answers; mother.md). Dust and rain stop in the air around the person for one breath. Their aura becomes visible for the first time, as clear shimmer. Bystanders feel their own name in their mouth; animals lie down. The reading changes: one italic line with the Unbind's name.
- WHAT IT DOES: one clear idea per person (table below): one change in the body, the space or the weapon.
- LIMITS: one to five minutes. Afterwards the Tenet is "hollow" (does not work) for one to three days. Each Unbind takes one small thing forever, tied to the conviction (a memory, a taste, the ability to say a certain lie). It cannot heal death, cannot undo the past, and does not stop slow and heavy blows the owner does not see.
- WITH RAIMENTS: Tolls ring twice as fast. With signature attacks: a new attack may appear during an Unbind (labelled).
- COUNTERS: a Choir's silence (the name cannot be spoken), waiting it out, terrain that its idea does not fit, another Unbind, and above all breaking the conviction: if the owner doubts it mid-way, it collapses.
- LOAN vs UNBIND: answering a relic's knock with your middle name is spoken low, to the relic alone, and wakes nothing. An Unbind needs the name declared aloud as a vow to the world, after a real test. Only the second rings the bells.
- WHO CANNOT: Hushborn and Veils (the planet no longer reads them), Void-born beings (Pillars, Nihl), Valkyries. Grafted people can force a false one: no bells ring (hint ch 49).
- RARITY: a handful per nation, a few hundred in the world. Most knights never awaken: the test must be real, and few will say their middle name before enemies.
- SOCIAL: a public Unbind is news on the bellwire within the hour. The person's middle name is now public (relics can be lent to them; enemies can use the name). Nations count Unbind-holders like fortresses.
- On the page: bold label **[Unbind: Form name]** when it begins; first use gets the full first-use description and heavy earlier hints.
