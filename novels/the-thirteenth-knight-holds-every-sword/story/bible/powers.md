# Powers: Melsche Door and Sepiroth Gate (reference)

## Why two (one soul, one Tenet)
- Sepiroth Gate is Eden's own: a Valkyrie's birthright (authority over the Vault), inherited from Liora.
- Melsche Door is not his: it is the Emperor of Void's power, poured into the unborn Eden in PR 93 together with the Emperor's whole essence, to wrap the half-Valkyrie child in "nothing" so Elysmar's Refusal could not unmake him. Absence cannot be refused.
- Everyone, Eden included, believes he is a rare "twin-Tenet" of Argentine blood.

## Why the two coexist
Melchsee's essence is the outer wall around Eden's soul; Sepiroth lives behind the Gate, a door inside. Eden's soul is the hall between; they reach each other only through him (gods.md).

## Why it unlocks slowly
At age 10 Liora's Valkyrie art Keeping sealed his Gate, his Door and his memory of that night. The seal erodes only when his conviction is tested and he chooses again; also when warmth returns (each real feeling loosens it, which is why Lysette matters). Major crack ch 175.

## Melsche Door (theme: "every ending is a doorway")
- He cuts doors into shadows. What sleeps in the Void comes through, or the door's frame becomes a weapon.
- Rule of edges: a door needs a shadow with a clear edge (light AND dark). Total darkness: no doors. Flat noon: tiny doors. Door size is capped by shadow size.
- Dark lance (daily form): the door's frame drawn out as a short black lance (1.5 m, matte, faintly cold). "Close": it unfolds into a tall door-panel shield with an iron-ring knocker. "Open": lance again. At the academy he uses it only to block.
- Summons step out of shadows. Soldiers cost a little mana a minute; Pillars a lot. A full legion needs a day to wake and a huge shadow (city at dusk, storm, eclipse).
- The Emperor's law, kept by the Door: summoned knights never kill humans (they disarm, drive, wall off). Against Hushborn they kill freely. Eden learns why at ch 170.
- Cost: the Frost. Deep use cools his feelings for hours. Warmth slowly undoes it.
- The dream of endless war: the Emperor's memories of aeons of war across worlds, leaking as the seal thins. Suffocating. Quiet only near Lysette (reason ch 205).
- Later forms: Doorstep (shadow-to-shadow step in sight, ch 70); Threshold (close a door on an attack and swallow it, ch 180); Throne Door (the Emperor's true form, ch 400).

## Sepiroth Gate (theme: "nothing made is ever lost")
- Liora was Sepiroth's host and the Vault's gatekeeper before him; Eden had partial access by blood, full at her death (liora.md). Relics trust him because she mended them.
- The Sepiroth Vault at Heaven Gate holds every holy weapon and treasure ever made on Elysmar; all were pulled in on the Night the Relics Vanished (PR 0).
- To call a relic, Eden must have met it in the dream Vault and learned its name. Relics have wills. The Dragon opens the gate.
- Lending: to a person whose middle name he knows, if the relic consents. Ends when he lets go or the borrower betrays the relic's ideal (it returns and burns them). Each loan is a steady drain.
- Cost: core drain per second held; fever and nosebleeds when overdrawn.
- Candor, the Honest Blade (daily relic): hand-and-a-half sword, pale blade like dawn through ice, plain grey grip. It does not shine; it makes light honest. Illusions fail, Veils show grey form, mana constructs and Grey Grafts split at their seams. Cheap to hold (it chose him). Public assumes a Tenet-made weapon.
- Ladder: start, Candor + 6 minor relics. First loan ch 47 (Thessa, round shield, to Felix; law: it always returns to its bearer's arm, which saves Felix at sea, ch 397). Ch 120: twelve relics. Ch 176: seal breaks, 100 relics. Ch 260-330: Candor refuses him after he kills without confirmation. Ch 280: Armory of a Thousand Names (mass loans). Vault floors (ten, one per Sovereign's seal): 1-3 by ch 200, 4-6 ch 420-445, 7-9 by ch 560, 10 ch 590 (see gods.md).

## The Dragon Sepiroth
Pearl-white, scales like stacked shields, molten-silver eyes. Lord of Heaven Gate; Valkyries are its knights. Secretly Sepiroth, Sovereign of Keeping (gods.md; reveal ch 425). Ancient enemy of the Emperor (keeping against ending). Sent Liora to glean relics from dying lands; she stayed for love. At her death she bound the Vault's gate to Eden and the Dragon swore to guard him, which means living in one body with its archenemy's essence. Eden calls it "Landlord".

## The Tutor
The Emperor's last echo: a tall man with his face always turned away, at a board game on a white floor that ends in nothing. Trains Eden. Refuses his name. He wants Eden to live as Eden, not as him (reveal ch 400). He is not training a successor; he is finishing.

## Armor (details: armor.md; tricks: battle-craft.md)
- The Knock: one knock on the door-shield's iron ring switches lance and shield; three knocks and a name open the Gate for a Raiment (holy armor from the Vault). Borrowers answer the third knock with their own middle name.
- Umra (Black Order armor) is drawn from his own shadow: no Tolls, costs Frost.
- Raiments toll; the seventh Toll Keeps a wearer forever. Eden cannot be Kept (Void); at his seventh the Emperor's essence surfaces instead.
- Eden's own armor is Aster, Liora's masterwork, sealed black until ch 175; it evolves in stages and spends her Keeps (armor.md, liora.md).
- Fighting style (Lintel and Lamp), forms, the ten retainers' fighting roles and team combos: combat-style.md. Melsche Door is the human spelling of Melchsee's Door (gods.md).
