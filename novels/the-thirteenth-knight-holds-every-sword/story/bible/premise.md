# Premise

**Pitch.** Elysmar, a world three times Earth's size, is being eaten from the south pole by the Hush, a silent starving dimension; 30% is already grey. In the Kingdom of Verandel, Eden Wren, 18, a fifth-year at the Royal Academy of Hallowcrest, sleeps on a rooftop garden, skips class, and still never leaves the top ten. Nobody notices him. That is the point. He is Eden Aster Argentine, hidden heir of the kingdom's greatest knight house, the whole of the secret Thirteenth Vigil that hunts Veils (invaders wearing human faces), and the holder of two abilities no person should have: Melsche Door (doors cut into shadow, an Emperor's sleeping army behind them) and Sepiroth Gate (every holy weapon ever made).

**Opening.** He wakes from a dream of endless war to a girl crying beside him, unaware he is there. He offers a handkerchief. Lysette Hollen, proud, sharp-tongued, Discipline Committee, has just watched the boy she loves invite someone else to the Lantern Ball. From then on she drags Eden to class, and class drags him into fights.

**Tone.** Korean web-novel / light-novel energy, grounded. Academy life, spy intrigue, terror plots, court politics, frontline war. Close third person on a flat, dry hero (the camera stays outside his feelings); humor lives in dialogue. Slow-burn romance. A boisterous comic Argentine household with teeth. Comedy never undercuts grief or danger.

**Core conflict.** Outside: the Hush advances; thirteen nations bicker over the last mana wells; the Ashen Crowns, heirs of kingdoms the old Iron King conquered, wage terror for revenge; Veils rot courts from inside. Inside: Eden's powers and memories are sealed by his dead mother, and what is unsealing is an Emperor's gift that may cost him himself.

**Want.** Stay unseen, find the Veil that killed his mother, keep everyone at arm's length so no one dies for him again.

**Need.** Feel again. Be known by name. Stand in the light and choose to be Eden, not a weapon, not an Emperor's echo.

**Theme.** Names are trust. Old sins (conquest, summoning) demand honest answers, not hiding. Being seen is braver than being strong.
