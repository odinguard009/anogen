# Romance: three couples, three flavours (reference). One partner each; no harem; pure and tender.

Principles: attraction shows in small behaviour before anyone names it. Misunderstandings come from character (pride, guilt, duty, insecurity), never idiot-ball silence. One real obstacle per couple, resolved for good. Every arc carries beats for at least two couples; none goes ~25 chapters without a small one. Each couple has warmth and humour, not only angst.

| | Eden and Lysette | Valerius and Ishani | Felix and Seraphine |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flavour | aching slow-burn, tragedy-adjacent | banter rivals; duty versus heart | oblivious-earnest comedy; quiet devotion |
| Rhythm | long silences, rare peaks | bursts at meetings, letters between | many tiny steady scenes |
| Motif | the handkerchief; the roof garden; the Lantern Ball echo | the lamp left on; his numbered memos vs her drawings | "Eaten today?"; sweet buns; her joke count |
| Obstacle | secrets and guilt (Corentin, the Thirteenth Vigil, his forgetting) | political duty (arranged betrothal) and her hidden god | his sense of worth (rank, ruined arm) |
| Confession | almost wordless, on the roof, after years | formal written courtship with clauses, read aloud; she amends it in ink | plain words over food in a field kitchen |
| Comedy | her tsundere scolding vs his literal deadpan | memo war; ducks in his dreams | she flirts, he answers literally; everyone sees but him |

## Couple 2: Valerius and Ishani. Banter rivals, duty against heart
- 115 She "naps" on his council papers (she cannot sleep; it is an act) and rearranges his dream into a tidy garden. He files a complaint. She frames it.
- 119 Memo war: his numbered objections, her replies drawn in the margins.
- 124 He finds her awake on the walls at the fourth bell. From then on he works beside her, lamp on, "for efficiency".
- 150 She sails home. Letters: his have numbered points, hers are drawings of him frowning. 230 She sends a drawing of the Wall before he gets there.
- 310-345 Obstacle: Saravant betroths her to a Qianlu prince. Duty keeps her quiet, pride keeps him quiet. 345 "The only nights I rested were the ones you sat beside me." He uses Verdict for himself once: a treaty dissolves the betrothal.
- 505-515 He learns she hosts Niveth and is hurt she hid it; he admits his own fear; forgiven.
- 520 Confession: a written courtship proposal read aloud. "Clause four: you may rearrange my dreams, with notice." She amends it in ink: "without notice."
- 545-585 Vehra in him; she fights inside his dreams in the garden she built as a prank. 600 She falls asleep on his shoulder, her first real sleep in years; he doesn't move for six hours.
- 840 Wedding: he planned every detail; she changes one; he lets her.
- Smiles: 115, 119, 124, 520, 600. Ache: 150, 330, 545.

## Couple 3: Felix and Seraphine. Oblivious, earnest, devoted
- 10 He is the only one who never laughs at her jokes. She starts counting attempts.
- 60 She sees Eden watch Lysette; Felix hands her a sweet bun: "Eaten today?" (Greta packed two, reveal 812).
- 88 She eats lunch near his corner "for the light". He moves to give her more light.
- 145-147 Eden refuses her kindly. Felix sits beside her on the funicular, saying nothing. Joke number 212 finally makes him laugh; she cries harder, smiling.
- 236 His guilt about befriending Eden on orders. "Orders start things. Choices keep them."
- 330 He guards her while she argues the tribunal; she finds a bun in her robe pocket.
- 382-397 Lost at sea. She keeps his last note and refuses to grieve. When he is found she runs across a beach in court shoes.
- 400-460 Obstacle: his ruined bow arm; he steps back "for her sake". She pretends to agree and recruits Greta, Kirana and Lysette.
- 466 Confession over a field-kitchen pot: "I'd like to ask if you've eaten. Every day. For the rest of it." She answers with joke 1,000; he laughs.
- 560 Engagement. 835 Wedding: his first public speech, four words.
- Smiles: 147, 397, 466, 835. Ache: 145, 382, 430.
