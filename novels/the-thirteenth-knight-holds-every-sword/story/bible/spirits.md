# Spirits and bonds (new system)

**Great Spirit of Elysmar.** The planet itself has a great spirit. It wants to help end the invasion. It bonds with chosen knights (not all can bond). Bond requires conviction + mana depth (usually Bells 3+).

**Bond effects.** Enhanced sense, mounted/combat aid, mana sharing, unique skill tied to the spirit's nature.

**Not all knights bond.** Only main knights and some masters. Ordinary soldiers do not.

**Origin plot.** Reveal great spirit → planet → wants to help fight invasion. Ties to mother/Aiwa secret later (mother.md). Spirit bonds introduced early (Arc 1-2) so late use feels earned.

**Spirit armor.** A bonded knight's armor carries the spirit's mark. Show when relevant.
