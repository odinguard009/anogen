# State (new draft, 2026-11-26)

## Time and place
- Wed 4 Nov PR 112, midday to afternoon. Hallowcrest, autumn term, Year 5. Ch 1 was Mon 2 Nov, ch 2-3 Tue 3 Nov.

## Eden
- 18, public name Eden Wren, rank 8, roof sleeper. Has a headmistress permit for "medical reasons" and will not show it.
- Dream (night of 2-3 Nov): the Tutor's white board, the Dragon complaining about "good linen", a locked door at the board's edge with a silver hairpin in the keyhole (head like a seed). The edge went soft under his foot; the Tutor: "Not that one. Not yet. Again."
- Gave his mother's handkerchief to Lysette on the roof (ch 1). She still has it.
- In Spell Grammar he answered Brandmeier in six words: "The ward has no subject, Professor." The room now dislikes him.
- Valerius at the stairs: "So you have finally stopped hiding?" Eden: "I'm on a timetable." Valerius: "Pity."

## Lysette
- Rank 4, Discipline Committee vice-chair. Delivered a timetable before dawn and a spare copy. Walked Eden to the dining hall and to class; sat him in the front half. Underlined so hard her page tore.
- Wrote one line in her black book: Why does the Prince know him?

## Others
- Felix: usual table, "Eaten today?" / "Technically." Pushes bread and accepts tea with half a spoon of honey. Did not ask about the handkerchief.
- Rowan Tallis: came from morning drill with clay dust and a practice-blade mark; three rolls; called across by Lysette.
- Clara: posting ribbon on her collar, dust on her cheek; quiet, sits across from Felix.
- Brandmeier: Spell Grammar, siege wards; she let the faulty ward smoke to test Eden, then fixed it and told the class to write it down.
- Valerius: crown prince, school council; sent two officers away with a nod; knows Eden from before.

## Night of Tue 3 Nov (ch 3)
- First Vigil job of the draft: the Veil wearing the night supervisor of the Solmarch Central Telephone Exchange on Bell Lane. Nine lure calls in three nights; a baker and a clerk missing. Eden went in through the coal chute, wore **[Umra]** for the first time, cut the switchboard's shadow with a Door, used **[Exa]** as a door-shield and **[Inead]** for the last cut. The Veil could not say his middle name. It died to grey dust: "You smell like the bottom of the world."
- The tower clock rang three tones and a faint fourth. Vesk: "That was not the hour."
- The exchange's call list names Hallowcrest Year 5 families; the name Tallis is circled twice. Ysolde's note: Form 13-B. Before the first bell.
- Vesk (Eden's Pillar, small man in grey, lamp-yellow eyes) works the street; he cannot enter deep dark.

## Wed 4 Nov (ch 4)
- Morning: Eden filed Form 13-B at the grey office before the first bell; the clerk asked what a Form 13-B was.
- Lunch: Seraphine Aurenmark (rank 6, the Prince's twin) sat at the south tables uninvited, called Eden "the sleeper", treated him as an old friend ("You look better than the last time", "Years ago", "You're always at the back") and took his roll, his spoon, and an apple. Lysette bristled and wrote in her book. Felix noticed: "She looked at you as if she had known you for years." Lysette: "You answered her two beats late, every time, and you answered me at once."
- Arms class (low arms hall, east wall): Okoru paired Lysette with Seraphine (batons against rapier, even at two touches each) and Eden with Rowan (spear against practice shield). Eden blocked only and did not strike back.
- A skipped spear tip from Rowan opened a cut above Lysette's elbow. Eden's stomach went cold and he counted two seconds before his first step; he had a clean cloth in his hand without remembering taking it. Clara brought linen and said nothing. Rowan: "I'm sorry, I didn't see her."
- Okoru held the practice shield and said: "That hold is not by feel, and somebody taught you." He looked too long. Seraphine covered for Eden: "He is eighth, he blocks well, and it is not a crime."
- After class, Lysette, not looking at him: "You are the calmest person I know. I think that's wrong."

## Thu 5 Nov (ch 5)
- Undercroft maze drill. Okoru: one construct, one student, three minutes. Before it started Eden saw the limit wire cut clean from this side; the tag said THIRTY PER CENT. A false wall was set to close on the student in the circle.
- Lysette fought the rigged construct alone for three minutes. She broke both stone arms and split one baton but could not crack the doubled brass plate over the core. Rowan and Clara were trapped behind the false wall; Felix had the bow but no angle.
- Eden used the Door in the construct's wall shadow, folded **[Exa]** into the door-shield and used **[Latch]** to catch and steer the wooden stump. The Door shut on the arm and he drove the shield edge into the open shoulder joint. The construct fell. His arm went numb and there was a cold ache behind his eyes.
- "Two seconds," Lysette said. "Again." Then: "I didn't need that." / "I know." Her hands shook once and she hid them behind her back.
- Okoru: "That hold again. Not by feel." / "That was not a drill." Lysette asked who signed the requisition. Okoru: "You will not like the answer."
- The cracked core was packed with grey dust, pressed so tight it kept the shape of the plate. Plant for 22-23, 95.

## Fri 6 Nov (ch 6)
- Okoru formed the Year 5 Lances. Lance Nine: Lysette (4), Rowan (2), Clara (3), Felix (19), Eden (8); Okoru called it the strongest mix on paper, not the most comfortable. Lysette's sheet says "supervise", not "advise"; Rowan asked to learn Eden's shield hold and was told to use his spear.
- Chapel corridor: Rowan's gold palm light matches the hands in the Lirenne fresco exactly ("The same colour." / "The same hands."). The wilted lilies near Clara lift and open; she does not notice and says it has happened for years. Eden tells her he has seen "that field" near her home. Rowan is easy to like; Lysette watches both Rowan and Eden and writes three lines.
- Lysette checked Eden's medical exemption: "It says rest. It does not say where." / "The roof is quiet." She put the slip back without writing on it.
- Stores office: the new drill core order is signed "Master Sorn", a forge master who died last year, and dated in the first week of November. A girl with a red band (Lysette) had already asked for the same line the day before and wrote the date down. Eden copied the date onto the back of his form.

## Mon 9 Nov (ch 7)
- Eden slept on the roof; a bird nested in the broken telescope. Rowan asked loudly to be friends and wants someone to tell him when he is wrong before everyone else laughs. Eden: "It is what dogs do."
- Lysette's first attendance warrant: third bell strategy, reason "roof" written by hand in the margin because the form has no box for it. She has one week before warrants go to the headmistress. Rowan's testimony as a witness on the stairs was useless.
- Lunch: Seraphine's first joke on Felix ("You are supposed to laugh" / "I will think about it"); she knows the shape of the Ashen card but tells him people get them at fairs. Rowan got a bigger soup ladle after Seraphine said she had measured three.
- A thin Cinder in a rough grey coat, ash on one glove cuff, slipped a black card into Eden's strategy book at page twelve. Pressed ash-rose, broken crown in grey, four words: Remember Thornmere. Eden keeps it as a bookmark; his thumb went to the corner twice.
- A man in a grey coat with no badge, book like a prefect's count book, watched Eden pocket the card and wrote one short line. Do not name him on the page yet (debt: grey-coat Ember = Corwen, due ch 18).

## Mon 9 Nov, night (ch 8)
- Undercroft workshops. Vesk waited at the stair (cannot enter deep dark). Gorrath (giant in grey, tower shield, apologises to stone, terrible liar) held the passage; Mirelle (plain black mask, one long thin blade, one-word speech) came with Eden.
- Eden found an order for one core plate signed by Master Sorn and dated early November; grey dust sweeping on the shelf, same as in the construct's core.
- The grey-coat saboteur came back for the dust. Eden opened a Door in the workbench shadow, Mirelle used **[Sile]** to pin his sleeve through the door without cutting skin, and they took a folded paper from his coat before letting him go (no kill without proof). Gorrath: "I am a terrible liar."
- The folded paper lists the forge master, two store clerks, a groundskeeper and a girl from the exchange; one older line is crossed out.
- Vice-headmaster Halvard Strent grades by moonlight because lamps give him headaches; he apologises to papers and people. He hummed four notes of Eden's mother's lullaby. Eden stood with one foot on the stair for four notes, twice, and did not take the next step until the humming stopped.
- Strent: "Wren. A Thornmere name. Your mother must have sung that to you too." Eden never told anyone she sang.

## Thu 12 Nov (ch 9)
- Monthly debrief at the palace. Ysolde (thin, grey dress, narrow face, folds paper in three, deadpan) corrected Form 13-B: "a construct with a cut wire", not "not technically a Veil"; "trace: dust, grey, in core".
- King Theodric (broad face, tired eyes, old worn sword with a gold line, lifts a hand and does not finish the gesture) asked if Eden was eating and gave a stores form for breakfast in class when there is early drill. He said "Your mother had a hand like yours. Steady." Eden did not ask about the sword or the mother.
- Exchange call list is the Ball guest list; Rowan Tallis circled twice in two different pencils. Ysolde: watch him without watching him; report anyone asking about his birth or offering him a cell.
- Ysolde slid a grey sealed envelope into a drawer: "Orders you haven't earned." Do not tell the king. Plant for ch 155.
- New target: Ember cell in the lower city dye-works, moving dust through the water lanes; children (Cinders) in the cell, do not touch them; no kill without proof. Come back by the bridge, not the ferry. "Watch the boy with the light as well as Tallis."
- Nine days to the Ball.

## Fri 13 Nov (ch 10) - Lysette POV
- Committee rounds: one hour and ten minutes; three crooked collars, a stolen spoon returned with "please", a late slip signed on the back. Her mother's letter came with the morning bread: "House Hollen has had enough of public matters. We will act as if it were not." C1 H rung; no name, no locket.
- Eden's file: sixty-one absences, ordinary marks, a permit signed by Ostwald. The absences cluster on afternoons with a free hour. Lysette: "Nobody is that clean. Someone has been filling these in for him." Felix: "He has practised." Lysette said "I like him too" before she chose to.
- Seraphine sat with Eden, started a joke count on Felix (joke one: reading at lunch), and asked Eden, "You'd save me, right?" He answered "Probably." S1 D rung; no explanation of how they know each other.
- Lysette realised she had not thought of Rowan for a full hour. She went to file the forms and found the handkerchief still in her jacket pocket, folded as his hands had left it, the feather caught on a thread.

## Sat 14 to Mon 16 Nov (ch 11)
- First graded field run in the Undercroft, Year 5 spec, with shoulder-height lamps, false walls and two timed constructs. Okoru marks time, touches and whether the Lance listens.
- Lance Nine came third in eleven minutes behind the top Lance and the fourth. Okoru's order: Felix, Lysette, Wren, Clara, Rowan.
- First uses: Felix's Trail (pale line of boot tracks; faded where someone sweeps the floor nightly) and Lysette's Witness (ghost-light replay of a hand pushing a wall; needs stillness). Rowan's spear and Clara's healing closed the construct's brass joint; Eden only blocked and read the lamp shadows to find a gap above the wall. He had seen the exit when they came in and let Lysette announce it.
- A man in a staff coat with a narrow face and a pen watched Rowan from the unlit gallery, wrote on a paper on his wrist, and was gone when Eden looked again. Do not name him yet.

## Tue 17 Nov (ch 12)
- Lunch behind the stables with Felix. He bought two sugared buns; "Eaten today?" / "Technically" ran once. He asked why nobody gets close to Eden; Eden: "I do not know how." Felix said his mother told him in Year 1 to sit with the quiet boy, and staying was his own choice.
- Felix gave his middle name, Perrin, first, so it would not be a trade. Eden could give only "Eden Wren" and said honestly that he had no name he could say out loud. Felix did not ask again. His kindness is in staying until the silence is ordinary.
- Lysette brought the second lunch with "Committee efficiency. You skip meals and you skip class. I am not losing track of you," still not looking at Eden. She had asked the gate prefect about the bun and wrote "bun, partial". Felix told her his middle name before leaving.
- The two-lunch routine starts here. The handkerchief is still unwashed; Lysette's glove has a green stain from the roof garden.
