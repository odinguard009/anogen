# State after chapter 8 (autumn, PR 112; Year 5; ch 3 = night 1, ch 4-7 = four days, ch 8 = that night)

## Eden (narrator)
- 18, public Eden Wren, roof sleeper (exemption: "the roof is a medical matter"; floor, stairs, classroom, hall are not). 61 absences. Nightly dream: grey ash, black banners; Tutor's siege game, the Landlord's voice, crooked door with silver hairpin in the keyhole ("Not yet").
- Black Order for Ysolde (tall, thin, grey coat). Ch 3: ended Veil-clerk Alaric Aubin Dunmore; its book lists Year 5 names, Rowan circled hard (Ysolde has it). Thinks someone told the Veil where he stood.
- Powers shown: Door (needs a shade edge), Umra (market shouted "Black Order"), Gate + Ea (pale sword), Ixa (public lance in a case; knock folds it into a door-shield with iron ring), Latch. Bell: 3 tones; only he hears a 4th.
- Looked away at Corentin's portrait; stomach turned; does not know why.
- Ch 8 (night, Undercroft west workshop): Vesk, Gorrath (9 ft, tower shield) and Mirelle (tall, grey mask, long thin blade, strikes only through a door Eden opens, [Sile]) came out. A man in a long coat packed a second core with grey dust, fled up a ladder (hat, no face seen). Eden took Vane's stamp (ink still damp). Upstairs Strent sat grading by moonlight (no lamps, "headaches"), hummed Eden's mother's lullaby, then said: "Wren. A Thornmere name. Your mother must have sung that to you, too."
- Ch 7: Lysette's first warrant (pale-pink seal, dry ink pad) is pinned to his coat. Deal: attend all morning classes and he gets 25 minutes of roof at lunch (no apple: "a bribe"). Banged his head on the desk in Spell Grammar. Got a black card with a pressed ash-rose from a boy in a grey scarf: "Remember Thornmere. Remember." Keeps it as a bookmark in the book in his coat. A thirtyish man (grey coat, broad flat face, tea-brown thumb) watched and wrote in a notebook.

## Lysette Hollen
- Vice-chair; colour timetable; keeps his handkerchief; locket = Corentin (dead two years; cause unsaid). Twin batons (one split ch 5). Hands shook ch 5 ("I didn't need that." "I know." "Thank you."). Watches Rowan; sat hurt between Rowan and Clara. Showed Eden the requisition file: warden core signed this autumn by Master Josselin Vane, dead last spring; Eden must share what he finds.

## Others

- Rowan (gold hair, loud, kind; palms glow gold; four loaves) declared himself Eden's friend to the courtyard. Clara (healer; lilies revive near her, unnoticed).
- Okoru (one arm): suspicious of Eden's feet; keeps the grey-dust core secret.
- Felix: quiet; rates Eden's desk bang 8/10. Brandmeier: "Trying is the beginning of failing."
- Valerius: "So you have finally stopped hiding."
