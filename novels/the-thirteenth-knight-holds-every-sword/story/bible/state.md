# State (new draft, first chapter written 2026-11-26)

## Time and place
- Mon 2 Nov PR 112, afternoon. Hallowcrest, the Old Observatory roof garden. Autumn term, Year 5. First week of November.

## Eden
- 18, public name Eden Wren, rank 8, sleeps on the roof. Skipped Spell Grammar again (sixty-one absences in the Committee book). Has a headmistress permit for "medical reasons" and will not show it.
- Gave his mother's handkerchief (old linen, small silver feather sewn crooked in one corner) to Lysette Hollen.
- The war dream (ash field, black banners, no air, the field waiting for one word) stopped when Lysette cried.

## Lysette
- Lysette Maren Hollen, rank 4, Discipline Committee vice-chair. Long platinum-white hair, one thin braid across the top, gold hoop in the left ear, red band on the left sleeve.
- Cried on the roof because Rowan Tallis asked Clara Belmont to the Lantern Ball at lunch in front of the whole year. Took the handkerchief: "I'll wash it."
- Knows Eden's name and his absences. Order: "Tomorrow you attend class. I will see to it personally."

## Others
- Rowan Tallis: made Clara a paper lantern by hand; it was crooked and caught fire on one side; Clara put it out with her tea.
- Professor Brandmeier: Spell Grammar; checks names at the first bell and forty minutes before the end.

## Plants (do not explain yet)
- Roof garden green out of season; one white flower on the highest twig of the apple tree. Nobody waters grass in November.
- Lysette tends the herb beds in secret (dirt under her nails, two broken).
