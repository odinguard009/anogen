# State after chapter 9 (autumn, PR 112; Year 5; ch 3 = night 1, ch 4-7 = four days, ch 8 = night, ch 9 = first evening of next month)

## Eden (narrator)
- 18, public Eden Wren, roof sleeper (exemption: "the roof is a medical matter"; floor, stairs, classroom, hall are not). 61 absences. Nightly war dream; Tutor's siege game; crooked door with silver hairpin ("Not yet").
- Black Order for Ysolde (tall, thin, grey coat). Ch 3: ended Veil-clerk Alaric Aubin Dunmore; its book lists Year 5, Rowan circled (Ysolde has it). Thinks someone told the Veil where he stood.
- Powers shown: Door (needs a shade edge), Umra, Gate + Ea, Ixa (lance; folds to door-shield), Latch, Sile (Mirelle). Bell: 3 tones; only he hears a 4th.
- Looked away at Corentin's portrait; stomach turned; does not know why.
- Ch 8 (night, Undercroft west workshop): Vesk, Gorrath (9 ft, tower shield) and Mirelle (tall, grey mask, long thin blade, strikes only through a door Eden opens, [Sile]) came out. A man in a long coat packed a second core with grey dust, fled up a ladder. Eden took Vane's stamp (ink still damp). Upstairs Strent sat grading by moonlight (no lamps, "headaches"), hummed Eden's mother's lullaby, then said: "Wren. A Thornmere name. Your mother must have sung that to you, too."
- Ch 7: Lysette's pink-sealed warrant is pinned to his coat; deal: all morning classes for 25 min of roof at lunch. Got a black card with an ash-rose, "Remember Thornmere. Remember." (bookmark). A grey-coated man with a tea-brown thumb watched and wrote in a notebook.
- Ch 9 (palace): Eden hid Strent's lullaby ("nothing to report"). Saved locksmith Mistress Ulrike's shop on Wick Street (Gorrath held the door, Eden dropped a flat-shadow Veil clerk through a door into a cellar; hired men fled). Ulrike gave him a plain old key that fits nothing. Ysolde keeps a grey-sealed envelope ("Orders you have not earned"); King Theodric (49, grey beard) asked "Are you eating?". New orders: Ember cell in old river tanneries; "Watch the Tallis boy."

## Lysette Hollen
- Vice-chair; colour timetable; keeps his handkerchief; locket = Corentin (dead two years; cause unsaid). Twin batons (one split ch 5). Hands shook ch 5 ("I didn't need that." "I know." "Thank you."). Watches Rowan; sat hurt between Rowan and Clara. Showed Eden the requisition file: warden core signed this autumn by Master Josselin Vane, dead last spring; Eden must share what he finds.
