# Chapter summaries (new draft; one entry per chapter as it is written)

## Ch 1 - Forty Minutes of Grass
Eden leaves Spell Grammar after the first name check and sleeps in the roof garden of the Old Observatory, where the grass is green out of season. His war dream of the ash field and the black banners stops when a girl cries one big step away: Lysette Hollen, who has just watched Rowan Tallis invite Clara Belmont to the Lantern Ball. Eden offers his mother's handkerchief with the silver feather. She takes it, orders him to class, comes back up four floors for his name, and finds sixty-one absences in her book. Last hook: "Tomorrow you attend class. I will see to it personally."

## Ch 2 - The Timetable
Eden's dream runs on the Tutor's white board, with the Dragon complaining and a silver hairpin stuck in a locked door at the edge; the floor goes soft and the Tutor says "Not that one. Not yet." At dawn Lysette brings a timetable and a spare, walks him to the dining hall and to class. Felix asks "Eaten today?" and gets "Technically." Brandmeier lets a faulty siege ward smoke and calls on Eden; he answers in six words, "The ward has no subject, Professor," and the room turns against him. At the stairs Valerius says "So you have finally stopped hiding?", gets "I'm on a timetable," and answers "Pity." Lysette writes in her book: Why does the Prince know him?

## Ch 3 - The Night Supervisor
At night Eden and Vesk watch the Solmarch Central Telephone Exchange on Bell Lane, where a Veil wearing the night supervisor has lured nine callers with the voices of their dead. Eden enters by the coal chute, puts on **[Umra]** in the cellar, and cuts the switchboard's shadow with a Door so the cables go dark. The Veil fights him with a copper bar; he folds **[Exa]** into a door-shield, refuses its trick when it cannot say his middle name, and ends it with one cut of **[Inead]**. It falls to grey dust: "You smell like the bottom of the world." The clock rings three tones and a faint fourth. The call list names Hallowcrest Year 5 families and circles Tallis; Ysolde's note demands Form 13-B before the first bell.

## Ch 4 - The Sleeper Comes to Lunch
Eden files Form 13-B before dawn. At lunch Seraphine sits at the south tables, calls him "the sleeper", and teases him like an old friend while Lysette bristles; Felix notices she looks at him as if she has known him for years. In Okoru's arms class Lysette fights Seraphine to an even two touches with batons against rapier, and Eden blocks Rowan's spear without striking back. Rowan's skipped spear tip cuts Lysette's arm; Eden's stomach goes cold and he is moving two seconds later, already holding a clean cloth. Okoru studies the way he held the shield: "That hold is not by feel, and somebody taught you." After class Lysette, not looking at him: "You are the calmest person I know. I think that's wrong."

## Ch 5 - The Thing in the Circle
In the Undercroft maze Okoru sets one student against a training construct for three minutes. Eden sees that the limit wire was cut from this side, but the drill starts with Lysette alone in the circle. The construct moves far faster than its THIRTY PER CENT tag, and a false wall slides shut on her. She breaks both of its arms and holds it the full three minutes, splitting a baton and failing to crack the doubled brass plate over its core. Rowan and Clara cannot get through the wall; Felix has no shot. Eden cuts a Door in the construct's wall shadow, folds **[Exa]** into the door-shield and uses **[Latch]** to catch and steer its arm, then drives the shield edge into the open joint. The construct falls, and Lysette says "I didn't need that." He answers "I know." The cracked core is packed with grey dust.

## Ch 6 - The Quietest Lance
Okoru forms the Year 5 Lances and puts Lysette, Rowan, Clara, Felix and Eden in Lance Nine as the strongest mix on paper, with Lysette assigned to supervise Eden. In the chapel corridor Rowan's gold palm light matches a faded Lirenne fresco, and the lilies beside Clara revive without her noticing; Lysette watches Rowan and Eden and keeps writing. Eden asks to see the order for the rigged construct's core in the stores office. It is signed by Master Sorn, a forge master who died last year, and dated in the first week of November. Lysette had asked for the same line the day before.

## Ch 7 - Remember Thornmere
Rowan finds Eden on the roof and asks, loudly, to be friends. Lysette brings the first attendance warrant and writes "roof" in the margin because the printed form has no box for it. At lunch Seraphine starts her jokes on Felix and deflects when she sees the black card. A thin Cinder slips the card into Eden's book: a pressed ash-rose, a grey broken crown, and the words "Remember Thornmere". He uses it as a bookmark. Across the hall a man in a grey coat writes one short line in a count book and watches him put it away.
