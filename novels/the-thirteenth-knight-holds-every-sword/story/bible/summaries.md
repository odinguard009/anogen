# Chapter summaries (new draft; one entry per chapter as it is written)

## Ch 1 - Forty Minutes of Grass
Eden leaves Spell Grammar after the first name check and sleeps in the roof garden of the Old Observatory, where the grass is green out of season. His war dream of the ash field and the black banners stops when a girl cries one big step away: Lysette Hollen, who has just watched Rowan Tallis invite Clara Belmont to the Lantern Ball. Eden offers his mother's handkerchief with the silver feather. She takes it, orders him to class, comes back up four floors for his name, and finds sixty-one absences in her book. Last hook: "Tomorrow you attend class. I will see to it personally."

## Ch 2 - The Timetable
Eden's dream runs on the Tutor's white board, with the Dragon complaining and a silver hairpin stuck in a locked door at the edge; the floor goes soft and the Tutor says "Not that one. Not yet." At dawn Lysette brings a timetable and a spare, walks him to the dining hall and to class. Felix asks "Eaten today?" and gets "Technically." Brandmeier lets a faulty siege ward smoke and calls on Eden; he answers in six words, "The ward has no subject, Professor," and the room turns against him. At the stairs Valerius says "So you have finally stopped hiding?", gets "I'm on a timetable," and answers "Pity." Lysette writes in her book: Why does the Prince know him?

## Ch 3 - The Night Supervisor
At night Eden and Vesk watch the Solmarch Central Telephone Exchange on Bell Lane, where a Veil wearing the night supervisor has lured nine callers with the voices of their dead. Eden enters by the coal chute, puts on **[Umra]** in the cellar, and cuts the switchboard's shadow with a Door so the cables go dark. The Veil fights him with a copper bar; he folds **[Exa]** into a door-shield, refuses its trick when it cannot say his middle name, and ends it with one cut of **[Inead]**. It falls to grey dust: "You smell like the bottom of the world." The clock rings three tones and a faint fourth. The call list names Hallowcrest Year 5 families and circles Tallis; Ysolde's note demands Form 13-B before the first bell.

## Ch 4 - The Sleeper Comes to Lunch
Eden files Form 13-B before dawn. At lunch Seraphine sits at the south tables, calls him "the sleeper", and teases him like an old friend while Lysette bristles; Felix notices she looks at him as if she has known him for years. In Okoru's arms class Lysette fights Seraphine to an even two touches with batons against rapier, and Eden blocks Rowan's spear without striking back. Rowan's skipped spear tip cuts Lysette's arm; Eden's stomach goes cold and he is moving two seconds later, already holding a clean cloth. Okoru studies the way he held the shield: "That hold is not by feel, and somebody taught you." After class Lysette, not looking at him: "You are the calmest person I know. I think that's wrong."

## Ch 5 - The Thing in the Circle
In the Undercroft maze Okoru sets one student against a training construct for three minutes. Eden sees that the limit wire was cut from this side, but the drill starts with Lysette alone in the circle. The construct moves far faster than its THIRTY PER CENT tag, and a false wall slides shut on her. She breaks both of its arms and holds it the full three minutes, splitting a baton and failing to crack the doubled brass plate over its core. Rowan and Clara cannot get through the wall; Felix has no shot. Eden cuts a Door in the construct's wall shadow, folds **[Exa]** into the door-shield and uses **[Latch]** to catch and steer its arm, then drives the shield edge into the open joint. The construct falls, and Lysette says "I didn't need that." He answers "I know." The cracked core is packed with grey dust.

## Ch 6 - The Quietest Lance
Okoru forms the Year 5 Lances and puts Lysette, Rowan, Clara, Felix and Eden in Lance Nine as the strongest mix on paper, with Lysette assigned to supervise Eden. In the chapel corridor Rowan's gold palm light matches a faded Lirenne fresco, and the lilies beside Clara revive without her noticing; Lysette watches Rowan and Eden and keeps writing. Eden asks to see the order for the rigged construct's core in the stores office. It is signed by Master Sorn, a forge master who died last year, and dated in the first week of November. Lysette had asked for the same line the day before.

## Ch 7 - Remember Thornmere
Rowan finds Eden on the roof and asks, loudly, to be friends. Lysette brings the first attendance warrant and writes "roof" in the margin because the printed form has no box for it. At lunch Seraphine starts her jokes on Felix and deflects when she sees the black card. A thin Cinder slips the card into Eden's book: a pressed ash-rose, a grey broken crown, and the words "Remember Thornmere". He uses it as a bookmark. Across the hall a man in a grey coat writes one short line in a count book and watches him put it away.

## Ch 8 - Four Notes on the Stair
Eden takes Gorrath and Mirelle into the unlit Undercroft workshops to trace the grey dust. He finds an order for a core plate signed by the dead Master Sorn, and a saboteur in a grey coat comes back for the dust. Gorrath holds the passage door, Eden opens a Door in a workbench shadow, and Mirelle's **[Sile]** pins the man's sleeve through it so they can take a folded paper from his pocket before letting him go. Upstairs, Vice-headmaster Strent grades by moonlight because lamps give him headaches, and hums four notes of the lullaby Eden's mother sang. Eden stops on the stair for one verse. Strent says Wren is a Thornmere name and asks if his mother sang it too.

## Ch 9 - Orders You Haven't Earned
Ysolde corrects Eden's Form 13-B and files the report on the exchange. King Theodric asks twice whether he is eating, gives him a breakfast order for early-drill days, and stops a hand short of his shoulder. Ysolde shows that the exchange call list is the Ball guest list with Rowan circled twice, and slides a grey sealed envelope out of sight: "Orders you haven't earned." The new target is an Ember cell in the lower-city dye-works, with children inside; Eden is told to watch Rowan and "the boy with the light". The Ball is thirty days away.

## Ch 10 - The Handkerchief
Lysette does Committee rounds and reads her mother's letter, which says House Hollen will act as if the family's shame were not. She studies Eden's file: sixty-one absences, ordinary marks, a medical permit, and no punishments. Felix notes that he has practised being boring. At lunch Seraphine asks Eden, half joking, "You'd save me, right?" and he says "Probably." Seraphine starts a joke count on Felix. Lysette realises she has not thought of Rowan for an hour, then finds the handkerchief she meant to wash still folded in her pocket.

## Ch 11 - The Direction of Shadows
In the first graded field run, Okoru sends the five Lances through the Undercroft maze with lamps, false walls and two timed constructs. Lance Nine works as a unit for the first time: Felix's Trail reads dragged stone, Lysette's Witness replays the hands that moved a wall, Rowan and Clara handle the constructs, and Eden reads the lamp shadows to find a gap over the exit without taking credit. They finish third in eleven minutes. A man in a staff coat watches Rowan from the dark gallery and vanishes when Eden looks up.

## Ch 12 - Perrin
Felix finds Eden behind the stables and asks why nobody gets close to him. Eden says he does not know how. Felix gives his middle name, Perrin, before asking for anything, and Eden says honestly that he has no name he can give back. Lysette arrives with a second lunch, says she is not losing track of him, and does not look at him once. Felix tells her his middle name on his way out. The two-lunch routine begins.

## Ch 13 - The Empty Frame
Eden ends a Veil wearing the Long Stair's lamplighter in the lamp-house at third bell and leaves no trace. In the morning Lysette runs the Committee inquiry while Eden stands against the wall. Her **[Witness]** shows a figure in black and a door-shaped shadow, and her **[Testimony]** shows the replay to the students before her wrist gives out. She finds an empty pale frame-shape on the wall, like one on her desk at school, and refuses to explain it. Last line: "The Thirteenth Vigil was here. I will find out who opens the doors."

## Ch 14 - The Herb Bed
Eden tries to keep a perfect nap record while Lysette's attendance campaign grows into warrants, runners and a stakeout. Felix plays double agent for lunch. Seraphine's joke count on Felix reaches nineteen. Eden sleeps in Lysette's herb bed for eleven minutes; she finds him, leaves him asleep, covers his boots with her coat, and asks him to tell Felix only the number. She knows he has kept her garden secret for a month.

## Ch 15 - The Boy Who Pays for Bread
Lysette follows Eden into the lower city on a Committee pretext while he scouts the dye-works from a distance for Ysolde. He knows she is there, changes route to keep her away from the Ember watcher, and ends at a refugee bakery where the children know his name. He pays for bread and does not eat it. The two agree not to write down the walk back. That night the Dragon stops mid-complaint with "I promised someone" and will not say who.


## Ch 16 - This Line Is Not Crossed
Valerius demands a sanctioned Ladder duel. In the sand yard he makes pale walls with ****[Injunction]****, each ruling costing a breath, while Eden stays behind them and refuses to use the lance point. Eden's ****[Hinge]**** turns a charge past him and lands the butt behind the knee; he keeps every miss within a finger's width. Valerius notices the restraint and the count of his breaths. The bell ends the round and Valerius drops his sword: "Again. And this time try."

## Ch 17 - A Finger's Width
The duel resumes. Valerius presses honestly and Eden repeats **[Hinge]** until it is no longer a surprise. He waits for the bell, moves his shield hand a finger's width, and lets the prince's blade touch him as the bell's sound begins, so the judges record a narrow loss. Lysette binds his split knuckles too hard and scolds while he lets her. Valerius, angry but controlled, says his father knows who Eden is, and so does he, and next time he will make him show them.

## Ch 18 - Alive, on the Night of Lights
Ysolde orders Eden to watch Rowan without being seen. Rowan notices everything except the grey-coat man following him through the lower city. In an unlit lane Eden opens a Door in a wall shadow, puts the watcher asleep in an empty cellar without a chase, and reads his book: Corwen, bagman. A folded copy of the exchange call list carries two added words, "Alive. Night of lights." Ysolde confirms the list is the Ball guest list. The Ball is nine days away.
