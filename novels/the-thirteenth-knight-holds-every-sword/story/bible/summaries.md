# Chapter summaries

(New start 2026-11-25: chapters 1-25 of the first draft are archived in archive/restart-2026-11-25/ and are NOT canon for the new draft.)

## Ch 1 - Forty Minutes of Grass
Eden skips Spell Grammar to sleep on the Old Observatory roof garden, where the grass and apple tree stay green out of season. The war dream (grey ash, black banners, no air) chokes him until a girl's crying cuts it off, and he wakes with Lysette Hollen a metre away, unaware he was there. He gives her his mother's handkerchief with the silver feather. She explains that Rowan asked Clara to the Lantern Ball, keeps the handkerchief "to wash it", and notices he is her record absentee. Hook: she writes it down and says he will attend class tomorrow, because she will see to it personally.

## Ch 2 - The Timetable
In the nightly dream Eden loses a siege game to the Tutor, and the Landlord scolds him for giving his mother's handkerchief away. A crooked door with a silver hairpin in its keyhole appears, and the Tutor says "Not yet." At six Lysette wakes him with a colour-coded timetable and walks him to class. Eden answers Brandmeier's trap question in six correct words ("A command gives. A request asks."), and the room turns on him, though Felix is impressed ("Eaten today?" "Technically."). At midday Crown Prince Valerius says in front of Lysette and the hall: "So you have finally stopped hiding." Eden stays silent.

## Ch 3 - The Bottom of the World
Thirteen houses on Bell Lane get one telephone call a night from their dead. Eden and Vesk watch the Solmarch Central Telephone Exchange from a roof: night supervisor Alaric Dunmore sent the operators home and listens to every line. Eden puts on Umra in a dark stairwell (full armor-up), proves the Veil by asking its victim's middle name (Aubin), and kills it silently with doors and Inead, a sword of gold light. It dies in eleven stolen voices; the last thing it says is "You smell like the bottom of the world." It said it was told a man in black would come. Its book lists Year 5 with Rowan circled. Ysolde asks for Form 13-B and "Who knew? You, I, your shadow."

## Ch 4 - Half a Second Late
The dining-hall telephone rings and Eden drops soup, the first drop in three years. Seraphine teases him and Lysette ("like a hunting dog"). In Arms Eden only blocks with Exa and Okoru watches his feet ("Nothing. That is what bothers me."). A practice spear clips Lysette's cheek because Eden shouts half a second late; Clara heals the cut. Lysette says he reacts late to everything except danger, and "You are the calmest person I know, and I think that is the sad part." He writes it on the back of the timetable.

## Ch 5 - Latch
In the Undercroft drill a training warden of grey stone goes berserk and charges Lysette while she watches Rowan's team; one baton splits on its first stroke. Eden draws Exa, knocks it into the door-shield and catches the blade in the iron ring (**[Latch]**), then cracks the machine at the joint. Lysette's hands shake: "I didn't need that." "I know." then "Thank you." Rowan and Clara arrive and Rowan calls Eden a hero. Okoru opens the dead construct and finds its core packed with grey dust, pressed down like tea in a tin, and tells the group to say nothing.

## Ch 6 - Lance Nine
Okoru forms the Year 5 lances in the lowchapel and puts Eden, Felix, Lysette (as his "supervisor"), Rowan and Clara in Lance Nine; Eden's request for Lance Fourteen is refused ("someone who can stand still"). Lysette sits politely between Rowan and Clara. Eden alone sees the dying lilies lift near Clara. In the corridor Rowan's palms glow gold to warm Clara's hands, and Eden notices the painted man in the LIRENNE fresco holds the same light, and says nothing. Lysette trades a look at the requisition file for a promise to share what he finds. The warden's core was ordered this autumn on a form signed by Master Josselin Vane, who died last spring.

## Ch 7 - A Very Small Warrant
Lysette climbs to the roof with her first attendance warrant, stamped in pale pink because her ink pad is dry, and Eden pins it to his coat. The headmistress has told her the roof is a medical matter but the floor, stairs and classroom are not, and they bargain: all morning classes for 25 minutes of roof at lunch. Rowan hugs Eden in the courtyard and announces to everyone that they are friends; Eden bangs his head on his desk in Spell Grammar; Seraphine starts teasing Felix about lightning scars. In the lunch crush a boy in a grey scarf slips Eden a black card with a pressed ash-rose, "Remember Thornmere". A grey-coated man with a tea-brown thumb watches him pocket it and writes in a notebook.

## Ch 8 - What the Moon Allows
Eden has 26 minutes of moonlight to search the Undercroft workshop where the wardens were made. He finds Master Vane's stamp with damp ink, then calls out Vesk, Gorrath and Mirelle when a man in a long coat comes to pack a second grey-dust core. Mirelle's blade, sent through a door Eden opens, misses his throat only because he flinches; he throws his lamp and escapes up a ladder while Gorrath smothers the fire. Upstairs Eden finds vice-headmaster Strent grading by moonlight ("lamps give me headaches"). Strent hums the lullaby Eden's mother sang, and says: "Wren. A Thornmere name. Your mother must have sung that to you, too."

## Ch 9 - Orders I Have Not Earned
On his monthly walk to the palace Eden hears a ram at a locksmith's door on Wick Street. A Veil clerk with a flat shadow has hired three men to break in for "the key she never cut". Eden cannot use his armor, so Gorrath comes through a door he opens and holds the shop door, and Eden drops the Veil through a door into a cellar by asking its middle name. Mistress Ulrike gives him a plain old key that fits nothing. At the palace Ysolde invents forms for a stone construct and a capture, asks about Strent, and Eden says "nothing to report" (hiding the lullaby). She puts a grey-sealed envelope away: "Orders you have not earned." King Theodric asks if he is eating. New orders: an Ember cell in the old tanneries, and "Watch the Tallis boy", as a promise.

## Ch 10 - A Clean Page (Lysette POV)
Lysette is out of ink and reads Eden's file: spotless, with a rubbed-out pencil note on the back. She rules a chalk-ring duel by the rule, not the smudge ("the rule is not always fair, only always the same"). At lunch her mother's letter talks of House shame and ends with "I hope you are eating", while Rowan and Clara laugh at the next table. Seraphine, Felix and Eden join her; Seraphine starts a joke count on Felix (attempt 1 failed) and asks Eden "You'd save me again, right?" ("Probably."). Lysette realizes she has not thought of Rowan for an hour. That evening she finds the handkerchief in her pocket, unwashed, and does not wash it.
