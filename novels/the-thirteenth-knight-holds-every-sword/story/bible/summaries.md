# Chapter summaries

(Chapters 13-15: see story/bible/archive/summaries-ch013-ch015.md)

## Ch 16 - Court Is in Session
Valerius posts a formal Ladder challenge two hours before the sixth bell; refusing means an Inquiry, so Eden accepts. Okoru warns that blunting rings do not cover the Verdict; Lysette, duel marshal, tells him to yield and she will ring it herself. In the Long Court Valerius draws lines in the sand that rise as pale walls; one slams Eden between the shoulder blades and cracks a rib. Eden uses the frosted walls as cover, pivots past the charge (Hinge) and taps the inside of Valerius's wrist with the lance butt so the sword drops, unseen by the year; Okoru stops chewing. Valerius, low: "Again. And this time try." Nobody has rung the bell.

## Ch 17 - A Finger's Width
Rule Nine of the Ladder Code lets Valerius resume the void bout; Brandmeier and Dame Ostwald vote yes. Valerius rules the court into lanes of walls and herds Eden, who runs, scrapes his right knuckles raw and picks a dead end beside the bell stand, watching Okoru's hand. He holds a winning lance thrust a finger from the Prince's throat and lets Valerius's blade touch his coat as the bell rings, losing by a hair. Valerius, furious, calls it a favor; Lysette bandages the knuckles too tight, fails three knots, and says she will find out what it is. In the corridor Valerius: "My father knows who you are. So do I. Next time I'll make you show them."

## Ch 18 - What the Bell Market Sells
On a free Sunday nine days before the Ball, Eden trails Rowan through Solmarch's bell market (Ysolde's order) with Vesk in his shadow, Gorrath at a pretzel stand and Mirelle on a roof. Rowan notices everything but the tannin-thumb Ember behind him. Eden, torn about using a real lost child (Odile, five), has her grab the Ember's sleeve while Gorrath guards her; the Ember runs into Gorrath's cart, Mirelle pins his coat, and Eden disarms him. Corwen, a bag man with a daughter in Thornmere, says the Embers want Rowan alive on the night of lights, and points to a lantern-maker's stall. Its ledger is the Ball guest list: 41 Year 5 names with lantern numbers, Rowan no. 1, and the old line "Before the last dance". Nine days remain.

## Ch 19 - The East Kitchen
Epigraph. Rowan signs Lance Nine up for the Ball-week bake in the east kitchen (eleven ovens, one works). Clara cannot bake, Seraphine drags in Felix and her joke 41 ("a rising star") fails, and Lysette arrives with Kitchen Safety Form 22 and a rosemary sprig in her apron pocket that Rowan nearly traces to the roof; a dropped whisk saves her. Eden's plan to slip away fails. Flour dust in the air ignites when Rowan opens the oven; Eden closes a hidden door-shield on the blast and calls it luck; nobody is hurt, and Lysette writes "Luck" on the form without believing it. Seraphine sweetly asks Lysette who she is going to the Ball with; she has not decided. Eden's cracked rib flares as he leaves.

## Ch 20 - The Bend in the Line
Third person, Valerius, with epigraph. His wrist still hums where Wren hit it; Seraphine teases him on the stairs. In the palace war room the Concord map shows the Hush front curving away from Verandel's southern coast, confirmed by fourteen watch-posts for nineteen years. Ministers whisper about the Void Host; the king looks afraid and silent, and Valerius covers with a joke. In the corridor Count Merrow, his kind mentor, advises him to worry about the Ball's security instead, then asks mildly which hall the lanterns hang in. Valerius answers (the glass atrium) and does not know why he feels cold.

## Ch 21 - The Crooked Collar
Five days before the Ball, Seraphine asks Eden to go with her "as a joke, mostly" on the Long Stair landing; he declines because he will be on the roof, and briefly suspects Felix of tipping her off (he did not). Rowan, quietly humming the waltz off-key, admits he asked Clara. Lysette arrives, hears it, and writes Seraphine up for a crooked collar, then keeps the page; Isolde steals a beat with the kitchen invoice (412 crowns). That night Lysette waters a dry garden, tells Eden about Corentin teaching her the waltz, admits she has no partner, and he stays quiet until she is done. She decides to go alone "to prove I don't care." Eden thinks of the glass between them.

## Ch 22 - Blue Hour at the Dye-Works
Four days before the Ball, Eden (Umra hood and mask) watches the Duskwater Dyeworks from a rafter: twenty-two Cinders, eleven of them children, three armed Embers, and a daily ritual count ("How many vats?") plus a patch of floor everyone walks around. Vesk wears the shadow of the Ember Dietrich and calls a false "Blue Hour" alarm, tricking foreman Theobald with a wrong sum; the children are marched to Mirelle and Ysolde's wagon. Eden uses Hinge and Draught on the guards without killing. Theobald, an Ember with grey veins in his arm and Veil dust on him, says his brother is buried under the avoided floor, that a man with a kind voice gave him the graft, and that Eden is late: "The Crown already has the hall."

## Ch 23 - Thirteen Shadows
Three days before the Ball, Eden walks into the Tallow Row tannery in the full Umra; the cell expects him and ambushes him with ten crossbows. Cornered, with his left hand numb from Frost, he knocks the shield ring and Gorrath answers from the shadows with a crowd of invented "agents"; the man with the brown thumb, who once watched Rowan, surrenders his men. The ledger-room paper lists "R. Tallis. S. Aurenmark" to collect "the night before the Ball", which makes no sense because the hall is shut then. Taking off his left gauntlet, Eden finds a tiny stamped feather he has never noticed. Ysolde doubles the Golden Seal guard and orders him to stay away from the Ball; he says "Understood" too fast, and she notices.

## Ch 24 - The Eleventh Bell
On Ball night Eden sits on the iron roof of the glass atrium, two hours before the lanterns go out for the last dance, and watches Rowan dance with Clara. Lysette climbs up in her gold gown, crying about the dress and Rowan, and nearly gets Eden to slip about the tannery; he shouts "Not there!" once when she steps on a weak pane. At the eleventh bell four Embers cut the chandelier chain; Eden's door in its shadow swallows the falling ring over Seraphine and Felix, and no one but Lysette sees something is odd. The Ashen Crowns bar the doors, and an Ember shouts for "the Thornmere boy, Wren". Lysette hears it and asks why they know his name.

## Ch 25 - The Glass Floor
Eden tells Lysette the Crowns must have a list of Thornmere scholarship wards, sends her for the Golden Seal with a ten-minute order, and promises her one true answer tomorrow. In Umra he drops six grafted Embers through doors in the moon-bar shadows to the school's straw room, while Mirelle cuts twelve bowstrings and Gorrath and Vesk open the doors so the guests run. He walks through the broken glass, silent, and the Embers step back without knowing why; Seraphine's shaking hand goes still. Merrow, a hostage, admires the Ostrevaal wall cloth. Eden beats Ilsabet Rorke (Crown of Kaldmark, frost-vein pale armor) with the Lintel and spares her; she says a man with a kind voice gave her the armor. Valerius, bleeding among the hostages, says nothing and looks straight at him.
