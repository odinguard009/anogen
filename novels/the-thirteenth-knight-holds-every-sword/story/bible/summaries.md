# Chapter summaries (new draft; one entry per chapter as it is written)

## Ch 1 - Forty Minutes of Grass
Eden leaves Spell Grammar after the first name check and sleeps in the roof garden of the Old Observatory, where the grass is green out of season. His war dream of the ash field and the black banners stops when a girl cries one big step away: Lysette Hollen, who has just watched Rowan Tallis invite Clara Belmont to the Lantern Ball. Eden offers his mother's handkerchief with the silver feather. She takes it, orders him to class, comes back up four floors for his name, and finds sixty-one absences in her book. Last hook: "Tomorrow you attend class. I will see to it personally."

## Ch 2 - The Timetable
Eden's dream runs on the Tutor's white board, with the Dragon complaining and a silver hairpin stuck in a locked door at the edge; the floor goes soft and the Tutor says "Not that one. Not yet." At dawn Lysette brings a timetable and a spare, walks him to the dining hall and to class. Felix asks "Eaten today?" and gets "Technically." Brandmeier lets a faulty siege ward smoke and calls on Eden; he answers in six words, "The ward has no subject, Professor," and the room turns against him. At the stairs Valerius says "So you have finally stopped hiding?", gets "I'm on a timetable," and answers "Pity." Lysette writes in her book: Why does the Prince know him?
