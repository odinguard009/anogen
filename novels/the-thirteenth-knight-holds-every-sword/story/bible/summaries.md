## Ch 1 - Forty Minutes of Grass
Eden skips Spell Grammar to sleep on the Old Observatory roof garden, where the grass and apple tree stay green out of season. The war dream (grey ash, black banners, no air) chokes him until a girl's crying cuts it off, and he wakes with Lysette Hollen a metre away, unaware he was there. He gives her his mother's handkerchief with the silver feather. She explains that Rowan asked Clara to the Lantern Ball, keeps the handkerchief "to wash it", and notices he is her record absentee. Hook: she writes it down and says he will attend class tomorrow, because she will see to it personally.
