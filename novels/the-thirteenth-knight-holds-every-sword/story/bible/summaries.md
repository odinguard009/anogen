# Chapter summaries (new draft; one entry per chapter as it is written)

(Chapters 11-20: see story/bible/archive/summaries-ch011-ch020.md)

## Ch 21 - The Locket
Seraphine invites Eden to the Ball as a joke and he refuses quickly. Lysette writes her up for a crooked collar and cannot say why. On the roof Lysette admits she has no partner and takes out her silver locket, shut, worn once a year; she closes her hand over it when the Black Thirteen is mentioned. She tells Eden that her brother came home from court and could not dance, and that her mother wrote that House Hollen would act as if it were not. Eden says nothing useful and stays until she is done. She decides to go to the Ball "to prove I don't care."

## Ch 22 - The Smell of the Dust
Eden and Vesk enter the lower-city dye-works. Vesk uses **[Mott]** to wear a dead Ember's shadow and sends the four Cinder children out on a false drill order. Eden ends the three cell leaders one at a time with **[Draught]**, then wipes the room. A dying Ember's grey veins and the dry smell of the dust tell Eden that the cell is tied to the Veil network, though he does not know what the veins mean. The last Ember laughs: "You're late. The Crown already has the hall."

## Ch 23 - Thirteen Voices
Eden raids the second Ember safehouse in full **[Umra]**. Gorrath's **[Knock and Answer]** fills the street with thirteen voices from thirteen shadows, and the hired men inside surrender to an army that is not there. Eden finds the Ball plan: Rowan and Seraphine are named as guests to collect, but the written night is Friday, a day before the Ball. He takes the paper and a brass crown weight. Unlacing his left gauntlet, he finds a tiny feather stamped inside. Ysolde then doubles the Justiciar Seal guard and orders him away from the Ball.

## Ch 24 - The Eleventh Bell
Eden watches the Ball from the roof above the glass atrium while Lysette, in a gown, cries over Rowan dancing with Clara. At the eleventh bell the lanterns go out and the chandelier falls; Eden catches it in a Door cut from its shadow across the roof glass, unseen by the room. The Ashen Crowns then seize the atrium with blades at throats and demand two guests. An Ember points up through the glass and shouts for "the Thornmere boy, Wren"; Lysette hears him say they thought he was theirs.

## Ch 25 - The Lattice
Lysette fetches the guard while Eden re-enters the seized atrium through Doors cut in the roof lattice's shadows. Ilsabet Rorke wears a graft in her right arm and uses **[Hrak]**; Eden takes a blade on his shield and answers with the first **[Lintel]**, a flat overhead shield with **[Inead]** drawn under it in one motion. The hostages see only a dark shape and a gold glint. He spares Ilsabet and uses the lattice shadows to move the students out. Valerius sees more than he should; Merrow admires an Ostrevaal wall hanging. At the door Lysette says she saw smoke where a man should have been and orders Eden back to the roof before heads are counted.

## Ch 26 - On My Foot
Back on the roof after the Ball siege, Lysette asks Eden whether he was up there the whole time and he answers "No." She accepts it. They stand on each other's feet for a slow dance while the band still plays below; he smiles once, a real one, and she does not mention it. Eden sleeps without the war dream for the first time in years. At dawn he finds his gloves on the stone with the handkerchief inside, washed and folded with the feather turned inward.

## Ch 27 - The Year on the Page
The steam press prints a paper blaming the Vigil for lawlessness at the Ball. Lysette folds it away when she reaches a Seal notice about the Hollen affair of PR 110; Eden reads the year twice and records a cold stomach and a pulse count without saying anything. Finals keep him at eight, and the board gives his first on-page reading, **[Knight]** and **[Black Lancer]**. Valerius walks past without a word. On the roof Lysette opens the locket to show him a young man's face, and Eden looks at the apple tree for eleven seconds. A Seal inquiry into the Ball night's unknown knight is posted.

## Ch 28 - The House at Seven
Eden and Felix take the veinline train to Silverfell, where the Argentine household is loud and the Duke pulls them both into dinner. Bandits on the carriage road are dealt with over soup and fish: a pistol misfires twice near Bertram and he takes it away politely, with no one hurt. Bertram's boots were muddy at dawn; the Duke nearly says "your mother had w-" before Greta's ladle strikes a dish; and a bell rings once by itself when the Duke laughs. Felix asks which of the family's noise and Eden's quiet is the act.

## Ch 29 - The Crown with No Centre
Felix presses Eden for the truth about his quietness and gets a half-truth he can live with. On the lake the Duke tries again to speak about Liora and something she was making for Eden, but cannot finish; they fish in silence and he puts his one catch back. That night the Tutor sets a board with nine pawns and tells Eden that a king once failed his test. On the edge of the board sits a crown with no centre.

## Ch 30 - The Boy's Late Mother
With the boys gone, the Duke studies the bandits' orders and notices they were paid by someone who knew his carriage road. An academy letter signed by Halvard Strent has a wrong total and asks after "the boy's late mother", though the Wren file lists none. He takes Sevar's helm into the chapel for an hour of humming and says nothing about what happens inside. At the door he asks Bertram for a horse and tells him not to put it in a ballad.

## Ch 31 - The Cold Here
Lance Nine arrives on the Southern Wall as its own unit for the Winter Rotation. A Wall sergeant shows why a mana-lock musket and Ward-plate are not enough against Huskborn: balls stop in grey flesh, and a blade needs an arm and aura behind it. When a Husk probes the wall, real soldiers finish it in two cuts. A kind camp groundskeeper hums out of tune, an old soldier says the ground is cold "like she turned her back", and at the third watch every bird leaves the battlement at once.
