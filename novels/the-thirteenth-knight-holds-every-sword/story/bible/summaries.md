# Chapter summaries (new draft; one entry per chapter as it is written)

(Chapters 21-30: see story/bible/archive/summaries-ch021-ch030.md)

## Ch 31 - The Cold Here
Lance Nine arrives on the Southern Wall as its own unit for the Winter Rotation. A Wall sergeant shows why a mana-lock musket and Ward-plate are not enough against Huskborn: balls stop in grey flesh, and a blade needs an arm and aura behind it. When a Husk probes the wall, real soldiers finish it in two cuts. A kind camp groundskeeper hums out of tune, an old soldier says the ground is cold "like she turned her back", and at the third watch every bird leaves the battlement at once.

## Ch 32 - The Silent Stretch
A Choir silence falls over the Wall and Lance Nine holds its stretch without voices or spoken spells. Okoru teaches three hand signs before the bell, and Lysette reads Eden's signs perfectly during the fight. Okoru uses the first [Tavu] to bind a climbing Husk to a brass bracket, Rowan finishes it with his spear, and Mirelle strikes unseen from the stair shadow. The circle holds. At dusk Eden feels a slow pull under a drain: the Choir is not dead but moving beneath the wall.

## Ch 33 - The Second Envelope
After the Choir silence, a bellwire announces the king's inspection of the Wall. Okoru notices Lysette's cold hand while Eden only records that her pencil slips. The king questions the sergeant and Rowan, and Eden hears Ysolde say the second envelope is still in the safe. Lysette gives him a grey-green scarf as equipment so she will not be stuck with his paperwork, and he wears it all winter. As the king leaves he stops only at Cadet Wren: "Your mother would have hated the cold."

## Ch 34 - The Cistern
Lance Nine descends into the cistern beneath the Wall to end the Choir before its next song. Felix's Trail reads the current under a submerged slab, and Rowan's first **[Dawnbreak]** floods the dark with gold for one breath, freezing the Choir's shadow edge long enough for Eden to cut a Door and drop the node into deep water. Sound returns. Okoru tells Rowan not to practice the light, and Eden keeps to himself that it made his doors easier. On the horizon a shape taller than the clouds moves once and is still.

## Ch 35 - The Lodge
During Wall rations week Seraphine uses the first [Switcheroo] to trade posts and sit beside Eden. She tells Felix the story of the winter lodge: a fire gone out, children under a table, a boy with a sword who counted them out in four minutes and gave her his glove. Eden corrects her number from nine to eleven, and she accepts it without asking how he knew. Lysette writes Seraphine up again for the post swap, but her hand shakes and she cannot change the nine she has already written.

## Ch 36 - The Second Reading
A Veil is wearing Storems, a popular garrison sergeant, and soldiers would defend him. Eden enters the roll room at change of watch, opens a Door behind the sergeant and bounces **[Inead]**'s light off a signal mirror so the grey shows without anyone seeing the sword. He cuts once. The reading in his head changes to **[Void Heir]** while he holds the Door, and he holds his breath for one beat. Vesk warns him the smell will stay on the coat. A veteran calls it "Thirteenth Vigil work. Like the Hollen boy", and Lysette hears.

## Ch 37 - The Last Page
Ysolde sends Eden the PR 110 file after he unknowingly requested it on a form. It carries his own confirm mark beside Corentin Hollen's name, but he cannot bring the face into focus, only a collar, a stair and a voice stopping. Felix tells him he has made a list of Eden's habits and offers help; Eden refuses. Lysette asks him back to the watch, and Clara brings tea. The last page is an index whose final two lines were crossed out before the file was copied.

## Ch 38 - Make Him Tell Me Why
A small fire in a barracks hut brings the Lance together. Beside the burnt curtain Lysette tells them about her brother's cart-wheel laugh, the paper that called him a traitor, and how he came home colder and could not dance. Eden says nothing: he repeats his rule that he always confirms the grey, but cannot bring the face back to prove it. For a week he is the one who looks away first. She tells him that if she ever finds the one who did it, she will not kill him but make him explain.

## Ch 39 - The First Oil Line
Rowan asks why Lysette hates him; Eden gives a sideways truth and Clara supplies the whole one, so Rowan plans an apology away from an audience. Okoru tells the story of the one-armed cistern hold with bells ringing by themselves, and Eden records it without connecting it to the man in front of him. He lays the first oil line under the camp for lamps. Vesk reports that the Mawgate is closer than the maps say: under the camp itself.

## Ch 40 - The Box for the Dead
At midnight Ysolde files the PR 110 report and pays for the broken mirror so Eden will not try to pay it himself. She refuses to bring the king's second envelope from the safe, answers the Duke's letter about Strent without asking questions, and pulls the old file of the physician who vanished the week Liora died. She leaves Liora's letter unopened and sends Eden wool socks as equipment with a form. A bellwire message reports a missing groundskeeper's boots at the camp.
