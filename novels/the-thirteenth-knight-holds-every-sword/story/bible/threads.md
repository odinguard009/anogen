# Thread ledger (reference). Dangle policy and every major open thread.

## Dangle policy
- Every major thread gets a feed (clue, development or mini-reveal) at least every 40 chapters, and a partial answer within about 30-40 chapters of being raised.
- Arc-level questions are answered by the arc's end (see Resolution lines in arcs files).
- Long-range mysteries are allowed only if they appear in the feed list below on schedule.
- Cliffhangers inside an arc resolve within 3 chapters; an arc-ending hook resolves or develops within the first 5 chapters of the next arc.
- Romance: no couple goes ~25 chapters without a beat (romance.md).
- Canon Keeper updates this ledger when a thread is raised, fed or paid; Development Editor checks it when planning each arc.
