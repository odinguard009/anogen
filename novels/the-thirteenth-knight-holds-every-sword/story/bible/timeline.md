# Timeline (reference). PR = Pale Reckoning (years since the First Breach). Story opens autumn PR 112.

## Before the Breach
- Before worlds: the Ten Sovereigns of the Sepiroth make worlds together. Kaurveth (Want) devours worlds and is cast beneath the Void; Melchsee (Ending) sits on the throne as the lid. Myrrhen dissolves into Elysmar as mana. Melchsee and Sepiroth war over the remnants. About 3,000 years before PR: Vehra's Binding of Thrones seals the gods' wills into mortal hosts on Elysmar, passing on death; Melchsee stays free as the lid; Sepiroth is bound to the Vault's gate (gods.md, gods-hosts.md).
- Ages ago: the Emperor of Void rules the Void from the Throne of Nothing; Ten Pillars and nine sleeping legions serve him; beneath him lies the Hush. The Dragon Sepiroth rules Heaven Gate and keeps the Vault; its Valkyries glean holy weapons from dying worlds. Dragon and Emperor: enemies for ten thousand years.
- Halveren is a patchwork of realms: Verandel (House Aurenmark) and eleven other kingdoms, plus Skarnheim and the Aurelis See. House Argentine has served the Aurenmark crown for centuries.

## Breach and conquest
- PR 0: the weary Emperor's throne thins; the Hush cracks Elysmar's pole (the Wound). The Dragon pulls every relic into the Vault (Night the Relics Vanished). Valkyries begin gleaning on the southern fronts.
- PR 0-61: Glaceren, then Ruhnmara fall (slow era).
- PR 9: Hallowcrest founded by the then-king.
- PR 40: the Tailor first recorded.
- PR 53: Gerulf Aurenmark crowned at 20. Starts wars at once.
- PR 53-68: Gerulf conquers all Halveren (eleven kingdoms made duchies; Skarnheim and Aurelis occupied). Becomes the Iron King. At 35 he plans to invade other continents despite the Hush.
- PR 83: Gerulf 50. Crown Prince Theodric (20) and young Duke Aldous Argentine (21) plot a coup; it fails; Aldous is sent to the Okarune front to die. There he meets Liora, a Valkyrie gleaning relics from a falling city; they fight side by side; she stays for him, sealing her wings. Soldiers call her the Valkyrie of Heaven Gate.
- PR 86: Ysolde Lisle (then a palace clerk for the prince) forges Liora's identity: last daughter of extinct House Wren of Thornmere. Aldous and Liora marry PR 88. PR 85-92 Liora forges Sevar for Aldous and Tamu and Oru for Theodric, and begins Aster for a child she hopes for (liora.md). Wren stays safe for Eden later: the house is extinct, the public only ever called her "the Duchess", her maiden name sits in sealed registers, and Wren is a common surname among Thornmere refugees.
- PR 93: Gerulf, 60, performs the Rite of the Far Door (text obtained from a Volkhovar oracle; truly Vehra's gift) in the sunken chapel of Grauvale to summon a weapon for three continents. The Emperor of Void answers, dragged through the Hush; the Wound tears wider. The Emperor's Void Host marches; nations flee; nobody dies, though nobody notices that until later. Inside Verandel people fear him more than the Hush.
- PR 93, winter: the Emperor's test ends. Theodric, Aldous and Liora (pregnant) turn on the Iron King. The Emperor intervenes, fights the three, acknowledges them, and kills Gerulf: "I have never killed a human on this world. Until now." He says he is empty, that endless life stole his purpose. Seeing Liora's child will be unmade by the Refusal, he pours all his essence and power into the unborn child: "Perhaps this is what I came for." He vanishes. Theodric is crowned. The public is told the Iron King died of a fever; the Void Host was "disbanded".
- PR 94: Eden Aster Argentine is born. Theodric frees Skarnheim and Aurelis. Liora, at an Ivory infirmary, saves an infant girl dying of Pale fever with one of her feathers: Lysette Hollen.
- PR 93-112: with the Wound wider, Okarune and Selvara fall. Volkhovar's Empress Dragomira (crowned PR 97) begins the Ledger of Souls. Tawanqar loses its Crown Well PR 101. The Concord of Solmarch meets yearly, never unites.

## Eden's life
- PR 99 (5): genius shows: reads adult texts, talks a panicked horse calm, asks why grown-ups lie to be kind.
- PR 100-104: dreams: a locked door, a faceless man at a board game, endless war.
- PR 104 (10), Liora's birthday: Eden secretly opens the Sepiroth Gate and draws out a silver hairpin, Ynde, as a gift. The flare of Heaven-light wrapped in Void is what the Tailor (wearing family physician Aubrec Wendell) had waited ten years to confirm. That night it comes to take the essence. Liora unseals her wings for the first time in 21 years and fights it in the glasshouse; with Keeping she locks Eden's Gate, Door and memory and pins the Tailor; the Refusal takes her as she finishes. She becomes light and feathers. The Tailor tears free hours later.
- Official cause: sudden mana-wither. Eden remembers the glasshouse, her hand over his eyes, cold, and believes he froze while she died. His feelings go flat. The Duke keeps the truth because Liora asked him to.
- PR 106 (12): Volkhovar's Frost Hand kidnaps Princess Seraphine at the Argentine hunting lodge. Eden goes alone, uses Door and Inead, and brings her back. The twins see everything.
- PR 107 (13): Theodric and Aldous create the Thirteenth Vigil for him. Eden asks that his existence be hidden until he chooses. Public: the heir is raised by tutors in the north.
- PR 108 (14): enters Hallowcrest as Eden Wren, scholarship ward. Headmistress Ostwald is told his name and power.
- PR 110: the Thirteenth Vigil executes "traitor" Corentin Hollen (a Veil).
- PR 112: Year 5 begins 1 Sept. The story opens Monday 2 Nov PR 112, six weeks before the Lantern Ball (Sat 12 Dec). Eden is 18 (born 3 March PR 94). Full day-by-day anchors: bible/calendar.md (it wins over this file).
- PR 114, 26 June: graduation (ch 200). Eden is 20.
