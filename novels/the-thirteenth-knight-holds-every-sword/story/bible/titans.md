# Hushborn by size, and the titan wars (reference)

## Size classes (Concord field terms)
1. **Veil class** (human size): Veils, Stalkers. Fought by: detection (Ea, middle-name oaths), Black Order, Golden Seal.
2. **Husk class** (dog to horse): Husks, small Choirs. Fought by: shield lines, crossbows, Ward-plate squads of five (Lances), fire.
3. **Ravager class** (elephant to house): armoured brutes that smash walls; large Choirs. Fought by: Crimson Rampart force walls, Umber Anvil ballistae ("veinspears"), Grey Gale lance charges at the joints.
4. **Wallbreaker class** (castle size, 30-60 m): siege beasts and living Mawgates. Fought by: mines and counter-tunnels (Umber Anvil), Violet Lantern siege spells, concentrated veinspear batteries, orders working together.
5. **Colossus class** (100-200 m): walking or swimming kaiju-scale bodies that change a battle by existing; they carry Husk swarms like fleas and silence for kilometres. Fought by: coalitions, terrain (straits, mountains, deep water), god-hosts, Eden.
6. **Titan class** (300 m and more): mountain-walkers, named, each a piece of Kaurveth's body grown independent. Nine known. Fought by: whole fronts.
7. **Sovereign scale**: Kaurveth's body itself (finale).

## Hierarchy (minds vs bodies)
Minds: Veils (scouts) < Hierarchs (generals, seven) < Kaurveth. Bodies: Husks < Ravagers < Wallbreakers < Colossi < Titans. Hierarchs often ride or steer a Colossus or Titan.

## How Eden fights giants (and why it stays creative)
- A giant's shadow is a giant door: the bigger they are, the bigger the door he can open, but the harder to control. Titans are the only things that let him use the Door at full scale.
- Doors under a titan's feet into deep water or the sky shadow of a cliff; doors inside its own shadow to move whole regiments onto its back.
- Threshold swallows a titan's silence-breath and releases it at its own Choirs.
- Relic loans to siege crews (a veinspear battery armed with relic bolts), the Thousandfold Knight (armor.md), and allied hosts: Han's walls, Kirana's tides, Rowan's sun for hard shadows.
- Rule: never the same method twice (battle-craft.md tricks 51-58).

## Politics of titans
- Command: the Titan Accord (Concord, ch 268) fights over who commands joint forces when a titan crosses borders; Volkhovar refuses to put the Ice Legions under anyone.
- Cost-sharing: who pays for veinspears and griffons; Ezharan bargains its Sandneck for shared costs (arc 6).
- Titan-bone trade: Hush flesh from dead giants is worth fortunes (grafts, research). The Lanternhouse and Volkhovar race to buy corpses; Eden burns them, which makes enemies (ch 290, 389).
- Blame: nations accuse each other of letting titans pass their coasts toward rivals.
