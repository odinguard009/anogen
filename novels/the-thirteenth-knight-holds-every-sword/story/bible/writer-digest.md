# Writer digest: power, battle and armor rules (short form for writers)
DERIVED from power-system.md (sections 1-16). On any conflict, power-system.md wins. If this digest lacks a detail you need, read only that section there. Update this file whenever power-system.md changes.

## The stack
MANA (fuel) -> AURA (body field) -> TENET (one unique power) -> CLASS/JOB (registered role naming learned skills). Outside it: gear (relics, Raiments, Ward-plate, guns) and god powers. NO ranks, NO levels.
- MANA: fuel. Bells (1-7) only measure tank size, never skill or hit strength. Refills with sleep, food, time near a well. Overdraw = mana-wither (cold hands, grey temples, slow heart).
- AURA: a hand-deep body field, raised with one deep breath, dropped by breathing out. It turns aside small fast things (bullets, stones, knives), dulls untrained blades, boosts strength and speed, holds back panic, fear-magic and the Hush's cold, and is felt by others. Every stopped hit costs mana. Slow and heavy things get through (bolts, axes, fists, falling stone, cannonballs). Civilians have almost none. An "aura moment" = a full deliberate raise. Eden keeps his low in public.
- TENET: one unique power with a Law; bends the rules once, cleverly, inside its law. Show the law.
- CLASS/JOB: registered role read by the planet-mind Aiwa; shown only to the owner and the reader as one rare bold-bracket line, e.g. **[Knight]**. Never a rank.

## Eden's god powers (names only: Door and Gate)
- Door = the Shadow Legions: he commands shadow knights (starts: up to 40; mass waking 10,000 at ch 248; nine legions of about a million in the full plan). Needs a shadow big enough; costs mana per minute; they never kill humans, they disarm and wall off; light kills the exit; the Frost follows deep use. Shadow doors on the page are the army's exits.
- Gate = the Vault: he summons any weapon, armor or relic stored in the Sepiroth Vault. Needs the weapon's name (met in the dream Vault); it may refuse a bad cause; each held relic drains him; overdraw gives fever and nosebleed. Lending needs the person's middle name.
- Army holds the field; the Vault gives the one right weapon. Neither is a level.

## Armor
- Armor exists to fight Invaders (the Hush) without the victor's side effects: Husk blood rots flesh (sealed plate, double-sealed cuffs); Stalker venom steals voice and mana (closed gorget with a tuned ring); Choir silence stops orders (bellwire sigils); Veil dust fogs memory (salt-silk filter helm, visor sealed first); Pale ground drains mana (core greaves, ward-soles).
- Against humans knights wear light guard kit; full armor is for invaders and big moments.
- Armor-up scene (Garo style): three knocks and the middle name; a sound and a shift in the light; closing order boots, legs, chest, shoulders, gauntlets, helm LAST with a bell-note; one unique detail; the Tolls are the clock; the cost lands after. Bold label **[Armor name]** when the helm closes.
- Ward-plate (common): seals wear out; unsealed glove or cracked seam = small disasters.

## Unbind (rare awakening; not a rank)
Appears only per the reveal ladder with earlier hints. Trigger: a conviction tested at real cost, chosen again, then the person says their own middle name aloud. Sign: every bell in earshot rings once, dust and rain stop for a breath, aura becomes visible. Effect: the Tenet loses its one limit, Law intact, for 1-5 minutes; then hollow 1-3 days; each Unbind takes one small thing forever. Label **[Unbind: Form name]**. A relic loan (middle name spoken low to the relic) is NOT an Unbind. Form names and rows: power-system.md section 14 (read only the rows of people in your range).

## Rules of battle
1. Say distances in steps (guns/bolts from fifty, spells thirty, blades and Tenets inside ten). 2. Aura stops fast and light, not slow and heavy. 3. Every action costs mana; show tiredness rising. 4. Beat the aura first: heavy blow, aura-pushed blade, sigil or trick. 5. In close fights a fast blade beats a slow spell; prepared wards beat both. 6. A Tenet bends the rules once, inside its law. 7. Matchup beats size. 8. After about fifteen hard minutes aura thins; costs land after the fight. 9. Guns and cannon do not end knights (mana fields foul powder and mana-locks). 10. Terrain and preparation decide most fights.
Checklist per fight: positions and light; each aura's job and what gets through; the ONE ability that changes the fight and its limit; visible cost; first use described (look, body, effect, limit); why the winner wins (never "more power").

## Word rules
Bell (grade) = tank size only ("three Bells"); bell (time) = hour, lower case; aura = body field ("raise", "drop"); edge = line between light and dark (lower case); Crown = a Well or an Ashen Crown leader, never a stage; reading = the registry line, italic, rare.
