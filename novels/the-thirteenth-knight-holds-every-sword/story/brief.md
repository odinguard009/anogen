## Genre and tone
- Fantasy world that may imitate and modify ideas from existing works (owner order 2026-11-25).
- Korean web-novel / Japanese light-novel feel. Language of the story: English.
- Audience: teenagers and young adults. Simple, easy-to-read English (see READABILITY in story/style/profile.md).
- Never boring: twists planted early, heartfelt moments, heart-pounding scenes.

## Protagonist
- Close third person, past tense (owner order 2026-11-26): the camera follows Eden from outside. He is flat: he feels little and late, so the narration shows actions, objects and others' reactions, never his feelings. Fights are shown like a tactician's plan.
- Nonchalant on the surface, secretly caring, cool, realistic, honest, principled.
- A chapter from another character's view (Lysette, Valerius, the Duke, Ysolde) about once every 8-10 chapters; they may feel more than Eden does.

## Romance and comedy
- Pure slow-burn romance. Yearning early, climax later. Complications allowed.
- No harem. Light comedy that never undercuts key emotional or tense scenes.

## Hard content limits (never break)
- No sexual content of any kind. No ecchi. No harem.

## World-building rules
- No info-dump: max 3 consecutive sentences of pure exposition.
- Max 1-2 new world concepts per chapter. Reveal through action and conflict.

## Battles
- Detailed, exciting, varied. Different tactic, terrain and stake each time.
