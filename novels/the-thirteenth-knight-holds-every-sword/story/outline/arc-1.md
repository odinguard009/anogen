# Arc 1: The Rooftop (chapters 1-50; Year 5 autumn term and Winter Rotation, Mon 2 Nov PR 112 to Mon 1 Feb PR 113). Rewritten whole 2026-11-26 (owner replan). Dates: bible/calendar.md (wins). POV: close third person (profile.md sections 3-4). Old drafts of ch 1-10 are superseded; nothing here refers to them.

Central question: can Eden stay unseen once someone insists on seeing him?
Enemy: an Ashen Crowns cell (Embers, two grafted Crowns) fed by a Veil network; the Hush behind the Wall.
RESOLUTION (ch 50): the Mawgate nest under the Wall is destroyed and the camp saved (Clara badly wounded, nobody of Lance Nine lost); the Ashen cell that hit the Ball is broken, Desmond Harrow dead, Ilsabet Rorke spared and gone; Eden is still officially rank 8 and unseen by the academy, but not by Lysette (her Witness saw white light where he stood) or Felix (who keeps the secret without asking). Eden and Lysette are real friends (roof dance 26, scarf 33); Felix holds his first relic.
HOOK (into arc 2): dying grey, the Crown of Gallowmarch says "The Tailor sends regards to Liora's boy." Someone knows his mother's name. Arc 2 develops this within ch 51-55.

## Rules this outline obeys (owner orders 2026-11-26)
- Power logic: the circle is at or above line-knight standard with three Summer Postings behind them (power-system.md section 17). They win ordinary fights themselves. They lose, or need help, only against out-of-spec things (rigged constructs, Veils, grafts, a lit hall of hostages). A chapter never shows them as first-years.
- Silent, secret, precise: every Vigil job is one quiet room, no crowd, no chase. The Black Thirteen is a half-believed legend.
- Eden is flat; the camera is outside him. Reveal rungs that used his inner voice are delivered through a speaker, an object or a short memory scene (marked "delivery" below).
- Labels **[Name]** every time (profile.md section 6). First use in the same scene.
- The Winter Rotation is a real step up from the summers: summers were rear and escort sectors with rows of veterans; the Rotation puts Lance Nine, as its own unit and for the first time, on a front battlement in Choir season. The new kit shown for the first time is shown on conscripts and sergeants; the circle watch it as veterans do.

Battle mix: flex 3, 22, 36; puzzle 5, 11, 25, 32, 34; strained 16-17; costly 45-50. Aura: 25 (glass walk), 49 (the look). Forms debut: Latch 5, Hinge 16, Draught 22, Lintel 25, Lampwick 49.
Interludes (other POV): 10 Lysette, 20 Valerius, 30 the Duke, 40 Ysolde. Comic chapters: 7, 14, 19, 28, 35, 42. Epigraphs: outline/epigraphs.md (1, 10, 14, 19, 20, 28, 30, 40).
Sections: the Ladder rungs are in bible/reveal-ladder.md (every rung chapter below is fixed there). First uses: bible/first-use.md.
