# Arc 1 concept: "The Rooftop" (ch 1-50, Year 5 autumn to Winter Rotation, PR 112)

Central question: can Eden stay unseen once someone insists on seeing him?
Three threads: (1) Lysette drags him to class, and class drags him into fights; (2) the Ashen Crowns mistake "Eden Wren" for a Thornmere sympathizer, then an obstacle; (3) Thirteenth Vigil nights: Veils feeding the Ashen Crowns.
Escalation: a card, a sabotage, a Ball under siege, a Wall attack where terrorists and Hushborn move together, and proof the enemy knows his mother's name.
Power rule: lance-as-shield in class; Inead only masked or when someone will die.

## First 5 chapters (hooks)
1. Rooftop garden, afternoon. Dream of endless war, can't breathe. Crying wakes him; a girl sits a metre away, unaware of him. He offers a handkerchief (tiny silver feather stitched in the corner). Lysette Hollen, mortified, sharp: Rowan invited Clara to the Lantern Ball. She checks her Committee list: "Eden Wren. Sixty-one absences." Hook: "Tomorrow you attend class. I will see to it personally."
2. Dream: the Tutor's board game, the Dragon ("Landlord") complaining; a locked door with a silver hairpin in its keyhole. Dawn: Lysette at his door. Felix: "Eaten today?" Brandmeier calls on him; he answers in six words, correct. Hook: Valerius, in front of Lysette: "So you've finally stopped hiding?"
3. Night, Solmarch lantern market, masked. A Veil wearing a tax clerk; door cut in an awning's shade; Inead. Dying: "You smell like the bottom of the world." Ysolde: Form 13-B. Hook: the clerk's ledger lists Hallowcrest Year 5 names, Rowan's circled.
4. Lunch: Seraphine teases Eden ("my saviour slept through breakfast again"); Lysette bristles. Arms class: Eden only blocks. Lysette's locket: her brother, executed by the Thirteenth Vigil. Hook: "I will prove they are monsters."
5. Undercroft drill. A training construct goes berserk and nearly crushes Lysette while she watches Rowan; Eden's lance becomes a door-shield just in time. Hook: the construct's core is packed with grey dust.

## Beat list
- 6-10: Lance Nine formed (Eden, Felix, Lysette, Rowan, Clara): Lysette suffers politely; Rowan finds the Lirenne fresco (6). Ashen card "Remember Thornmere" (7). Strent's lullaby (8). Ysolde's envelope (9). Seraphine's "you'd save me again, right?" (10). Ch 10 interlude: Lysette.
- 11-15: Felix gives his middle name (12). Lysette's Witness at a Thirteenth Vigil kill site shows a door-shaped shadow (13). Lysette, Committee duty, follows Eden off-campus. Dragon: "I promised someone" (15).
- 16-20: Ladder duel vs Valerius: Eden fights only defensively and loses by a hair on purpose; Valerius is furious he held back. Concord map (20). Ch 20 interlude: Valerius.
- 21-26: Ember cell in the lower city; a dying Ember's graft (22). Lantern Ball (24-26): Eden skips it for the roof; Lysette arrives in her gown to cry again; the Ashen Crowns seize the atrium to take Rowan and Seraphine. Eden enters through the roof glass shadows; fights the Crown of Kaldmark (Ilsabet Rorke) with lance and Gorrath's cover and spares her; Valerius sees. Merrow admires a tapestry (25). Eden and Lysette's first dance happens on the roof after, to the band still playing below.
- 27-30: Aftermath, finals; Eden still rank 8. Weekend at Silverfell with Felix: the household's comedy, bandits dispatched politely; the Duke nearly says "your mother had wings" (28). Tutor's "test" (30). Ch 30 interlude: the Duke.
- 31-40: Winter Rotation on the Southern Wall. A Choir silences the battlement; Lance Nine fights with hand signals. King's "second envelope" (33). Lysette tells the Lance about Corentin; Eden realizes he killed his Veil (38) and says nothing. Rowan asks why Lysette hates him (40). Ch 40 interlude: Ysolde.
- 41-50: Ashen Crowns and Hushborn hit the student camp together through a Mawgate under the Wall; grafted Embers. Night battle: Eden lights oil fires to make edges for doors. Loans Thessa to Felix (47). Clara is wounded saving Rowan (cost). Eden attacks seriously with Inead; the Lance sees only white light; Lysette replays it with Witness and stares at him. The Crown of Gallowmarch, dying grey: "The Tailor sends regards to Liora's boy." (50)
