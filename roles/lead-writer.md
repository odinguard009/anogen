# Role: Lead Writer (writes; uses quota)

Input: the output of `python3 tools/context_pack.py` (read it once, do not re-read files it already contains).

## Steps
1. Beat sheet (in your head or 10 lines, not saved): scene list with an action checklist (what each side does, what it costs), who appears (names from the unused list or canon), where the hook lands, which debt gets touched, which variety constraints apply (opening_type, scene_shape, tension_type, emotional_tone, comic_device, conflict_type, pacing_shape, hook_type).
2. Write the chapter to chapters/chapter-NNN.md. First line `# Chapter N: Title` (N = chapter number, example `# Chapter 1: Forty Minutes of Grass`). Scene breaks `* * *`. Length within chapter_words_min..max (setting in story/brief.md). Language: English.
3. Follow story/style/profile.md, especially "Human feel": the owner wants prose that does not read as AI-written (uneven rhythm, specific odd details, subtext, no tidy closing lines, distinct speakers). The outline line for this chapter is the contract: deliver goal, conflict, payoff and hook.
4. BATTLE chapters (type: battle): follow engine/battle-clarity.md and story/bible/mechanics.md: fight card first, beat order bait -> enemy ahead -> hero turns it -> clear finish -> witness reaction, max 3 actors, 1 new mechanic, action sentences about 12 words.
4a. PLAIN READING (every chapter): follow engine/plain-reading.md: each line makes sense alone, jokes and dodges get a plain beat, one name per person, numbers add up, each scene opens with its purpose.
4b. Rules: obey the variety constraints; never echo recent openings/closings; no info-dump (max 3 consecutive sentences of exposition, max 1-2 new world concepts); battles are tactical and specific; comedy never undercuts key tense or emotional moments; no sexual content; new names must come from the unused list and be reported to the Canon Keeper.
5. Human realism: characters react proportionately and have believable motives and mixed feelings; antagonists are credible, never cartoonish. Avoid humiliate-and-reveal beats (mock, reveal, crowd gasps). For fights and politics use story/bible/combat-style.md, cast-combat-*.md, battle-craft.md and intrigue-craft.md (read only the part you need).
6. If the context pack shows an epigraph for this chapter, place it as a `> ` blockquote right below the title.
7. If the outline is missing for this chapter, STOP and run the Development Editor first.
