# Royal Academy of Hallowcrest (reference)

## Basics
- On a terraced plateau above Solmarch, linked by the Long Stair and a funicular of vein-stone. Founded PR 9 by royal decree "to forge the hands that will push the grey back". The most famous academy on Elysmar; foreign nations pay to send exchange students (a diplomatic weapon).
- Run by the Bronze Bell Order. Headmistress Dame Hildegard Ostwald (old, sharp, half-blind, rarely leaves her tower). Vice-headmaster Halvard Strent runs daily life and is loved by students.
- About 2,400 students, 600 per year. Entry at 16 after the Kindling (Tenet known) and a Bell test of at least 2 Bells.

## Four years, graduation at 19/20
- Year I, Foundations: mana control, spell grammar, history of the Breach, riding, basic arms, the Concord's languages.
- Year II, Arms and Tenet: Tenet discipline, duels, small-unit drills in the Undercroft (a maze of training halls under the plateau).
- Year III, Field: Lances (5-student squads) take real tasks: escort refugee convoys, patrol the Verdant marches, the Winter Practicum on the Southern Wall (live Hushborn contact, supervised).
- Year IV, Command: students lead Lances of younger students, serve as Concord honor guard, and face the Crossing (final trial: a night on the Teeth beyond the Wall). Graduates are posted the same month: to the front, or as squire-knights to one of the twelve orders.
- Transfers into Year III are rare and need a royal writ. Eden is one.

## Ranks and life
- The Ladder: public class ranking by duels, field marks and theory. Top 10 of each year wear a bronze pin.
- Lances are fixed for a full year. A dead or vanished member's slot stays open with the name on the roster until term ends (custom).
- Dorms: stone towers by year, two to a room (Eden rooms with Tobias). Curfew bell at the tenth hour; the Undercroft is sealed at night.
- Events: Autumn Bell Assessment; Founding Lantern Night; Winter Practicum; the Silver Lists (spring tourney, nobles and orders watching, scouts recruiting); summer break; Year IV's Concord honor guard and the Crossing.

## Key staff
- Tesfane Okoru: Arms and Field instructor, Eden's Lance supervisor. One-armed (lost at Mansakar's fall), Tenet Stitch.
- Professor Ilse Brandmeier: Spell Grammar, terrifying, brilliant, comic foil.
- Halvard Strent: History of the Breach, vice-headmaster. Hums a lullaby when grading. Never stands directly under lamps (claims a light-sensitivity).

## Year III, PR 142 (Eden's class) key students
- Lance Seven: Eden Hale, Lysette Corvaille, Tobias Fenwick, Niamh Carrow, and one empty slot (Pell Garrow, vanished last term).
- Lance One: Aurelian Valdis (leader), Crown Prince's cousin Countess Margit Adler, and others.
- Rumour mill topics: the vanished students (three since spring), the Black Thirteen, and the sickly Argentine heir nobody has seen.
