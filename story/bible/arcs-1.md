# Arc roadmap, part 1 (arcs 1-7, ch 1-350). Each arc: question, enemy, beats, tactics, win (how he earns it), cost, growth.
Victory rule: Eden is overpowered and the reader trusts he will win. Tension comes from the path (what he must reveal, who he must protect, which rule he must keep) and the price, never from fake last-second rescues. He prepares, plans and strains; wins look hard-earned.

## Arc 1 The Rooftop (1-50) school + terror. Year 5 autumn.
Q: can Eden stay unseen once someone insists on seeing him? Enemy: Ashen Crowns cell, a Veil inside it. See outline/arc1-concept.md.
Tactics: lantern-shadow chase; berserk construct; Ball siege via roof-glass shadows; silent Wall; oil fires for door edges.
Win (47-50): Eden scouted the Mawgate route with Vesk for three nights; he lights the oil lines he laid, loans Wardenshell to Felix (middle name given ch 12) to hold the students, and ends the grafted Crown. The difficulty is doing it without being seen. Cost: Lysette's Witness sees light where he stood; Clara badly wounded; the enemy now knows his mother's name.
Growth: Eden smiles at Lysette once (26). Felix: first relic. Lysette starts doubting her picture of Rowan.

## Arc 2 Lanterns and Ledgers (51-100) economic + court. Year 5 spring.
Q: who profits from the fear? Enemy: the Lanternhouse Compact (Ambrose Kell) selling unstable stones to the Ashen Crowns; the Iron Faction (Archduke Halberg) pushing a war budget.
Beats: Ambrose Kell introduced, smiling, "I always want one more" (55); Silver Lists tourney (60-68; Eden "loses" in the quarterfinal to protect his cover, Valerius furious); a Black Order raid on a Lanternhouse warehouse collides with a Golden Seal raid led by Lysette's mother; Lysette lets Rowan go (90, quietly, in the garden); Hald unlocks (80); Doorstep (70); grafts are Hush flesh (95); Volkhovar money behind Lanternhouse (98).
Tactics: tourney duel reading crowd shadows; warehouse of volatile stones (no fire, doors only); rooftop sniper duel with Hald.
Win (96-100): Kell's stone shipment is set to blow the Founders' Day parade while fake Black Order agents frame Eden. He has already mapped Kell's routes; Felix's Trail confirms the last cart; Eden empties every cart through doors in the parade's banner shadows. Cost: the public blames the Black Order for deaths the Compact caused; Golden Seal gets emergency powers against it.
Growth: Seraphine starts seeing Eden look at Lysette (60). Rowan learns Clara is shielding him from attention.

## Arc 3 The Concord (101-150) foreign politics. Year 6 autumn.
Q: can thirteen nations sit at one table while the Hush waits outside? Enemy: Volkhovar delegation (Dragomira's envoy Belozerov), Aurelis Inquisitor Theodora, Merrow's double game.
Beats: students as honor guard; Han Shuzhen of Qianlu duels Eden in an exhibition and strains him into a real draw (125); Ishani of Saravant drifts into Eden's dream (115); Dragomira's envoy studies Valerius (120); a delegate assassinated, framed on the Black Order (105); Eden learns the Veil-rescue rule and realizes he severed Corentin (105); Rowan's Lirenne blood exposed by Ashen Crowns propaganda (110), Rowan tempted; Rowan finally notices Lysette, too late (130); Lysette's Witness proves Eden is the Black Order and that he killed "Corentin" (140); rupture; Seraphine confesses and is gently refused (145); Second legion (145 sleeping, unused); Merrow is the Regent (150).
Tactics: summit protection detail; poison and counterpoison; funicular duel; a hearing as battlefield.
Win (146-150): Merrow's men seize the Concord hall with delegates inside. Eden could break it open, but that would expose him to thirteen nations; he slips in through Seraphine's Mischief swap (practiced as a prank in ch 10 and 60) and takes the hall in eleven minutes while Lysette, furious, still guards the door for him. Cost: Lysette's trust broken; Valerius learns his own intelligence chief betrayed the crown under his nose.
Growth: Seraphine becomes a diplomat at the table; Valerius learns to ask Eden for help.

## Arc 4 Argentine (151-200) internal coup. Year 6 spring, graduation.
Q: who is Eden when he stops hiding? Enemy: Iron Faction + Ashen Crowns + the Tailor.
Beats: Eden turns 19; Ysolde gives Liora's letter (155); Eden declares himself heir at the Order Council to shield Rowan and Lysette from a show trial (160); the Argentine household's comedy is revealed as a veteran guard who always knew his nights (163); Iron King truth (170); Eden learns he opened the Gate and his mother was a Valkyrie (175); seal breaks, 100 relics (176); Lysandre comes later; Threshold (180); Strent is the Tailor (185); graduation coup (190-199); middle names with Lysette (198); Merrow killed by the Tailor's trap (199); the Tailor destroyed (200).
Tactics: palace coup at night with the lamps cut (no edges: Eden must fight without doors); a hall of mirrors where Candor sees true; the household's kitchen ambush.
Win (195-200): the Tailor holds the king and Lysette and demands the Emperor's essence. Eden walks in already holding Morrowpin (the hairpin from ch 2, a Keeping relic) and locks the Tailor exactly as his mother did; the hard part is facing what it was. Cost: Eden's existence is public; he loses the anonymity he chose; Duke injured (loses two fingers of his sword hand).
Growth: Lysette chooses to stand beside him again, not as lover yet. Eden cries for the first time since age 10 (175).

## Arc 5 The Teeth (201-250) monster war, siege. Graduates posted.
Q: can they hold the narrowest place on Elysmar? Enemy: Hierarch the Tidewright, who drains the strait to make a land bridge.
Beats: Eden posted as a junior knight under the Crimson Rampart; Kirana's fleet guards the strait's sea side; the feather truth (205); kill-order revealed (214), Eden keeps fighting for a king who might kill him; Felix's friendship began as an order from his father (236), bond strained then healed; Sixth Pillar Sefa (240); Clara replaced by a Veil during a night patrol (228, not yet known); Lord Marshal Gideon Valdis dies holding the inner gate (247).
Tactics: falling-tide war; drum-code against Choirs; mining under Mawgates; door-bridges over a drained strait.
Win (245-250): the Tidewright drains the strait for a final push. Eden prepared for a storm-front with Kirana's forecasts; under the clouds' shadow he wakes 10,000 of the First Legion while Valerius' Verdict holds the breach. Victory is certain once he chooses; the cost is that the world sees black knights. Cost: rumours of black knights at the Teeth reach every capital; Gideon's death.
Growth: Valerius takes Gideon's place at the Wall; Rowan learns to command, not shine.

## Arc 6 Across the Middle Sea (251-300) monster campaign + ally politics.
Q: can the south be taken back? Enemy: Hierarch the Ashen Choir; Shah Kaveh's bargain.
Beats: crossing to Okarune; Han Shuzhen's Qianlu corps joins; Mansakar's Prince Dawit; Ezharan cuts the Sandneck supply until Verandel promises well rights; Eden breaks his own rule and kills an unconfirmed suspect to save a convoy (260) and Candor refuses him (until 330); Clara is a Veil (262), imprisoned alive so she can be saved one day; Ashen Crowns under Ilsabet Rorke join the war (270); Armory of a Thousand Names (280, his answer to losing Candor); front stalled by design (285); Void Host revealed when Eden wakes three legions (290); Lysandre (300).
Tactics: desert lancers vs Husk tides; burning grass for noon shadows; relic loans to an army; night siege of a grey city.
Win (295-300): retaking the Crown Well of the Ashen Highlands. Eden is strong enough but overdrawn after the Armory; the Ashen Crowns he spared hold his flank (mercy to Ilsabet, ch 25) and Han Shuzhen raises the causeway Eden asked for in arc 3. Cost: the world now knows Verandel's "hidden order" is one man.
Growth: Rowan grieves Clara without hating; Lysette's Witness becomes a war tool.

## Arc 7 The Void's Son (301-350) legal, religious, economic.
Q: is Eden a saviour or the cause? Enemy: Concord tribunal (Halberg as witness), Aurelis Inquisition, Volkhovar embargo and propaganda.
Beats: Eden summoned to trial at Solmarch; Volkhovar embargo starves the Wall; Inquisitor Theodora investigates fairly; Seraphine defends him as envoy; Valerius stands against his own father's kill-order (330); Candor returns when Eden confesses the rule he broke in open court (330); Kaedros (350); the Emperor gave himself, Eden is not the Emperor (340); king refuses to hand Eden over, risking war (345).
Tactics: evidence duels in court; embargo convoy war; a cathedral assassination.
Win (346-350): the tribunal leans toward "surrender him". Eden refuses to use force on a court; he had asked Theodora to investigate (310); her findings and Lysette's Witness of the Iron King's ritual chamber carry the vote. Cost: Verandel is isolated; Eden's name is feared.
Growth: Eden says "I'm not fine" to Felix's daily question for the first time.
