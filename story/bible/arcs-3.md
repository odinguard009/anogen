# Arc roadmap, part 3: battle mix and aura moments per arc
Style stage per arc (combat-style.md): I arcs 1-2, II arcs 3-5, III arcs 6-7, IV arcs 8-9, V arcs 10-14, VI arcs 15-16 (arcs 13-15 detail: arcs-4.md).
Tags: flex (easy, awe), puzzle (rules, terrain, timing), strained (he must truly try), costly (win with a real loss). Aim per arc about 30/35/25/10. Aura: rare, earned by hiding, always with reaction shots.

## Arc 1 (1-50)
Battles: market Veil hunt 3 flex; berserk construct 5 puzzle; Valerius duel 16-18 strained (holding back is the strain); Ember cell 22 flex; Ball siege vs Crown of Kaldmark 24-26 puzzle; silent Wall 31-34 puzzle; Mawgate night 45-50 costly.
Aura: 25 he walks through the Ball's broken glass and the grafted Embers step back without knowing why (reaction: Seraphine's hand stops shaking). 49 a single look at the dying Crown stops a hostage knife (reaction: Felix sees, says nothing, asks "Eaten today?" the next morning).

## Arc 2 (51-100)
Battles: tourney rounds 60-66 flex (he "loses" on purpose in 67, puzzle); warehouse of volatile stones 78 puzzle; rooftop sniper duel with Hald 84 strained; parade carts 96-100 puzzle; Black Order vs Golden Seal raid 72 costly (public blame).
Aura: 67 he lowers his lance in the quarterfinal and the arena's seasoned scouts go quiet (reaction: Okoru stops chewing). 88 at Kell's dinner he says one quiet sentence and a hired duelist resigns on the spot.

## Arc 3 (101-150)
Battles: delegate assassination chase 105 puzzle; poison night 117 puzzle; Han Shuzhen exhibition 125 strained (draw); funicular duel 136 flex; Concord hall 146-150 flex executed quietly; Lysette's discovery 140 costly (trust).
Aura: 125 the Jade General bows first after the draw (reaction: some delegations clap politely, Dragomira's envoy writes something down, Seraphine whispers "show-off"). 149 Eden walks into the hostage hall and the lamps' shadows stand up; Merrow's men weigh it and most lower their weapons; one does not and is simply disarmed (reaction: a Volkhovar aide, shaken, starts counting shadows on the wall).

## Arc 4 (151-200)
Battles: Iron Faction ambush 158 flex; palace coup with lamps cut 190-194 strained (no doors); hall of mirrors 196 puzzle; the Tailor 195-200 costly (Duke's fingers, his anonymity).
Aura: 160 the declaration: he washes out the dye and gives his full public name at the Order Council (reactions vary: Valerius mutters "finally"; two masters stand, one stays seated in protest at being lied to; Lysette's mother leaves the hall without a word; the Duke cries loudly and is told to stop). 163 the academy finds out who slept on the roof: no gasp, a week of awkwardness, a few classmates angry he hid, Brandmeier asks dryly whether 61 absences count as royal service. 199 the coup's soldiers lower their blades; later several testify they did it because they recognized the old Argent oath, not out of fear.

## Arc 5 (201-250)
Battles: first Husk tide 203 flex; Choir silence 218 puzzle; Mawgate mines 231 puzzle; Tidewright's drained strait 245-250 strained; inner gate 247 costly (Gideon).
Aura: 222 a quiet "Hold." stops a routing company (reaction: veterans obey a nineteen-year-old and only ask why later). 248 ten thousand black knights rise under the storm (reaction: both armies go silent; Valerius laughs once, disbelieving).

## Arc 6 (251-300)
Battles: desert lancers vs Husks 255 flex; convoy 260 costly (broken rule, Candor lost); grey city siege 274 puzzle; Armory 280 flex (an army armed at once); Crown Well of the Ashen Highlands 295-300 strained.
Aura: 280 a thousand relics leave the air and choose hands (reaction: Mansakar's old soldiers sing). 290 three legions wake; the Shah's envoy goes pale and, very carefully, asks for a revised treaty.

## Arc 7 (301-350)
Battles: cathedral assassins 312 flex; embargo convoy 322 puzzle; tribunal as battlefield 340-350 strained (no force allowed); Kaedros' first siege 350 flex.
Aura: 336 in court he answers a threat with silence until the accuser forgets his words (reaction: the scribe's pen keeps scratching in the quiet). 345 the king refuses to hand him over and Eden bows to him for the first time.

## Arc 8 (351-400)
Battles: raft boarding 356 flex; succession hunt on Kirana 370 puzzle; typhoon battle 382 costly (Felix lost); Nihl 395-400 strained.
Aura: 398 he steps between Nihl and the legion; half a million soldiers turn their faceless heads to him (reaction: Kirana mutters a sailor's prayer).

## Arc 9 (401-450)
Battles: weapon-rack duels 412 puzzle; Valkyrie champions 428 strained; trial by ten relics 441-445 costly (memories).
Aura: 425 the Dragon reveals itself as a Sovereign; some Valkyries kneel, Aethra pointedly does not. 444 Aethra's verdict breaks against his still face.

## Arc 10 (451-500)
Battles: condor drops 458 flex; Isthmus Company mercenaries 476 flex; Crown Well cleansing 488 puzzle; Thousand Mouths 496-500 strained.
Aura: 477 he tells the Company's general "Leave"; the man does the arithmetic, withdraws, and files a written protest (reaction: Wayra's riders laugh for an hour).

## Arc 11 (501-550)
Battles: dream war 505-510 puzzle; Nihl's test 515 strained; shadowless cathedral 546-550 strained; Frost Hand succession hunt 532 flex.
Aura: 520 Rowan's Ashkar and Eden face each other and the crusade's sun dims (reaction: the Luminarch sits down on the cathedral steps, an old man who has just lost his god's voice). 541 at Dragomira's death he closes her eyes; Volkhovar's guards lower spears.

## Arc 12 (551-600)
Battles: seven Hierarch fronts 561-584 (two flex, three puzzle, two strained); Valerius under Vehra 585 strained (he must hold back); polar night 595-600 costly (Frost fever).
Aura: 560 the last four legions rise (reaction: eleven flags dip, Volkhovar's does not; Han's stone arm creaks as he salutes). 592 he and Lysette open the tenth floor and every host in the camp feels it.

## Arc 16 (751-800)
Battles: Vehra's threads 770 strained; Kaurveth's body 785-790 strained; finale 795 costly (the Door given up).
Aura: 790 he speaks to the god of Want as an equal and the Hush goes still (reaction: Kell weeps without knowing why). 798 no aura, by design: the vow is quiet.

## Arc 17 (801-850)
Battles: leftover Husks at the festival 817 flex (one sweep, students cheer).
Aura: 805 first lecture: three hundred students go silent before he says a word, then he falls asleep at the lectern (reaction: Lysette writes him up).
