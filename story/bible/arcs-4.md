# Arc roadmap, part 4 (arcs 13-15, ch 601-750): room for titans, retainers, the Ledger and the Void
Same rules: Eden wins; tension is path and price; battle mix ~30/35/25/10; 2-4 aura moments; a light chapter every 5-8; every fight debuts a trick. Adjustable: these arcs can shrink or grow.

## Arc 13 Titanfall (601-650) monster war on every continent, retainer-led fronts.
Q: what happens when the Wound answers back? At the pole camp, Lament's fall cracks the Wound wider; three Titans and dozens of Colossi break north at once toward Kesharra, Vastlund and Jayavara. The coalition must split.
Beats: the Ten Retainers each lead a front beside a nation (teamwork arc): Ombrevar with Volkhovar's Ice Legions, Kaedros with Han's Qianlu corps, Hald with Norhaven griffons, Oriane at Concord command, Sefa linking all fronts by shadow, Nihl at Eden's back. Gravemother rises under Jayavara (615-620, with Kirana); Cinderback burns the Endless Grass (628-632, Orduhan cavalry); the Weeping Crown walks on Norhaven (642-646). Felix commands scouts on three continents; Seraphine runs the Titan Accord as Speaker. Valerius, Vehra quiet inside him, holds the Teeth again.
Battles: 607 flex (Colossus swept off a cliff by one door); 618 strained (Gravemother); 624 puzzle (two fronts at once via Sefa); 631 costly (Cinderback burns a refugee steppe city; Eden saves the people, not the city); 645 strained (Weeping Crown); others flex/puzzle by rhythm.
Aura: 620 Gravemother sinks and every Jayavara ship's bell rings without hands (reaction: Kirana salutes him like a sailor, not an admiral). 646 the Thousandfold Knight lifts the Weeping Crown's ruined city off its back and sets it down whole (reaction: Norhaven's Chancellor takes off his hat).
Armor: Aster Garden stage first full war use 605; Redwall and Verdict hold the Teeth 636; Stonewarden vs Cinderback 630. Tricks 59-62.
Romance: Eden and Lysette, engaged, keep missing each other across fronts: letters, and one stolen hour on a griffon at 633 (smile). Felix sends Seraphine a bun by griffon (comedy, 626). Ishani keeps Valerius awake in his dreams so Vehra cannot speak (640).
Light chapters: 604 (retainers argue over who gets which front), 611, 622 (Gorrath adopts a war orphan's kitten), 637, 648.
Win (646-650): every front holds because each retainer and host was placed by Eden's plan weeks before. Cost: Sefa's link burns his maps (he loses his memory of the Void's roads); the world now knows each retainer by name.
Resolution: three titans fall, every front holds, the retainers become public heroes.
Hook: Belozerov seizes Volkhovar with the Ledger of Souls.

## Arc 14 The Ledger of Souls (651-700) political-divine thriller.
Q: who owns a person: a ledger, a god, or themselves? After Dragomira's death, spymaster Belozerov seizes Volkhovar with the Ledger of Souls: Vehra's threads still run through every name written in it, and with them he can puppet soldiers. Kell, host of Want, is courted by Kaurveth's whisper and by Belozerov's money.
Beats: Belozerov's coup in Kremhold (655); Volkhovar's puppeted Ice Legions march on Qianlu (662); Valerius discovers Vehra's threads answer his Verdict too and must refuse to use them (670); Ishani's dream war across a whole army (675-680); Kell betrays the coalition's titan-route maps for a want he cannot name, then turns back (685, his choice); Eden enters Kremhold alone, unarmored, to burn the Ledger (690-695); Volkhovar's people read their own names aloud in the square (700).
Battles: 658 puzzle (escape from a city where anyone could be a puppet); 668 flex (Eden stops a puppeted cavalry charge without hurting a rider); 684 costly (Kell's betrayal costs the Skyspine depot); 693 strained (Belozerov wearing Vehra's last threads like armor).
Aura: 694 Eden walks into the throne hall without armor; Belozerov's puppets let go of their weapons one by one as he passes (reaction: an old Volkhovar general starts to cry and doesn't know why). 700 the square of names.
Armor: Frostplate turned against them 662; Dawnglass loaned to Lysette again to read puppet threads 676; Firstplate worn by Nihl as an ally 690. Tricks 63-65.
Romance: Valerius tells Ishani that if Vehra takes him she must leave; she refuses with a drawing (672, ache then smile). Eden and Lysette set a wedding date and argue about the roof garden's capacity (comedy, 666). Felix and Seraphine fight their first real argument and make up over soup (681).
Light chapters: 653, 661 (the Argentine household "invades" the capital to help plan weddings), 666, 679, 688, 697.
Win: names are spoken back to their owners. Cost: Valerius is scarred by Vehra; Volkhovar has no ruler (Seraphine helps it choose a regency council).
Resolution: the Ledger burned, names returned, Kell turns back, Volkhovar chooses a regency council.
Hook: to close the Wound from both sides Eden must walk the Void Road to the Throne of Nothing.

## Arc 15 The Void Road (701-750) divine journey, the retainers' arc.
Q: what was the Emperor's court before it slept in a boy? To close the Wound from both sides, Eden must reach the Throne of Nothing through the Void. Only the Ten know the road.
Beats: each retainer's past in the Void (one or two chapters each, a ladder of memories); Nihl's history with Melchsee and the Iron King's test (710); the Tutor shows the Throne for the first time (720); the chains that hold Kaurveth's will in Kell, seen from the other side (728); Sepiroth and the Tutor finally speak face to face in the Void, a duel of words that becomes a truce (735, big comedy-drama); Lysette's Seedtime plants the first growing thing the Void has ever held (742); the Ten swear to Eden, not to Melchsee (748).
Battles: 705 puzzle (fighting where there are no shadows because everything is shadow; doors need light, so Rowan's sun comes too); 716 strained (Kept Knights of the Void, the Emperor's former enemies); 731 flex (Ten Retainers, Open House rehearsal); 744 costly (Gorrath holds a collapsing road and is left asleep in the Void; woken in the finale).
Aura: 720 the Throne seen; 748 ten oaths in a silence older than worlds.
Armor: Nullcrown and Aster merge further 721; Wingwake in the Void (Lysette flies where nothing is) 742. Tricks 66-68.
Romance: in the Void Eden admits he is afraid he will become the Emperor; Lysette says she will drag him back by the collar, as always (726, smile). Valerius and Ishani dance in a dream at the edge of the Void (737). Felix stays behind to guard the gate and writes Seraphine a list of things he likes about her (number 41 is "the jokes") (733).
Light chapters: 703 (packing for the Void), 712 (Vesk's tour of the Void's gossip), 724, 738, 746.
Win: the road is open and the Throne is in reach. Cost: Gorrath left asleep (returns 790); Sefa's lost roads.
Resolution: the road is open, the Ten swear to Eden, Sepiroth and the Tutor make peace.
Hook: the Throne is in sight, Kaurveth's body wakes, and Gorrath is left asleep on the road.
