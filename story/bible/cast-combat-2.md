# Cast combat styles, part 2: elders, household, allies, rivals, antagonists (reference)

## Duke Aldous Argentine: "the Lake Freezes" (Sentence, Vowsilver)
Greatsword used one-handed. Loose, sloppy-looking stance until he names a target and a deadline; then very still, very fast, every cut at the hilt or the joint. Rhythm: a long pause and then one sentence. Signatures: Sentence (target and deadline; his blade will not miss before it), Ninth Bell (many targets, one deadline, 193). Weakness: a deadline he cannot meet costs him for days; after 200 he lacks two fingers and must adapt grips. Growth: comic dad (1-190); showpiece (193); fights left-handed-heavy (200+); with Eden back to back (601-650). Spotlight: 193-194; 251 (the tent); 640 holds a pass while Eden fights a titan.

## King Theodric IV: "the Last to Draw" (Patience, Kingsrest)
A plain grey sword that grows heavy for whoever attacks him. Stands still and lets enemies tire. Rhythm: one step for every ten of theirs. Weakness: old injuries, a king's duty not to fight. Spotlight: 345 draws for the first time in nineteen years to refuse handing Eden over; 694 stands in Kremhold's gate while Eden goes in.

## Dame Hildegard Ostwald: "the Bell Warden"
Half-blind; fights by sound with a bronze staff-bell. Every strike rings; she hears where the echo bends around bodies. Spotlight: 194 holds the academy gate during the coup with first-years behind her.

## Tesfane Okoru: "One Hand, Many Knots" (Stitch)
One-armed; Stitch binds wounds, ropes, and enemy limbs. Wrestler's patience. Spotlight: 33 teaches the Lance to fight in silence; 296 in his homeland's highlands with Prince Dawit.

## Ysolde Lisle
Never fights in public; two throwing quills and an eye for exits. Spotlight: 192 escorts the king through servants' passages in the dark coup.

## Bertram Quell and Greta Vos
Bertram: teaspoon, garrotte, and silence; a door opens and someone is simply gone. Greta: ladle and shield-captain's footwork; holds kitchens like keeps. Spotlight: 163 kitchen ambush; 661 the household "invades" the capital.

## The Tutor (Melchsee's echo)
Never fights Eden for real; spars on the board's edge with a single shadow-cane, showing the form Eden needs next. Rhythm of a chess master. Spotlight: 720 shows Eden the Throne by fighting him one last time.

## Ten retainers: see combat-style.md. Gorrath (tower shield, voices) gets spotlights at 23, 622 and 744 (holds a collapsing road alone).

## Admiral Kirana (Thalos): "Tidecall"
Curved sea-blade and command of currents and storms. Wide sailor's stance on a deck that never stays still. Tactics: makes the sea choose sides. Weakness: sickens away from salt water. With Eden: storm shadows for his doors (248), troughs for doors (356, 618). Spotlight: 370 survives a succession hunt by drowning the assassins' boat; 618 Gravemother.

## Han Shuzhen (Kaelthos): "the Jade General"
Glaive and Living Mountain: walls, roads, fortresses in an hour. Planted, unhurried, grandfatherly. Weakness: his body turns to stone. Against Eden: 125 exhibition duel, a true draw. With Eden: causeways and anvils (300, 630). Spotlight: 575 Kaelthos speaks; 630 Stonewarden.

## Empress Dragomira (Vehra): "Loom of Days"
Never draws a weapon; she tugs fate-threads so ambushes arrive late and bridges crack on cue. Rhythm: chess played on time itself. Weakness: each great pull ages her. Credible motive: she believes order prevents famine and war, and she has seen both. Spotlight: 530 her duel of threads with Ishani's dreams; 540 her last free act.

## Nihl, the First
Eden's style mirrored and darker (combat-style.md). Spotlight: 398 foe; 548 ally, back to back.

## Count Arkady Belozerov
Spymaster; a cane-sword and the Ledger's threads (651-700). Fights only cornered; prefers to make others fight. Motive: he loved the Empress and thinks the world killed her. Spotlight: 693.

## Ambrose Kell (Kaurveth's will)
No style; people simply give him what he wants, including openings. Weakness: he can never be satisfied. Spotlight: 685 his betrayal and turn; 790 he gives a god an ending.

## Ilsabet Rorke, Crown of Kaldmark
Ice-axe and Ashmail; a giant who fights fair. Spotlight: 25 vs Eden (spared); 270-296 holds Eden's flank; Clara removes her graft at 482.

## Valkyrie Marshal Aethra
Spear-wing and Heaven Gate drills; flawless and joyless. Credible motive: duty to a law older than worlds. Spotlight: 428 her champions; 444 she yields and becomes Lysette's flight teacher.

## Halvard Strent, the Tailor
Fights by being someone else: borrowed faces, borrowed skills, grey threads. Spotlight: 195-200.

## Count Lucan Merrow; Archduke Halberg
Merrow: never fights; a duel-trained diplomat with a hidden stiletto. Halberg: old-school knight, brave, wrong. Both have real grievances or convictions; neither sneers.
