# Comedy plan (reference)

## Rhythm
- A light chapter (type: comic or slice-of-life) every 5-8 chapters, plus small jokes inside serious chapters. Never inside a key emotional or tense scene; a light chapter may follow a heavy one to let the reader breathe.
- Each arc has 6-8 light chapters within its 50. Arc 17 (801-850) is a warm slice-of-life finale arc with three weddings.

## Recurring comic engines
- The Argentine household: the Duke's disguises and ballads, Bertram and the Duke's tea war, Greta's ladle, Pim and Odessa's gossip intelligence.
- Discipline Committee raids: Lysette's attendance warrants, Eden's legal loopholes, the roof as contested territory.
- The Rooftop Garden Club: grows from one member to a ridiculous club (Felix, Seraphine, later Ishani) arguing over tomatoes.
- Seraphine's mission to make Felix laugh; Felix's literal answers.
- Ishani's dream pranks on Valerius; his formal complaints and memos.
- Dragon vs Tutor bickering through Eden; Gorrath's thirteen voices; Form 13-B; Brandmeier's terror; Han's grandfather jokes; Kirana's sailor bluntness; the cooking club's explosions.
