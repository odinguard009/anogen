# Continents of Elysmar (reference)

Elysmar is three times Earth's surface area; every continent is roughly three times its Earth analog. Layout is Earth's map mirrored east-west: the Asia analog lies west of the Europe analog, the Americas analog lies east across an ocean. Halveren stays in the north-centre. (Owner assumption flagged in proposals.md.)

Rough map, west to east:
Akizane isles | Kesharra (Qianlu far west, Orduhan centre, Volkhovar north, Saravant south, Jayavara isles south-west) | Ezharan | HALVEREN (Skarnheim north, Verandel, Aurelis south, Brennoch isles off the east coast) | Grey Gulf ocean | Vastlund
South of Halveren: Middle Sea, then Okarune. South-west: Ruhnmara. South-east: Selvara. Bottom: Glaceren.

## Halveren (Europe analog), north-centre
- Temperate forests, wine valleys, high alps (the Crownspine), northern fjords. Verandel covers about 90%. Skarnheim holds the northern peninsula; Brennoch the isles off the east coast facing the Grey Gulf; the Aurelis See is a walled city-state in the south.
- South coast faces the Middle Sea. The Teeth: a 14 km strait at the EAST end of the Middle Sea, where it meets the Grey Gulf, between Halveren and Okarune; held by the Southern Wall. At the WEST end a land bridge (the Sandneck) joins Okarune to Ezharan.
- Invasion: 0%. Grey dust arrives on southern winds; refugees everywhere.

## Kesharra (Asia analog), west and largest
- Steppe heart (the Endless Grass), frozen north (the Whitewood taiga), great river basins and jade mountains in the far west, monsoon south, island chains in the south-west.
- Invasion: about 8%, all on the south-west islands and coasts facing Ruhnmara. Jayavara is the front line there.

## Vastlund (North America analog), east across the Grey Gulf
- Prairies, red canyons, cold north, a long western isthmus pointing toward Selvara. Its far north-east horn (the Alaska analog, called Frostreach) nearly touches Volkhovar across the Narrow Ice on the other side of the globe.
- Invasion: about 5%, the southern isthmus. Norhaven holds the Isthmus Line.

## Okarune (Africa analog), south of Halveren
- Was savanna, deserts, rainforest, great lakes. Now grey nearly everywhere.
- Invasion: about 97%. Survivors hold only the Ashen Highlands (Mansakar's mountain fortress-plateau in the north-west, near the Sandneck) and a few walled ports on the Middle Sea.

## Selvara (South America analog), south-east
- Was rainforest and a long mountain spine (the Skyspine).
- Invasion: about 98%. Tawanqar holds the highest terraces of the Skyspine, supplied by Norhaven airships of griffons.

## Ruhnmara (Australia analog), south-west
- Was red desert, reefs, eucalyptus-like silver forests. Its union of city-states fell completely by PR 61, the slow era before the Wound widened.
- Invasion: 100%. A grey continent; the Hush's second staging ground.

## Glaceren (Antarctica analog), south pole
- Ice over black rock. Site of the Emperor's last duel with the Sepiroth Dragon and the First Breach (the Wound).
- Invasion: 100% since PR 0. Nobody has returned from it.

## Oceans
- Grey Gulf (Atlantic analog) between Halveren and Vastlund. Middle Sea (Mediterranean analog) between Halveren and Okarune. The Wide Water (Pacific analog) on the far side. The southern seas below about 45 degrees south are Pale (still, grey, silent).
- Coverage math: southern continents plus Pale seas equal about 30% of the planet's surface.
