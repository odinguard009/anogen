# Throne-bearers: the gods' hosts (reference)

## How a Throne works (original mechanics)
- Stages: Dormant (the host's own Tenet bends toward the god's domain; nobody suspects), Stirring (dreams, domain accidents), Awakened (a world-class domain power), Enthroned (the god speaks and can lend its full domain in a city-wide radius for minutes; rare, catastrophic).
- Every Throne has a Toll that grows with use, tied to its domain.
- On a host's death the god passes to the nearest resonant mortal (Tenet theme close to the domain). States therefore run "succession hunts": tracking hosts, grooming candidates, and staging deaths near a prepared successor. This is the darkest politics on Elysmar.
- A host is not possessed. Will against will: strong hosts master their god; weak hosts are steered.
- Known Thrones are strategic deterrents, like fortresses that walk.

## The hosts
1. **Eden Aster Argentine** (Verandel, 18). Ormath (by gift, unique) + Seraiel (Gate, passed from his mother Liora, its previous host, at her death). Melsche Door and Sepiroth Gate. Toll: the Frost; core drain. Known: see secrets.md. The only person ever to hold two.
2. **Empress Dragomira** (Volkhovar, 44). Vehra. Power: Loom of Days (sees fate-threads across a city and tugs them: an ambush arrives late, a bridge cracks at the right second). Toll: she ages a season per great pull; looks sixty. Known to her inner court as her "Tenet"; the world calls her the Fate-Empress. Volkhovar's No. 1 rank rests on her and the Ice Legions. Antagonist. Vehra discards her at ch 540 (she dies refusing Vehra's last order: her one free act) and jumps to Valerius (545), whose Verdict resonates with Order.
3. **Han Shuzhen** (Qianlu, 52), the Jade General. Kaelthos. Power: Living Mountain (raises walls, roads and fortresses from stone in an hour; reshapes a battlefield). Toll: his body turns to stone (left arm already jade-grey). Known worldwide; Qianlu's deterrent. Rival who can genuinely strain Eden (duel ch 125: Eden must open real doors and still only draws). Ally from arc 6, friend by arc 12. Kaelthos speaks through him ch 575.
4. **Admiral Kirana** (Jayavara, 31). Thalos. Power: Tidecall (currents, storms and the deep's refusal over a sea region). Toll: a day away from salt water and she sickens. Known; Jayavara's front holds because of her. Volkhovar and the Isthmus Company each try to buy or capture her (arc 8). Big-sister ally to Eden and Lysette.
5. **Ishani** (Saravant, 20), a court astrologer's daughter. Niveth. Power: Sleepwalk (enter and reshape the dreams of everyone sleeping within sight; can put an army to sleep). Toll: she can never sleep or dream herself. Secret; Saravant's Maharaja keeps her as a hidden deterrent. She has wandered into Eden's dream rooms since the Concord (ch 115). Trickster, ally with a price. Reveal ch 505.
6. **Rowan Tallis** (Verandel/Lirenne, 18). Ashkar. Dormant as Dawnbreak. Awakened ch 470: Noonbringer (a sun-disc that burns the Pale clean in a valley). Toll: he burns his own warmth and memory of the night. Aurelis wants Ashkar "freed" (Rowan dead near a prepared priest); Eden protects him. Ashkar himself denounces the crusade through Rowan (ch 520).
7. **Clara Belmont** (Verandel, 18). Myrrhen. Dormant as her healing Tenet (wilted things revive near her). The Tailor's Veil takes her shadow (ch 228) because Kaurveth wants the Wellmother's will in the Hush. Rescued ch 480; awakened as Wellspring (mends land, wells and bodies; first green grass in the Pale, ch 498). Toll: she feels every wound she heals.
8. **Ambrose Kell** (Norhaven-born arms dealer, 41). Kaurveth's will. Power never named: whatever he wants, people want to give him. Toll: he can never be satisfied. Hidden even from himself until the Tailor whispers it (arc 2). Antagonist with a pitiable core; revealed ch 560. At the end Kaurveth is given an ending through him; Kell survives, emptied, ordinary, finally content (ch 640).
9. **Lysette Maren Hollen** (Verandel, 18). Aurelune. Dormant as Witness (Beginning remembers where everything started). Liora saved her as a dying infant because the Dragon told her the child carried the Gardener. Awakens ch 580 as Seedtime (makes a new start possible: grows a beginning where something ended). Toll: whatever begins, she must let go of. Reveal ch 580; with Eden (Ending, Keeping) she closes the Wound (645).
10. **Valerius Aurenmark** (from ch 545). Vehra, after Dragomira. He fights her will from inside: his Verdict versus her Loom. Strains Eden genuinely (arc 12 duel at the pole's edge). Masters her by ch 600 and binds her to time without power over mortals (643).

## Who knows (at series start)
Han, Kirana, Dragomira: their powers are world-famous; that gods are inside them, nobody but Dragomira (and she believes Vehra serves her). Ishani: Saravant's Maharaja. Others: nobody.

Arc 9 Heaven Gate (Seraiel's court, Valkyries); arc 10 (Ashkar and Myrrhen wake); arc 11 The Ten Thrones (hosts gather, succession hunts, Vehra's moves, crusade); arc 13 finale (Vehra in Valerius, Kaurveth's body and will).
