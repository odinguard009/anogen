# The Ten Sovereigns (creator gods; lore reference). Hosts: gods-hosts.md. Reveals from ch 425 on.

## The Sepiroth
Before worlds, ten Sovereigns stood in a covenant called the Sepiroth: a tree of ten domains, each a root of reality. Together they made worlds. The Sepiroth Vault and the Sepiroth Dragon carry its name because the Dragon kept the covenant's records. Mortals remember none of this; the Lumen faith remembers one god (Ashkar).

## The Ten (domain; what they made; where body and will are now)
1. **Ormath, the Emperor of Void.** Ending. Made the Void, the boundary that lets things finish. Body: the Void and the Throne of Nothing. Will: never bound; gave itself to unborn Eden (PR 93).
2. **Seraiel, the Sepiroth Dragon.** Keeping. Made memory, Heaven Gate and the Vault; Valkyries are its knights. Will: bound to the Vault's gate, which passes down the Valkyrie gatekeepers; now Eden's Sepiroth Gate.
3. **Myrrhen, the Wellmother.** Flow. Made life-current. Dissolved her body into Elysmar as mana so mortals could make (Tenets). The Refusal is her body's instinct. Will: bound in a mortal line (now Clara Belmont).
4. **Kaelthos, the Forgefather.** Form. Made matter and bodies. Body: Halveren's Crownspine. Will: Han Shuzhen of Qianlu.
5. **Vehra, the Weaver.** Order. Made law, time, fate-threads. Designed the Binding. Fears mortals who can rewrite laws (the Sovereign Tier). Will: Empress Dragomira of Volkhovar; jumps to Valerius (ch 545).
6. **Ashkar, the First Flame.** Light. Made sun and sight. Will: Rowan Tallis (Lirenne line). The Luminarch's "Ashkar" oracle is Vehra in disguise.
7. **Niveth, the Dreamer.** Mind. Made thought and dreams; Eden's dream rooms exist in her domain. Will: Ishani of Saravant.
8. **Thalos, the Tide.** Sea and weather. Deep water refuses the Hush (why deep sea breaks veins). Will: Admiral Kirana of Jayavara.
9. **Aurelune, the Gardener.** Beginning. Made the first garden; left a seed "for when an ending and a keeping agree". Will: Lysette Hollen (dormant).
10. **Kaurveth, the Starving King.** Want. Made desire; his want became hunger and he devoured worlds (the Devouring). Body: imprisoned beneath the Void as the Hush, driven by Hierarchs. Will: bound in a mortal line, now Ambrose Kell. Body and will reunited on the empty throne = every world eaten.

## The Binding of Thrones (about 3,000 years before PR)
After the Devouring and the long war, Vehra wove a treaty: every god's will is sealed into a mortal soul on Elysmar (the world Myrrhen had made of herself) and passes on the host's death to a resonant mortal. Gods may act only through mortal hands and consent. A god inside a person is a **Throne**; the host is a **Throne-bearer**. Exceptions: Ormath stayed free as the lid over the Hush; Seraiel was bound to a gate, not a person. Vehra meant the Binding as a leash. She did not foresee mortals mastering their gods.

## Why Ormath and Seraiel are enemies, and why they share Eden
After the Devouring, Ormath wanted the eaten worlds' remnants ended (mercy); Seraiel wanted them kept (memory). War across worlds. They coexist in Eden because they occupy different rooms of him: Ormath's essence is the outer wall that wraps his soul against the Refusal; Seraiel lives behind the Gate, a door inside. Eden's own soul is the hall between them. They can only reach each other through him (the source of their bickering, and of the dream geography: the Tutor's board at the edge of nothing, the Dragon at the Vault gate).

## Why the Breach
Ormath's weariness thinned the throne (PR 0); Kaurveth's body pushed up at the pole, where Heaven Gate's nearness thins the sky. Vehra, through Volkhovar's oracle line, gave the Iron King the Rite (PR 93) to drag Ormath off his throne: with Ending gone and Keeping hidden, she could reweave the world without mortal will.

## Why the Refusal rejects Valkyries and Void-born
Mana is Myrrhen's body; it accepts only what she birthed. Valkyries and Void-born are foreign. Eden lives because Void wraps him (absence cannot be refused). At the end Myrrhen accepts him (ch 645).
