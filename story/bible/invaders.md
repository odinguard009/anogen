# Invaders: the Hushborn (reference)

## The Hush
- Beneath the Void lies the Hush: sediment of everything the Void unmade but never finished erasing. Grey, soundless, starving remnants of dead worlds.
- The Emperor of Void's throne pressed the Hush down. Over aeons he grew weary; his weight thinned. In PR 0 the floor cracked at Elysmar's south pole (the Wound), because Heaven Gate's nearness made Elysmar's sky "thin" there. The Hush rose: the First Breach.
- PR 93: the Iron King's summoning dragged the Emperor across, through the Hush. The channel tore the Wound wider. Since then the Hush moves several times faster. Okarune and Selvara fell in those 19 years.
- Hushborn eat mana and sound. Where they feed, land turns grey (the Pale), rivers still, birds drop silent.

## Why they come
1. Hunger, and the Refusal: Elysmar's mana unmakes foreign beings. Hushborn survive only by draining land until it can no longer refuse. They must keep eating.
2. The throne: Kaurveth, the Starving King, wants to sit on the empty Throne of Nothing. He needs the Crown of Nothing, the Emperor's essence, which now wraps Eden. With it, the Hush would pour into every world.
3. So they hunt the vessel. Veils are scouts. The front deliberately stalls before Verandel: Kaurveth wants the essence intact, and the Hush fears the Void it was crushed under (reveal ch 230).

## Spread
Through mana veins in rock, like frost through cracks. Fast on land, very slow over deep ocean. Land bridges and straits are where wars are fought.

## Hierarchy (size classes and named titans: titans.md)
- Husks: dog-to-horse sized, grey, many-jointed, eyeless swarmers.
- Stalkers: tall, thin ambushers that lie flat as a shadow on stone.
- Choirs: bloated nodes that "sing" silence over a battlefield, killing voice commands and spell chants. Kill the Choir and sound returns in a rush.
- Mawgates: living tunnels that move Husks under walls.
- Veils: infiltrators.
- Hierarchs: generals with human-level minds and a theme borrowed from a dead world. Seven known.
- Kaurveth: never seen north of the pole. Secretly the fallen tenth Sovereign, god of Want (gods.md; reveal ch 440). The Hush is his body; his will is bound in a mortal, Ambrose Kell (reveal ch 560).

## Veils
- Drink a person's shadow (mana silhouette) over one night. The victim falls into the Hush, conscious; the body turns to grey dust by dawn.
- The Veil gains face, voice, recent memories, skills, mana signature. Old feelings come through flat (facts of love, not the feeling).
- Tells: shadow does not move with breath (Melsche Door senses it at a glance); true grey form in Candor's light; cannot speak the victim's middle name; animals that loved the victim avoid it.
- Victims stay tethered while their Veil lives. Killing the Veil with an ordinary blade severs them forever. Killing it with a relic while speaking the victim's middle name, from inside the Hush, could pull them back. Eden learns this at ch 105 and realizes he severed Corentin Hollen in PR 110 because he never knew his middle name. Clara Belmont, replaced ch 228 (revealed 262), is kept tethered by imprisoning her Veil alive and pulled back at ch 480. Corentin cannot be saved.
- Grey Grafts (ashen-crowns.md) are slivers of Veil flesh; grafted humans slowly become Hushborn.
- The Tailor: Kaurveth's spymaster, oldest Veil in Halveren, recorded since PR 40. Wore the Argentine physician Aubrec Wendell to kill Liora (PR 104); now wears vice-headmaster Halvard Strent. Sponsors the Ashen Crowns.
