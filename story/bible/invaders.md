# Invaders: the Hushborn (reference)

## The Hush
- Beneath the Void (the nothing between worlds) lies the Hush: the sediment of everything the Void unmade but could not finish erasing. Grey, soundless, starving remnants of dead worlds and dead things.
- For ages the Emperor of Void sat on the Throne of Nothing, and its weight pressed the Hush down. When he abdicated (chose to die at Elysmar's south pole, PR 0) the pressure lifted at that exact point. The Hush rose through the crack: the First Breach.
- The Hushborn eat mana and sound. Where they feed, land turns grey (the Pale), rivers still, birds drop silent.

## Why they come
1. Hunger: Elysmar is mana-rich; the Hush is starving.
2. The throne: their sovereign, Kaurveth the Starving King, wants to sit on the empty Throne of Nothing. To do so he needs the Crown of Nothing, which went with the Emperor's soul into the Vault and then into Eden. Whoever holds the throne decides what the Void unmakes; the Hush would pour into every world.
3. So they search for the vessel. Veils are scouts. The front deliberately stalls before Verandel because Kaurveth suspects the vessel is there and wants it alive (reveal ch 230).

## How they spread
Through mana veins in rock, like frost through cracks. Fast on land (a few km a week when pushing), very slow over deep ocean. Land bridges and narrow straits are where wars are fought.

## Hierarchy
- Husks: dog-to-horse sized, grey, many-jointed, eyeless; they swarm and drink. Weak alone.
- Stalkers: tall, thin, ambushers; can lie flat as a shadow on stone.
- Choirs: bloated nodes that "sing" silence over a battlefield, cutting voice commands and spell chants. Kill the Choir and sound returns in a rush.
- Mawgates: living tunnels that move Husks under walls.
- Veils: infiltrators (below).
- Hierarchs: generals with human-level minds, each with a "borrowed" theme from a world the Void ate. Seven known by the Concord.
- Kaurveth, the Starving King: never seen north of the pole.

## Veils (infiltrators)
- Drink a person's shadow (their mana silhouette) over one night, usually in sleep. The person falls into the Hush, conscious, in grey limbo. The body turns to fine grey dust by dawn.
- The Veil gains face, voice, recent memories, skills and mana signature. Old emotional memories come through weak and flat (they remember facts of love, not the feeling).
- Tells: its shadow does not move with its breath (only Melsche Door senses this at a glance); in Candor's light it shows true grey form; it cannot speak its victim's middle name; animals that loved the victim avoid it.
- A Veil can wear one face for years. Some climb high: ministers, officers, teachers.
- Victims stay tethered to the Hush while their Veil lives. Killing the Veil with an ordinary blade severs them forever. Killing it with a relic while speaking the victim's middle name, from inside the Hush, can pull them back. Eden learns this from the Dragon at ch 110 (so he imprisons Niamh's Veil instead of killing it) and realizes he severed Corentin Corvaille because he never knew his middle name. Rescue attempted ch 330.
- The Tailor: the oldest Veil in Verandel, Kaurveth's spymaster. Has worn many faces since PR 40. Killed Liora wearing the face of the Argentine physician Aubrec Wendell; now wears Halvard Strent.
