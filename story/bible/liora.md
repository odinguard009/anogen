# Liora: the Valkyrie smith (reference)

## Who she was
- A Valkyrie of Heaven Gate and the Vault's gatekeeper: the host of Seraiel, the Sepiroth Dragon, before Eden. Seraiel's will is bound to the Vault's gate, and the gate passes down the Valkyrie gatekeepers. Eden had partial access by blood (that is how he opened it at 10); at her death Seraiel passed to him fully.
- The greatest smith who ever lived. In the Vault she spent centuries on the Mending: repairing nearly every broken holy weapon kept there. Relics trust Eden because they remember her hands.
- She and the Dragon were old friends who argued like a married couple about everything except metal. She called it "old lizard". It called her "the only thief I ever let in". It still cannot say her name without stopping.
- On Elysmar (PR 83-104) she forged for the two men who would face the Iron King and the Emperor: her works let Aldous and Theodric stand even against a god in PR 93.

## Her craft: Keepforging
- Materials: starsteel (sky-iron that fell from Heaven Gate), vein-stone from Elysmar's wells, her own moulted feathers, and memory.
- Technique: Keepforging. With each fold of the metal she folds in a Keep: a moment of her voice, a hum, a sentence, held by her Keeping art. A Keep plays when the right person wears or holds the work under the right condition. Spending a work's stored light spends its Keeps; a spent Keep is gone forever.
- Signature (maker's marks): a tiny feather stamped inside the left gauntlet or under the grip; four notes of her lullaby engraved where only the wearer's skin touches; a hair-thin silver vein running like a feather's shaft through every plate.
- Her works do not Toll and never Keep their wearers: she made them to keep people alive, not to keep them. Their cost is the stored light (and the Keeps that go with it).

## Named works (appearance)
1. **Aster** (Eden's armor; see armor.md). Forged before he was born to grow with him; sealed black by her Keeping at her death, so it looks like the Faceless Mantle. Evolves in stages (175, 199, 400, 446, 590, 645). Keeps hold messages to Eden.
2. **Vowsilver** (Duke Aldous's armor). Silver plate, lake-blue enamel, feather veins. Amplifies his Tenet Sentence. Holds Keeps of her voice for him alone. Showpiece ch 193-194.
3. **Patience** and **Kingsrest** (King Theodric's sword and mail). A plain grey sword that grows heavier for whoever attacks its bearer; mail that never rusts. Seen ch 345 when the king refuses to hand Eden over.
4. **Wingwake**: her own Valkyrie plate, forged in Heaven Gate. Found ch 425; worn by Lysette ch 446.
5. **Candor**: she re-forged its broken blade before Eden was born and asked it to choose her son (reveal ch 176).
6. **Wardenshell** and **Morrowpin**: Vault relics she mended; her feather mark inside (Felix notices it ch 47; nobody thinks about it).
7. **The Glasshouse Forge**: her hidden forge beneath the Silverfell glasshouse where she died. Found ch 176. Inside: tools still warm with Keeping, an unfinished sword ("Last Light", finished by Kaelthos and Han, ch 575), and two plain rings in a box labelled "Not yet. For whoever he loves." Payoff: proposal ch 600.
8. **Letters**: the letter Ysolde keeps (155); the Keeps inside Aster and Vowsilver.

## Her death, reconciled
She sealed her wings for 21 years to slow the Refusal; hosting Seraiel and making the Mending's last repairs cost her more. At Eden's 10th birthday-gift night she unsealed her wings to fight the Tailor, sealed Eden (Gate, Door, memory, and Aster), and the Refusal took her. Seraiel passed to Eden. Vowsilver was hanging in the glasshouse; the Duke's left gauntlet still holds the last Keep she made that night.

## Aster's messages (Keeps)
- 176 her humming, the first time the helm opens.
- 205 a sentence under the pauldron: "If you find her, the girl with my feather, be gentle. She was so small."
- 400 to the Emperor: "Thank you for my son. Don't you dare take him back."
- 446 to the Dragon, through Eden: "Be kind to him, old lizard. He's kinder than both of us."
- 600 when he kneels to propose: "Finally. Took you long enough."
- 645 the last Keep, heard by Eden and Aldous at once through Aster and Vowsilver (see argentine.md).
