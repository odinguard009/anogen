# The Knight Orders of Verandel (reference)

Twelve public orders, each about 2,000-6,000 knights plus squires, each with a color worn as a cloak lining and sash. Masters sit on the Order Council under the king. Knights fight with weapons, spells, Tenets or any mix. The orders, the academy and the Southern Wall are why Verandel ranks second in the world.

1. **Argent Order** (silver). The Royal Knight Order: guards the crown, the capital and the Concord. Hereditary master: House Argentine (now Duke Aldous Argentine). Style: disciplined formations, ward-lances. Secret: its founding charter names the Argentine "wardens of the Gate"; nobody living understands the phrase except the king.
2. **Crimson Rampart** (red). Holds the Southern Wall on the Teeth. Master: Lord Marshal Gideon Valdis (Aurelian's father). Style: force walls, heavy infantry. Rivalry: despises the Golden Seal's "desk knights".
3. **Azure Tide** (blue). Navy and strait patrol, vein-keel warships. Master: Admiral Rosalind Mereaux. Style: water and wind spells.
4. **Verdant Thorn** (green). Rangers of forests and frontier marches; scouts the Pale's edge. Master: Warden Fennick Ashgrove. Style: traps, camouflage wards.
5. **Golden Seal** (gold). Internal law, investigation, treason trials. Master: Lady Justiciar Beatrix Hollen. Style: truth-wards, binding seals. Rivalry: obsessed with exposing the Black Order, which executes nobles without trial. Lysette is a cadet here.
6. **Violet Lantern** (violet). Mage corps; guards and studies the Crown Well under Solmarch. Master: Archmage Cyprian Vey. Style: high spellcraft, siege magic.
7. **Ivory Order** (ivory). Healers, battle medics and wardens of the sick. Master: Mother Superior Agnete Rosk. Liora Argentine served here as vice-master. Style: healing, sealing, purification.
8. **Grey Gale** (storm-grey). Cavalry and griffon riders; rapid response. Master: Captain-General Matthias Kord. Style: speed charges, wind-lances.
9. **Amber Hearth** (amber). Logistics, refugee camps, well engineering, roads. Master: Steward Helene Varga. Style: utility magic, fortification wards. Quietly the most important order.
10. **Bronze Bell** (bronze). Academy wardens and instructors; recruit training; guards Hallowcrest. Master: Headmistress Dame Hildegard Ostwald. Tesfane Okoru teaches under it.
11. **Indigo Veil** (indigo). Foreign intelligence and diplomacy; spies abroad. Master: Count Lucan Merrow. Rivalry: jealous of the Black Order's domestic reach; would love to absorb it.
12. **Umber Anvil** (umber brown). Siege engineers, sappers, mana-arms forges, tunnels under the Wall. Master: Forgemaster Brann Okafor-Dunst (half Mansakar). Style: earth spells, engines.

13. **Black Order** (black). Officially founded PR 137 by royal writ; members unknown; records sealed by the king. Duty: hunt Veils who wear human faces, by any means, including nobles and officers. Executions are announced as "treason". Public belief: 13 masked agents ("the Black Thirteen"), assassins above the law. Feared by almost every other order; hated by the Golden Seal.
- Reality: Eden alone, plus his Pillars and shadow-soldiers posing as masked agents (Gorrath does the voices, Vesk does disguises). Black mask, plain black coat, no insignia.
- Known only to: Eden, King Theodric, Ysolde Lisle (handler), Duke Aldous.
- Methods: Ysolde sends sealed tasks; Eden confirms a Veil (shadow tell, then Candor light) before killing. Rule he set himself: no kill without confirmation. Planned: he breaks it once at ch 260 under pressure; it haunts him.
- Count so far: 31 Veils killed in five years. One was Corentin Corvaille's Veil (PR 140).
- Paperwork: Ysolde requires Form 13-B for every death. Running joke.
