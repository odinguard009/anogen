# Politics and the antagonist ladder (reference)

## Human antagonists (beyond the Hush)
- **Ashen Crowns** (ashen-crowns.md): terror network of the eleven conquered kingdoms. Regent: Count Lucan Merrow. Sponsored secretly by the Tailor.
- **The Iron Faction**: court nobles nostalgic for the Iron King's conquest. Leader: Archduke Konrad Halberg, the king's cousin, charming, patriotic, ruthless. Want: a war king, the Void Host as a weapon, and Valerius replaced by a pliable heir. Ally of convenience with Merrow (coup, ch 190-199). Survives disgraced; returns in arc 7 as the tribunal's star witness.
- **The Lanternhouse Compact**: cartel of Verandel and Norhaven merchant houses that controls processed mana-stone (lanterns, wards, weapons). Front man: Ambrose Kell, smiling arms dealer who sells unstable mana stones to anyone. Secret financier: Volkhovar (reveal ch 98). Want: monopoly on the Crown Wells' output; war is profitable. Kell secretly hosts Kaurveth's will (reveal ch 560).
- **Volkhovar Empire**: Empress Dragomira (cold, brilliant, devout to "the Weaver of Fate"); spymaster Count Arkady Belozerov (Frost Hand). Want: Verandel weakened, the Void Host's secret, the relics, and a world ledger of souls. Dragomira hosts Vehra (reveal ch 530); dies refusing Vehra's last order (ch 540).
- **Aurelis See**: Luminarch Severus (vow name), sincere zealot; Inquisitor Sister Theodora, kinder and smarter. Want: relics returned to the faith; the "Void's Son" judged. Becomes Ashkar's crusade (arc 11), then an ally.
- **Norhaven Confederation**: Chancellor Grady Owen Marsh, populist, wants Concord leadership; the Isthmus Company (corporate faction) grabs liberated Crown Wells (arc 10).
- **Shahdom of Ezharan**: Shah Kaveh, proud and cornered; withholds Sandneck supply to bargain for well rights (arc 6), then keeps his word at great cost.
- **Qianlu Dynasty**: patient; Minister Shen Ruolan plays all sides; useful neutral.
- **Akizane, Jayavara, Mansakar, Tawanqar**: allies with prices (arc 6, 8, 10).

## Why nations won't unite (engine of every political arc)
Finite Crown Wells; northern profit from southern blood; fear of Verandel's Void Host; Volkhovar's ledger ambition; the Aurelis claim on relics; Lanternhouse profit from war; Veils and later Vehra sabotaging treaties.

## Enemy type per arc (each arc a different kind of enemy)
1. Terror cell + Veil (ch 1-50).
2. Economic + court: Lanternhouse Compact, Iron Faction (51-100).
3. Foreign political: Volkhovar and Aurelis at the Concord; Merrow (101-150).
4. Internal coup: Iron Faction + Ashen Crowns + the Tailor (151-200).
5. Military monster: Hushborn siege of the Teeth, Hierarch the Tidewright (201-250).
6. Monster campaign + ally politics: Okarune, Ezharan's bargain, Hierarch the Ashen Choir (251-300).
7. Legal, religious, economic: the Concord tribunal, Aurelis Inquisition, Volkhovar embargo, propaganda "the Void's Son" (301-350).
8. Naval monster war + Volkhovar sabotage + Nihl (351-400).
9. Divine: Heaven Gate's Valkyrie Marshal Aethra and the Dragon's court (401-450).
10. Monster campaign + corporate greed: Selvara, the Isthmus Company (451-500).
11. Divine + religious: Vehra's puppets, Ashkar's crusade, Dragomira (501-550).
12. Monster final campaign: Glaceren, Kaurveth's Hierarchs (551-600).
13. Divine finale: Vehra and Kaurveth (751-800).

## Victory rule (every arc)
Eden is overpowered; the reader should trust he will win. Tension comes from the path and the price: what he must reveal, whom he must shield, which rule he must keep, what it costs (power, memory, trust, standing, injury, bonds, principles). He prepares, plans and strains; no fake last-second rescues. Battle mix per arc: arcs-3.md.

## God-hosts in politics (gods-hosts.md)
- Known hosts are deterrents: Volkhovar's Fate-Empress (Vehra), Qianlu's Jade General (Kaelthos), Jayavara's Admiral Kirana (Thalos). Saravant hides Ishani (Niveth).
- Succession hunts: Volkhovar's Frost Hand tracks hosts and grooms successors, then stages deaths near them. Targets: Kirana (arc 8), Rowan (arc 11), any host found.
- Aurelis wants Ashkar "freed" from Rowan; the Isthmus Company tries to buy Kirana's contract; the Tailor stole Clara for Kaurveth; Kell, unknowing host of Want, fuels every greed scheme.
- Once Verandel is known to hold several hosts (arc 11), the balance of power breaks and the Ten Thrones arc begins.
