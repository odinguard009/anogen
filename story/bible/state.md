# State at the start of chapter 1 (autumn PR 142)

## Eden (narrator)
- 18. Public name Eden Hale, ward of House Hale (minor knight house of the northern march). True name Eden Aster Argentine; nobody at the academy knows. Grey eyes, black hair, average height, lean. Plain clothes, black gloves.
- Location: Solmarch, night before transfer into Hallowcrest Year III by royal writ.
- Mission (from Ysolde): find what took three students since spring (Pell Garrow, Anneliese Kroft, Dorran Yeats); suspect a Veil among students or staff. Cover: keep grades middling, stay unremarkable, never reveal the Black Order.
- Shows publicly: dark lance (defence only), claims a 3-Bell core and a "shadow-shaping" Tenet. Candor is used only as the Black Order, masked, or when he is serious.
- Real current power: Melsche Door with lance/shield, short summons, Pillars X Vesk, IX Gorrath, VIII Mirelle; First Legion (never woke more than 40). Sepiroth Gate: Candor plus 6 minor relics known by name. Has never lent a relic. True Bells unknown (he has never pushed the Bell fully).
- Knows: he is the Black Order; Veil tells; the Dragon and the Tutor exist in his dreams; his mother died the night of her birthday when he was 10 and he believes he froze.
- Does not know: why he has two Tenets; the Tutor's identity; that he opened the Gate that night; the Tailor; his mother's seal; Ysolde is his aunt; the letter; why the front stalls.
- Emotional state: flat, efficient, avoids attachment. Dry humor survives. Deep habit: counts exits, reads shadows of everyone he meets.
- Black Order tally: 31 Veils in five years, including Corentin Corvaille's Veil (he remembers the face, not the name yet).

## Relationships
- Ysolde Lisle: handler; dry mutual respect; she nags about Form 13-B.
- King Theodric: employer, distant warmth, met monthly in the library annex.
- Duke Aldous: father; they have not spoken except formally for two years. Eden believes his father blames him.
- Dragon ("Landlord"): friendly, bickering. Tutor: wary respect; trains him most nights.
- Pillars: Vesk (banter), Gorrath (protective, comic), Mirelle (silent loyalty).
- Not yet met: Lysette, Tobias, Niamh, Aurelian, Okoru, Strent.

## World now
- The front holds at the Teeth. Grey dust on southern winds this autumn is heavier than usual.
- Concord summit planned for next year at Solmarch.
- Golden Seal is pushing the Order Council to unmask the Black Thirteen after a duke's nephew was executed last month (a Veil).
