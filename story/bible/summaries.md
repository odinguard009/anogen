# Chapter summaries

## Ch 1 - Forty Minutes of Grass
Eden skips Spell Grammar to sleep on the Old Observatory roof garden, where the grass and apple tree stay green out of season. The endless-war dream (ash, banners, no air) chokes him until a girl's crying cuts it off; he wakes without the usual ash taste. Lysette Hollen sits a meter away, unaware of him, and when she wipes her nose on her Committee armband he offers his mother's feather handkerchief. Mortified and sharp, she bargains for silence (he hides his Ostwald permit), admits Rowan invited Clara Belmont to the Lantern Ball with a lopsided paper lantern that caught fire, laughs once, and keeps the handkerchief "to wash it." Minutes later she returns with the Committee book: "Eden Wren. Sixty-one absences. Tomorrow you attend class. I will see to it personally."

## Ch 2 - The Timetable
Lysette slips a corrected timetable under Eden's door. In the dream the Tutor (face turned away) beats him at a siege game on the white board, the Dragon ("Landlord") complains about the given-away linen, and Eden notices a locked door with a silver hairpin in its keyhole; the board's edge nearly erases him until the Tutor says "Not that one. Not yet." Lysette escorts him to breakfast, where Felix asks "Eaten today?" ("Technically."); in Spell Grammar Brandmeier lets a flawed live siege ward smoke and calls on him, and his six words ("The ward has no subject, Professor") make the room hate him. At the stairs Valerius asks "So you've finally stopped hiding?"; Eden answers "I'm on a timetable," Valerius says "Pity," and Lysette writes: "Why does the Prince know him?"

## Ch 3 - Short Shadows
In a Solmarch bookbinder's back room Ysolde gives Eden the file on Emeric Fauconier, a revenue clerk replaced by a Veil (the pear-seller's cat avoids him), warns of Golden Seal investigators, and reminds him to ring only three tones at the Bell Assessment; Eden recalls a faint fourth tone last year. At the lantern market, where soft overlapping light leaves few edges, Eden in the Faceless Mantle (with Vesk gossiping from the shadows) spots the clerk's unbreathing shadow; the Veil sees him and runs, and Eden drops it through a door in the dye-seller's awning shade into the cellar. In total darkness he can open no doors; Candor hums one note on its own, he strikes a match for an edge, confirms the Veil cannot say its victim's middle name, and kills it; dying, it says he smells "like the bottom of the world." Ysolde wants Form 13-B; the clerk's ledger holds a list of Year 5 names in another hand, with Rowan circled twice: "Lantern Ball. Before the last dance."

## Ch 4 - Eleven Blocks
Eden finds that the ledger's Year 5 list matches the Bell Assessment order pinned on the public atrium board, so his theory of an inside leak collapses: anyone could have copied it. At lunch Lysette sits with him "as Committee", keeps touching a chain at her collar whenever she looks at Rowan, and Seraphine teases ("my saviour slept through breakfast again") while Eden carries Rowan's circled name alone. In Okoru's arms class Eden only blocks Bastien Ardenne eleven times, then takes a real hit to the ribs and steps out to lose; Okoru tells him he "watched his left shoulder eleven times." When Lysette's chain snaps in her own bout, the locket holds her brother Corentin, executed for treason by the Black Order; Eden's stomach turns for no reason he can name and he looks away first, as she vows to the yard: "I will prove that they are monsters."
