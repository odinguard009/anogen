# Chapter summaries

## Ch 1 - Forty Minutes of Grass
Eden skips Spell Grammar to sleep on the Old Observatory roof garden, where the grass and apple tree stay green out of season. The endless-war dream (ash, banners, no air) chokes him until a girl's crying cuts it off; he wakes without the usual ash taste. Lysette Hollen sits a meter away, unaware of him, and when she wipes her nose on her Committee armband he offers his mother's feather handkerchief. Mortified and sharp, she bargains for silence (he hides his Ostwald permit), admits Rowan invited Clara Belmont to the Lantern Ball with a lopsided paper lantern that caught fire, laughs once, and keeps the handkerchief "to wash it." Minutes later she returns with the Committee book: "Eden Wren. Sixty-one absences. Tomorrow you attend class. I will see to it personally."

## Ch 2 - The Timetable
Lysette slips a corrected timetable under Eden's door. In the dream the Tutor (face turned away) beats him at a siege game on the white board, the Dragon ("Landlord") complains about the given-away linen, and Eden notices a locked door with a silver hairpin in its keyhole; the board's edge nearly erases him until the Tutor says "Not that one. Not yet." Lysette escorts him to breakfast, where Felix asks "Eaten today?" ("Technically."); in Spell Grammar Brandmeier lets a flawed live siege ward smoke and calls on him, and his six words ("The ward has no subject, Professor") make the room hate him. At the stairs Valerius asks "So you've finally stopped hiding?"; Eden answers "I'm on a timetable," Valerius says "Pity," and Lysette writes: "Why does the Prince know him?"

## Ch 3 - Short Shadows
In a Solmarch bookbinder's back room Ysolde gives Eden the file on Emeric Fauconier, a revenue clerk replaced by a Veil (the pear-seller's cat avoids him), warns of Golden Seal investigators, and reminds him to ring only three tones at the Bell Assessment; Eden recalls a faint fourth tone last year. At the lantern market, where soft overlapping light leaves few edges, Eden in the Faceless Mantle (with Vesk gossiping from the shadows) spots the clerk's unbreathing shadow; the Veil sees him and runs, and Eden drops it through a door in the dye-seller's awning shade into the cellar. In total darkness he can open no doors; Candor hums one note on its own, he strikes a match for an edge, confirms the Veil cannot say its victim's middle name, and kills it; dying, it says he smells "like the bottom of the world." Ysolde wants Form 13-B; the clerk's ledger holds a list of Year 5 names in another hand, with Rowan circled twice: "Lantern Ball. Before the last dance."

## Ch 4 - Eleven Blocks
Eden finds that the ledger's Year 5 list matches the Bell Assessment order pinned on the public atrium board, so his theory of an inside leak collapses: anyone could have copied it. At lunch Lysette sits with him "as Committee", keeps touching a chain at her collar whenever she looks at Rowan, and Seraphine teases ("my saviour slept through breakfast again") while Eden carries Rowan's circled name alone. In Okoru's arms class Eden only blocks Bastien Ardenne eleven times, then takes a real hit to the ribs and steps out to lose; Okoru tells him he "watched his left shoulder eleven times." When Lysette's chain snaps in her own bout, the locket holds her brother Corentin, executed for treason by the Black Order; Eden's stomach turns for no reason he can name and he looks away first, as she vows to the yard: "I will prove that they are monsters."

## Ch 5 - Latch
In the Undercroft drill Eden runs a Lance of three with Felix ("Eaten today?" "Half a roll.") and Lysette, who assigned herself to them. Through an iron grille Lysette pauses to watch Rowan's team, and a drill-warden that should not be able to run comes out of a maintenance door with a training blade and charges her; her baton snaps on its first stroke. Limited to his public blocking lance, Eden knocks it into the door-shield mid-thrust and wedges the blade against the knocker ring (Latch), and Lysette drives her last baton into its chest crystal. Afterward her hands shake: "I didn't need that." "I know." She does not use Witness, and answers Rowan coolly. Okoru pries out the core: the hollow is packed with gray dust, and he asks who had the keys to the warden store.

## Ch 6 - Lance Nine
In the lowchapel, among pews carved with the names of Year Sixes lost on the Crossing, Okoru forms the Year 5 Lances: Eden had hoped for quiet Lance Fourteen and gets Lance Nine with Felix, Rowan, Clara, and Lysette, officially assigned to supervise his 61 absences. Rowan is loud, warm and easy to like (half a honey-cake pressed into Eden's glove); dead remembrance lilies stand up where Clara sat; in the half-scraped fresco corridor Eden alone sees the Lirenne fresco's gold leaf take the exact shade of Rowan's Spark. In the vestry Eden asks for a transfer; Okoru, with the door left open to Lysette's hearing, says "Your lance closed... they don't slam," then chooses not to push. The warden store ledger shows the dust-filled warden's core was signed for by Clement Daubert on the 11th of the sixth month; Okoru, folding the dust cloth, says Daubert died in the fourth month and he carried the coffin.

## Ch 7 - Medical Exemption
Eden wakes on the roof with Lysette's first attendance warrant pinned to his coat (the seal pale pink: her ink pad is dry), after a morning of Rowan hunting him loudly across the academy to be friends. Lysette climbs up to collect him; he shows Ostwald's permit excusing him while resting "on the roofs", she finds no handbook rule to beat it, and Eden, noticing her trowel left in the sage after her dawn weeding, says nothing about it. She outflanks him with lunch: Lance Nine now eats together by order of Okoru, and he agrees because he's hungry. At the table Seraphine announces she'll make Felix laugh before the Ball; in the bread crush a kitchen girl in south-hills green slips Eden a black card with a pressed ash-rose, "Remember Thornmere", and he uses it as a bookmark. A new staff man in a gray coat, thumbnail black with ash, watches him pocket the book and writes a line in a notebook; Eden takes him for a Golden Seal investigator.

## Ch 8 - What the Moon Allows
With thirty-four minutes before the moon leaves the air-shaft grate, Eden searches the Undercroft west workshops, where the late Clement Daubert gave each of his twenty-two wardens a name (the rogue one was Tortoise). Two more dust-packed wardens, Margery and Uncle, wake and stand in the moonlight to steal his edges; Gorrath holds one on his shield, Vesk lures the other with a knife-hilt rap, and Eden opens book-sized doors in the grate's lattice shadow so Mirelle can cut each crystal from below. The store daybook's sixth-month "C. Daubert" is too steady, and the blotter holds forty mirror-written practice signatures. In Daubert's moonlit drawing room Eden finds Vice-headmaster Strent grading without a lamp ("lamps give me terrible headaches") and humming the wordless lullaby Eden's mother made up; Eden freezes for the first time since he was ten. Strent: "Wren, isn't it? Thornmere name. Your mother must have sung that to you too." Eden never told anyone she sang.

## Ch 9 - Not Technically a Veil
On his way to the monthly palace debrief Eden finds the pear woman's stall sealed by the Golden Seal, and on Wick Street a Lantern Ward crowd with oil-filled lanterns besieging the Seal post where she is held; hooded, he stands at the chandlery door, takes a flung burning lantern on his door-shield and a cobblestone above the left eye rather than step aside, and slips away when Seal whistles come. In Ysolde's office she reports the woman released and the Seal's description of a hooded youth with a door-shield, then makes him argue Form 13-B for a construct that isn't a Veil. The King looks long at his cut and asks if he's eating ("a boy in my Lance keeps giving me his eggs"). Strent filed a lenient note on Eden's night in the workshops; Eden says there is nothing to report, and Ysolde, knowing he lied, puts back an old gray-sealed envelope: "Orders you haven't earned." She gives him an Ember cell in the tannery streets and: "Watch the Tallis boy."

## Ch 10 - Clean Paper
Third person, Lysette: the Committee is down to one officer, a dry ink pad and deferred requisitions; she pulls Eden's file and finds it suspiciously clean (no parents, no letters ever, "medical: see headmistress"). At lunch Rowan and Clara laugh at the next table; Eden says his cut is from "a door"; Seraphine asks him, "You'd save me again, right?" and gets "Probably.", then fails joke attempt one on Felix and writes a 1 on her hand; Lysette notices she hasn't turned toward Rowan for over an hour. Judging a duel alone, she uses Witness for the first time since the drill and sees Florian Eschenbach's Tenet drift the chalk line under Gisela Morvaine's heel. Her mother's letter says the Seal will drag the Black Order into the light, that she means to find and thank the hooded youth who saved their Wick Street post, and that Corentin would have been twenty; Lysette folds Eden's handkerchief, unwashed, back into her pocket.

## Ch 11 - Nine Minutes of Somebody Else's Maze
Okoru sends Lance Nine into a graded Undercroft maze (nine minutes, false walls, the bursar's cheap Kennel of sand-and-cane dust-hounds loosed at minute four). Eden sees the lamps' faint second shadows all leaning toward a bright exit lamp, but must pass it off as a guess; Lysette's Witness shows a canvas wall dropped that morning, Felix's Trail points to a worn decoy path, Rowan's Spark light stalls the hounds and Clara closes a cut on Felix's arm. Behind a portcullis Lysette notices Eden's nine-day-old cut and awkwardly hands him a Committee lint pad. They finish third; Okoru says nobody picks the left branch by feel. In the finish gallery the gray-coat man from ch 7 watches Rowan; his black thumbnail is oak-bark tannin, not coal, and he is gone when Eden turns.
