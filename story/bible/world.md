# World: Elysmar (compact rules; details in continents, nations, mana, invaders, ashen-crowns, orders, powers, academy, argentine, timeline, politics, gods, gods-hosts .md)

**Planet.** Three times Earth's surface; seven continents in Earth's layout mirrored east-west. Halveren (Europe analog) north-centre; Kesharra (Asia analog) to its WEST; Vastlund (North America analog) EAST across the Grey Gulf; Okarune (Africa analog) south past the Middle Sea; Selvara (S. America analog) south-east; Ruhnmara (Australia analog) south-west; Glaceren at the south pole. Year: PR (Pale Reckoning) 112, autumn.

**Mana.** The planet's life-current in rock veins and wells. People hold it in a core; capacity in Bells (1-7). Each person has one Tenet (ability): a theme from their deepest conviction, deepening in Tiers (Spark, Shape, Edge, Crown, Sovereign) only when that conviction is tested. Overdraw: mana-wither. The Refusal: Elysmar's mana slowly unmakes beings not born of it.

**Names.** Verandel: given, hidden middle (soul-bound, used in oaths and relic loans), house. Public form: given + house. Telling your middle name is deep trust. A Veil cannot speak its victim's middle name.

**Other realms.** The Void (nothing between worlds) was ruled by the Emperor of Void. Beneath it the Hush: starving sediment of unmade worlds. Above, Heaven Gate: realm of the Sepiroth Dragon and its Valkyries, keepers of the Sepiroth Vault.

**Invasion.** PR 0: the Emperor's weariness thinned the Void's floor; the Hush broke through at the pole (the Wound). That night the Dragon pulled every holy weapon on Elysmar into the Vault (the Night the Relics Vanished). Hushborn spread through mana veins, turning land grey and silent (the Pale) to survive the Refusal. Slow for 93 years; since the Iron King's summoning (PR 93) the Wound widened and the south fell fast. Now 30% Pale. Deep sea slows them. The front stalls at Verandel's coast; the public credits the Southern Wall.

**Veils.** Infiltrator Hushborn that drink a person's shadow overnight and wear their face. Tells: a shadow that does not breathe, true grey form in honest light, no victim's middle name.

**Verandel.** Once one realm among many on Halveren. King Gerulf Aurenmark, the Iron King, conquered them all (PR 53-68): eleven kingdoms, now duchies, plus Skarnheim and the Aurelis See, which Theodric freed in PR 94. At 60 he summoned the Emperor of Void as a weapon; the Emperor's Void Host spread terror but killed no one. His son Theodric, Duke Aldous Argentine and Liora (the Valkyrie of Heaven Gate) turned on him; the Emperor killed the Iron King and vanished. Theodric IV now rules (PR 93-). Other nations believe Verandel still hides the Void Host as a secret order; this is why it ranks second. Capital Solmarch; 12 knight orders, a secret 13th (Black Order), the Royal Academy of Hallowcrest, the Southern Wall on the Teeth.

**Politics.** Thirteen sovereign nations, each holding one of the thirteen surviving Crown Wells, plus Tawanqar (remnant observer). They will not unite: finite wells, northern profit from southern blood, old grudges, fear of Verandel's Void Host, and Veils sabotaging treaties. Ranking: 1 Volkhovar, 2 Verandel, 3 Qianlu, 4 Norhaven.

**Inside threat.** The Ashen Crowns: terror network of the eleven conquered kingdoms' heirs, seeking revenge on House Aurenmark (ashen-crowns.md).
