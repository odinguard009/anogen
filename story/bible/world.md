# World: Elysmar (compact rules; details in continents.md, nations.md, mana.md, invaders.md, orders.md, powers.md, academy.md, timeline.md)

**Planet.** Elysmar: three times Earth's surface, seven continents laid out like Earth's but mirrored east-west. Halveren (Europe analog) sits in the north-centre. Kesharra (Asia analog) lies WEST of it; Vastlund (North America analog) lies EAST across the Grey Gulf ocean. South of Halveren, past the Middle Sea, lies Okarune (Africa analog). Selvara (South America analog) in the south-east, Ruhnmara (Australia analog) in the south-west, Glaceren at the south pole. Calendar: PR (Pale Reckoning) years since the First Breach. Story opens PR 142.

**Mana.** Life-current of the planet. It runs through rock in veins and pools in wells. People hold it in a core behind the sternum. Capacity is measured in Bells (1-7) by the academy's Resonance Bell. Every person develops one Tenet (ability) at 12-15: a theme born from their deepest conviction, which deepens in Tiers (Spark, Shape, Edge, Crown, Sovereign) when that conviction is tested, not by grinding. One soul, one Tenet. Overdraw causes mana-wither: grey hair, cold hands, death.

**Names.** In Verandel everyone has three names: given, hidden middle (the keeping name, soul-bound, used in oaths and binding), house. Public form is given + house. Telling someone your middle name is an act of deep trust. A Veil can never speak its victim's middle name.

**The invasion.** PR 0: the Night the Relics Vanished (every holy weapon in the world disappeared) and the First Breach at the south pole. The Hushborn rise from the Hush, a dimension of hungry remnants beneath the Void. They spread by drinking mana veins, leaving grey dead land and silence (the Pale). Deep ocean breaks the veins and slows them. Now 30% of the planet's surface is Pale: Glaceren, Ruhnmara, nearly all of Okarune and Selvara, and the southern seas. Hushborn eat sound: battle near them is eerily quiet.

**Veils.** Infiltrator Hushborn. A Veil drinks a person's shadow over one night and takes their face, facts and voice. The victim falls into the Hush. Tells: weak old feelings, a shadow that does not breathe, false form under honest light, cannot speak the victim's middle name.

**Politics.** Thirteen sovereign nations survive, each holding one of the thirteen remaining Crown Wells (21 before the invasion), plus Tawanqar, a well-less remnant with an observer seat. The Concord table at Solmarch has 20 seats; seven are draped in grey for fallen nations. Nations will not unite: wells are finite and the alliance leader would control them; northern powers profit while southern ones bleed; the old Well Wars left grudges; everyone fears Verandel's academy; and Veils inside courts quietly sabotage every treaty. Strength ranking: 1 Volkhovar Empire, 2 Kingdom of Verandel, 3 Qianlu Dynasty, 4 Norhaven Confederation.

**Verandel.** Kingdom covering most of Halveren (about 3x Earth's Europe). Capital Solmarch. King Theodric IV. Feudal monarchy of duchies, Franco-Germanic-Latin culture. Strong because of its 12 knight orders, the Royal Academy of Hallowcrest, and the Southern Wall fortresses on the Teeth (the narrow strait to Okarune). A secret 13th, the Black Order, hunts Veils. The front stalls at Verandel's shore. Publicly that is credited to the Wall. The real reason is a secret (secrets.md).

**Look and feel.** Lamp-lit stone cities, mana lanterns, steam-free low tech, cavalry, griffons, ships with vein-keels. Grey dust on the southern wind. Refugees from Okarune everywhere.
