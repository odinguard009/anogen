# Arc 1 concept: "The Transfer" (ch 1-50, Year III autumn to late winter, PR 142)

Central question: what is taking Hallowcrest students, and can Eden find it without being found?
Double life: days as the middling transfer Eden Hale in Lance Seven; nights as the Black Order, masked, with Vesk, Gorrath and Mirelle. Lysette investigates the Black Order from the other side, so every gain for Eden is a risk.
Escalation: one vanished student becomes a pattern, then a nest, then proof the enemy knows who Eden is.
Power rule: dark lance only as a shield at the academy; Candor only masked or when someone will die otherwise.

## First 5 chapters (hooks)
1. Night in Solmarch. Eden, masked, hunts a Veil wearing a tax clerk through a lantern market; reads shadows, opens a door in an awning's shade, Candor's light shows the grey form. Dying Veil: "You smell like the bottom of the world." Ysolde hands him the writ: transfer tomorrow. Dream: a locked door with a silver hairpin in the keyhole.
2. Dream of the Tutor's board game and the Dragon ("Landlord") arguing about breakfast. Arrival on the Long Stair; Tobias attaches himself. Hook: the Bell test is tomorrow and Eden must fail it on purpose.
3. Bell Assessment. Eden holds back to 3 tones; a faint fourth only Okoru hears. Gossip about the frail Argentine heir; Eden corrects a detail by reflex. Lysette accuses him of rigging the Bell, then announces in class she will unmask the Black Thirteen.
4. Lance Seven formed: Eden, Lysette, Tobias, Niamh, and the empty slot of vanished Pell Garrow. Lysette's locket (Corentin). Hook: Pell's name on the roster has been rewritten overnight in fresh ink.
5. First Undercroft drill. Aurelian's Lance ambushes them; Eden only blocks (lance to door-shield) and still wins the exchange on tactics. Hook: they find Pell's bronze badge, still warm, in a corridor where their footsteps make no sound.

## Beat list
- 6-10: Lance training and comedy (Tobias counting, Niamh singing badly, Brandmeier). Okoru notices Eden's shield footwork is a veteran's. First night op inside the academy via Vesk. Strent introduced: kind, avoids lamps, hums (ch 8). Ysolde's sealed envelope (ch 9). Ch 10 interlude: Lysette's POV.
- 11-15: Second vanishing (Anneliese's roommate). Eden and Lysette paired as investigators. Tobias blurts his middle name (12). Strent's lullaby is the one Eden's mother sang (12, Eden says nothing). Lysette's Witness shows a door-shaped shadow (13). Dragon's "my prison" slip (15).
- 16-20: Duel with Aurelian on the Ladder: Eden fights only defensive, wins by turning Aurelian's Rampart walls into cover. Grudging respect. Concord war map in Strent's class: the front bends around Verandel (20). Ch 20 interlude: Aurelian.
- 21-25: Black Order mission in a Golden Seal noble house; Lysette is on the raid. Masked Eden and Lysette face each other: she nearly catches him; he saves her from a Stalker and vanishes. She keeps a scrap of his black coat.
- 26-30: Anniversary of Liora's death. Eden at the grave; Lysette finds him; he says it is a stranger's grave. First real quiet moment. Father seen from afar at the cemetery gate, leaves without speaking. Tutor flinches at "regret is useless" (30). Ch 30 interlude: Duke Aldous.
- 31-35: Winter Practicum on the Southern Wall. First live Hushborn: a Choir silences the battlement; Lance Seven fights with hand signals. Eden uses Gorrath's shadow as cover without showing himself. King asks for "the second envelope" (33).
- 36-40: Back at Hallowcrest. Lysette tells the Lance about Corentin. Eden realizes he killed his Veil (38). He stays her partner and says nothing. Ch 40 interlude: Ysolde.
- 41-45: Third trail leads under the Undercroft: an old sealed level, a Veil nest feeding on students' shadows through their sleep. The Veil among the staff is a groundskeeper Hierarch, not Strent (misdirection). Eden must choose: expose powers or lose the students.
- 46-50: Climax. The nest battle in near darkness where doors are hard to open; Eden lights torches to make edges. Lends the relic shield Wardenshell to Tobias (47), first loan. Pell and Anneliese pulled out alive but hollow-eyed; Dorran is already dust (cost). Eden attacks with Candor; the Lance sees only white light. Lysette replays the scene with Witness and stares at Eden. The Hierarch dies saying: "The Tailor sends regards to Liora's boy." (50)

## Set pieces
- Battle: lantern-market chase (1); Undercroft ambush (5); Ladder duel vs Rampart (16-18); masked raid and Stalker (22-24); silent battlement (31-34); the nest (46-50).
- Emotional: the grave (27); Corentin's story and Eden's silence (36-38); Tobias holding a relic he does not understand, and Eden trusting him (47); the hollow-eyed rescued students (49).
- Comedy: Gorrath's thirteen voices; Tobias's odds of survival; Dragon vs Tutor; Form 13-B for a Husk.

## Unlock in arc 1
No new Pillar. First loan (47). Doorstep not yet. Eden learns Wardenshell's name in the dream Vault at ch 44.
