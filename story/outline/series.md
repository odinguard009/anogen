# Series roadmap (13 arcs, ~650 ch). Detail: bible/arcs-1/2/3.md, twists-1/2.md, politics.md, gods.md, gods-hosts.md, deaths.md

1. 1-50 The Rooftop (school, terror): can Eden stay unseen once someone insists on seeing him?
2. 51-100 Lanterns and Ledgers (economic, court): who profits from the fear?
3. 101-150 The Concord (foreign politics): can thirteen nations share one table? Lysette learns he is the Black Order (140).
4. 151-200 Argentine (coup): who is Eden when he stops hiding? Declares himself (160); mother's truth (175); Tailor ended (200); graduation.
5. 201-250 The Teeth (siege war): can they hold the narrowest place on Elysmar?
6. 251-300 Across the Middle Sea (campaign): can the south be taken back? The Void Host is Eden (290).
7. 301-350 The Void's Son (tribunal, faith, embargo): saviour or cause? He is not the Emperor (340).
8. 351-400 The Grey Sea (naval war): can the sea hold when the land cannot?
9. 401-450 Heaven Gate (divine): what does Eden owe the dead who made him? Gods in hosts revealed (430). He forgets Lysette; she brings him back.
10. 451-500 The Skyspine (campaign, greed): can a dead continent be given back?
11. 501-550 The Ten Thrones (gods, hosts, crusade): what if gods want the world unmade? Vehra jumps into Valerius (545).
12. 551-600 The Pale (final campaign): can every nation march together once? Lysette hosts the Gardener (580).
13. 601-650 Eden (finale): what should end, be kept, and begin?

## Rules
- Enemy type changes every arc. War arcs alternate with political, school, spy and divine arcs.
- Eden is overpowered and wins; tension is the path and the price (power, memory, trust, standing, injury, bonds, principles), not fake odds. Battle mix ~30% flex, 35% puzzle, 25% strained, 10% costly; 2-4 earned aura moments per arc (arcs-3.md). Main circle lives (deaths.md).
- Ten creator gods live sealed in mortal hosts (Thrones): Eden carries two; others sit in other nations as deterrents or in secret. Their conflict drives arcs 9, 11, 13.
- 36 major twists, each planted 10+ chapters ahead. Debts file holds only the current window.

## Romance (Eden and Lysette only)
Handkerchief (1), roof dance (26), she lets Rowan go (90), rupture (140), middle names (198), the feather (205), forgetting and return (420-445), her confession (450), his confession and first kiss (500), proposal (600), vow (648). Seraphine: refused kindly (145), becomes Concord Speaker.

## Comedy
Eden's deadpan vs Lysette's rules; the Argentine household; the Duke's disguises and ballads; Dragon vs Tutor; Gorrath's voices; Form 13-B.

## Ending
Ending, Keeping and Beginning (Eden and Lysette) close the Wound; Kaurveth gets an ending through Kell; Valerius binds Vehra; the Emperor finishes; the Wellmother accepts Eden. He gives up the Door, keeps the Gate: a living man who is seen, the Pale greening, duchies given a voice, names spoken openly.
