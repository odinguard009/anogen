# Research notes, arc 1 (technique only; nothing copied)

Searched once: web-serial pacing, chapter-ending hooks, slow-burn pacing.
- Vary the hook by kind, not just intensity: question, threat, arrival, discovery, line of dialogue, choice, emotional sting, object. Two "danger" hooks in a row feel like one.
- Breathers after peaks: a lighter or quieter chapter right after a set piece lets the reader digest and makes the next spike land.
- Slow burn runs on small, repeated, escalating gestures (an object exchanged, a habit noticed) rather than declarations; each beat should change the distance between the two by a step.
- Multi-chapter set pieces: each chapter of the set piece needs its own mini-goal and turn, so the middle chapter does not sag.
- School + secret-identity serials: the cover itself is the constraint that makes easy wins tense (who sees, what it costs to be seen).
Applied in outline/arc-1.md: hook kinds rotate; comic chapters follow heavy ones; the Ball and Mawgate set pieces give each chapter its own turn.
