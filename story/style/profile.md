# Style profile (swappable; engine reads only this file for style)

## Voice
- First person, past tense. Dry, observant, understated humor; feelings shown through small actions rather than stated.
- Tactical narration in fights: read the terrain, name the options, pay the cost.

## Prose
- Concrete nouns and strong verbs; avoid stacked adjectives and filler adverbs.
- Vary sentence length. Short paragraphs in action; longer in quiet beats.
- Dialogue carries subtext; people rarely say exactly what they mean.

## Chapter shape
- Open in motion or with a concrete image, never a weather or waking-up cliche.
- End on a hook that is different in kind from the previous chapters' hooks.

## Freshness
- Do not reuse a simile, a gesture (sighing, clenching fists) or a sentence pattern more than once per arc.
- Each chapter: at least one surprising specific detail that belongs only to this world.
